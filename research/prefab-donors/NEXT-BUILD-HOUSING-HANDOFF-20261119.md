## Locked Generic implementation policy (approved 2026-11-19)
1. Preserve stock Prefab Starter House and its selectable styles, bed and glass colors as **one** of 19 one-time personal graduation options, not fifteen different entitlements. Audit individual native styles for resource, terrain, dimension and hidden loot; especially Subaquatic, Tower, Tree and Mushroom before permitting them in free grant. Keep ordinary separately paid/crafted Prefab houses subject to recipe/EMC/economy tests.
2. Prevent first-join automatic house: `ModConfiguration.newPlayersGetStartingItem=false` and `startingItem=Nothing`. Only **personally earned graduation** grants one free house, never to teammates and never once per style.
3. Preserve bed, crafting table, standard lighting and empty chest. Disable free stock chest content `starterHouseOptions.addChestContents=false`; disable shaft `starterHouseOptions.addMineshaft=false` and `includeMineshaftChest=false`. In the inspected source, chest defaults yield 5 tools, leather armor, bread, dirt/cobble, seedlings and torches; mineshafts excavate and can yield resources. Keep chests themselves enabled: `starterHouseOptions.addChests=true`.
4. Critical exception: `StructureHouse.AfterBuilding` calls `BuildingMethods.FillFurnaces` unconditionally and grants 20 coal per spawned furnace independent of chest settings. Prevent this for free Generic by `starterHouseOptions.addFurnace=false` (player crafts a furnace normally), or implement a server-authoritative free-grant-only sanitizer if separate nonfree Prefab houses must retain normal furnaces. Check collateral effect on paid construction before rollout.
5. UI source initializes the mineshaft and chest-content checkboxes from crossed configuration fields. The two features must be off **server-side**, not just hidden by GUI; exact release 1.0.9 may differ from public 1.21.1 branch, so validate packet handler and placement rechecks. No blanket disable of unrelated Prefab functions, no automatic strict-mode toggle, no unapproved dependency.
6. One free personal selection across all houses. Failed placement, blocked claim, invalid location, relog, death and inventory full must not consume the entitlement or duplicate items. Verify multiple players on same FTB team, server/client config authority, early construction, repeated blueprint attempts, normal separately purchased/crafted placement, structural rotation and material parity.
