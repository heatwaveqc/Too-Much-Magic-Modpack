## Bedrock shaft — mandatory integration blocker
Fixed local hatch (24,3,32), ladder descends to local Y0, support to north. Runtime shaft must extend below template and choose safe natural bedrock landing for Bore (3×3 machine, bedrock contact beneath its blades). Stock Prefab 1.21.1 HouseConfiguration addMineShaft and StructureHouse trapdoor marker invoke BuildingMethods.PlaceMineShaft, which stops at minBuildHeight+21 and may place a chest of collected drops. **This does not reach bedrock and is not automatically available to standalone custom NBT.** Do not use stock shaft to fulfill this requirement. Separate shaft-placement-policy.json records owner requirement and runtime acceptance criteria; not an executable hook. No fixed bedrock blocks or fictitious depth baked in. Disable stock shaft on all starter choices; custom true bedrock shaft only Dwarven Foundry. Natural bedrock, actual Ember field and blade contacts must be checked on selected site. No free ores or loot. Exact hook, protection, rollback, terrain fit and shaft/chamber generation must be implemented and tested before deployment.
