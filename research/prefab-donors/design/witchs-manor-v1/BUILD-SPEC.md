# Witch's Manor — Gothic Tudor coven estate (proposed V1 design)
2026-11-19. Owner asked for design phase, not construction yet. Primary JAWR; includes JAWR: Forbidden Magic, Hexerei and Roots Classic. Original architecture; no donor, no species selection or transformed-player requirement. One of the 17 mutually exclusive Newbie graduation reward choices. No runtime grant, pin or template built by this design.

## Architecture and placement
Provisional 65×29×55, surface local Y6 (offset -6). North approach; stepped two-story central hall with two asymmetrical wings, tall layered gables and chimneys, timber frame, pale infill, stone plinth, leaded glass and an enclosed kitchen garden. A dark, furnished wine-cellar-style basement beneath the central residence is accessible via interior switchback stairs and a second exterior stair from the rear; roofed and windowless with a dogleg entry, not a guarantee of native vampire sunlight immunity. Use native decorative timber families where ordinary acquisition/registry allow, e.g. Hexerei willow/mahogany; dark oak/spruce/stone and pale terracotta fallback. No unapproved gate skips: no Rowan or other exclusive Mutandis-derived logs/saplings, rare magic materials, active ritual markings, free transformed forms, entities, spawners or inventory payloads. Do not pre-create a vampire feeding farm. Material counts/EMC review in construction phase.
