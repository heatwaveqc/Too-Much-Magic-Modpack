## V1 design (implementation choices, not additional owner rulings)

Ground at local y=16, upper floor y=27, roof spring y=37. Basement chamber below grade, 16 blocks below floor; layered basalt/calcite/amethyst geode, provisional 12 budding amethyst, no ore/advanced contents, separate Hex lab. Explicitly clear rooms/basement, no exterior air overwrite. Preserve courtyard with separate 13×7×13 Fusion Shrine and 8×7×9 Cinderhearth pad, flower garden and entrance paths. Pedestal 17×9×17 on ground, enchanter 15×7×15 and Spirit Instiller 12×7×13 upstairs; all independently clear, future multiblocks assembled by player. Room space includes walking border, enchanter and cinderhearth may replace floor and excavate; reserve subfloor depth as needed. Normal world-height, terrain/anchor (ground at y16), material parity, advancement, importer, collision and lighting test required.
