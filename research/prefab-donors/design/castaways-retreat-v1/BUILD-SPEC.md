# Castaway's Retreat — fishing bungalow and tiki bar (first-pass design)
Owner authorized housing expansion/design 2026-11-19. Original architecture; no donor required. Part of 19 mutually exclusive graduation housing choices. This is a build proposal, not yet a generated NBT or deployed grant.

## Theme
A compact tropical fishing bungalow raised on piles, with pale native palm timber, thatch gables, shutters, turquoise fabric/accents, warm lanterns, small open-air cocktail bar, shaded seafood kitchen, sun deck and a short fishing jetty. The goal is a complete holiday fishing home, not a commercial brewery (Adventurer's Inn) or a large-scale farm (Farmer's Homestead).
 
## Coordinate plan (provisional, inclusive, x/z)
Envelope 35 x 16 x 33; local surface Y3 on bank, elevated deck/floor Y4, walkable Y5; north road/land at z0, shore near z23, water toward positive z. Site placement on suitable existing shoreline is mandatory for proper fishing functionality; not biome-locked at design stage. Avoid terrain replacement and artificial pond/sand island.
- x3..16 z4..15: 14x12 enclosed bungalow, one bedroom with two ordinary beds or bed + guest bed, living room, empty storage, crafting bench. Broad covered porch.
- x19..31 z4..15: 13x12 open-sided thatched tiki bar and kitchen; bar service lane at least 2 blocks clear, Palm Bar and Mini Fridge reservation, stools and dining tables, separate 3x3 seafood prep bay for Farmer's Delight cutting board/cooking pot/stove plus normal prep and fuel access. Empty barrel/pantry; one reserved Keg for Cocktails Delight/Brewin' and Chewin', no brewed drink or cheese grants. All stations installed only after exact recipe/state audit. 
- x3..12 z17..21: shaded tackle/filleting station and weatherproof storage; reserve tackle box, empty fish storage, seat and clear service access. x22..32 z17..21: sun deck and native beach furniture; preserve open access to beach.
- x14..20 z17..24: shared boardwalk and steps to natural bank. x15..19 z24..32: short 5x9 timber fishing pier with open-water end and clear casting lanes, no roof or overhead decorations at casting end. Leave water/belowgrade terrain outside spaced piles untouched; do not emit explicit air into water or rely on floating blocks. Site survey and variable terrain support/live placement acceptance pending; short piles cannot guarantee seabed support on arbitrary shores. A shallow-bank location or player-added extensions are required. Do not use by default on cliffs or inland slopes.
- x2..4 z1..3 and x30..32 z17..19 landscaping; native decorative palms if exact safe growth/block IDs verified; do not generate loot-bearing seashells, washed-up bottles, coconuts or merchant NPCs. Palm sprout only if early acquisition/recipe confirmed. No artificial water-filled treasure fishing basin. All remaining terrain sparse; no shaft/basement.
