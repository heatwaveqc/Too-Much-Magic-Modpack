# Farmer's Homestead — V1 design proposal
2026-11-19. Owner approved this as one of 19 personal graduation housing choices; requested design pass. Original architecture, no donor. Await construction approval. Not a generated structure, runtime grant, integration-ready feature or mod pin.

## Intent and aesthetic
A productive working country farm, distinct from Hearthkeeper (cozy living/companions/Roots) and Adventurer's Inn (hospitality, brewing, hunting contracts). Cream plaster and oak/spruce farmhouse, broad porch, stone plinth, warm wood floors, red timber/plank barn with pale trim and steep dark roof, accessible hayloft, fences, hedges and farm market stall with striped awning. No industrial tech, generic logistics, magic initiation stations, or decorative costly blocks without approval. Prefer verified Farmer's Delight/Furniture/Cooking for Blockheads native cabinets, crates only empty or genuinely decorative (crop storage-block recipes grant their contents; avoid filled produce crates/hay mountains), rope/supports and kitchen floor; vanilla fallback only where exact native state/recipe unverified. Target 2,400–4,200 ordinary nonair blocks provisionally, not measured; total site intentionally larger than stock starter footprint due to fields, not a hard value target. No new mods.

## Kitchen and equipment policy
Primary food mods exact pins below. Working connected kitchen with Cooking Table, oven, fridge, sink, two counters, tool rack, and a contiguous native kitchen floor/connector path to local pantry. Reserve 3×3 separate manual FD prep zone (cutting board, knife, cooking pot over compatible heat source/stove, skillet with safe heat and inventory interaction), include player-side 2-wide aisle, empty storage and a tool rack for Pam utensils. Native floor connects kitchen participants even when not adjacent per official docs, not all inventories are recognized by default. Coordinate #367 unified CFB+Occultism pantry initiative: reserve removable single pantry socket for future Occult Cooking/wormhole and local service access; no remote storage grant, no installed Occultism/Occult Cooking block, no claim of cross-mod automatic processing. Keep cutting board and brewing keg manual (no keg planned here; Inn owns brewing). Skillet/pot routing only when exact integration says supported; preserve recipes, heat, time, remainders and transformation diets.
Suggested one-time starter gear, exact recipes/effective override/acquisition pending: one Market (subject to full trade gate, player places to initialize native merchant), two Feeding Troughs, one Chicken Nest; one each FD Cutting Board, iron Knife, Cooking Pot, Stove and optionally Skillet if eligible; ordinary hoe, water bucket, crafted vanilla storage, furnace/smoker, two beds. One CFB Cooking Table, oven, sink, fridge, two counters and tool rack plus minimal connector floor are a recommended modest material exception, conditional on owner approval of potential diamond recipe cost and parity. Secondary 21.1.1 recipe guide reports two diamonds in Crafting Book used to craft Cooking Table; exact version 21.1.24 and current recipe overrides not verified (upstream connector lookups unreadable). If exception declined, reserve table socket and provide eligible simpler equipment; no extra diamonds or free duplicate Crafting Book. Appliances preferably manually placed from sole audited starter chest if native initialization uncertain, storage empty, no Cow in a Jar, free milk/food, enchantments, fertilizer stockpile, rare saplings, potions, or spawn eggs. Do not imply actual connected operation until native placement and exact kitchen discovery test. No Bountiful distribution or runtime change.
