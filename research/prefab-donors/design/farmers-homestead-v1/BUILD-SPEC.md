# Farmer's Homestead — V1 design proposal
2026-11-19. Owner approved this as one of 19 personal graduation housing choices; requested design pass. Original architecture, no donor. Owner requested refinements below; design remains pre-construction. Not a generated structure, runtime grant, integration-ready feature or mod pin.

## Intent and aesthetic
A productive working country farm, distinct from Hearthkeeper (cozy living/companions/Roots) and Adventurer's Inn (hospitality, brewing, hunting contracts). Cream plaster and oak/spruce farmhouse, broad porch, stone plinth, warm wood floors, red timber/plank barn with pale trim and steep dark roof, accessible hayloft, fences, hedges and farm market stall with striped awning. No industrial tech, generic logistics, magic initiation stations, or decorative costly blocks without approval. Prefer verified Farmer's Delight/Furniture/Cooking for Blockheads native cabinets, crates only empty or genuinely decorative (crop storage-block recipes grant their contents; avoid filled produce crates/hay mountains), rope/supports and kitchen floor; vanilla fallback only where exact native state/recipe unverified. Target 2,400–4,200 ordinary nonair blocks provisionally, not measured; total site intentionally larger than stock starter footprint due to fields, not a hard value target. No new mods.

### Integrated rice irrigation — 2026-11-19
Owner proposed using the ordinary field irrigation sources for rice instead of a separate paddy. Source-level support: upstream Farmer's Delight 1.21 branch at immutable commit dd8b9b0bfec916a2da88a6d64e3a37f4a02c3259, [RiceBlock.java](https://github.com/vectorwing/FarmersDelight/blob/dd8b9b0bfec916a2da88a6d64e3a37f4a02c3259/src/main/java/vectorwing/farmersdelight/common/block/RiceBlock.java): placement/survival requires water amount 8, and getFluidState unconditionally returns Fluids.WATER.getSource(false). Source rice is itself a water-fluid provider; vanilla farmland hydration detects water fluid states within four horizontal blocks, including diagonals. Four standard 9×9 fields therefore retain 320 dry-crop positions plus four rice centers. Leave former dedicated paddy as unfilled expansion space; overall estate dimensions unchanged. Build verification: four full water centers on dirt, open panicle space, all farmland within range; live test plant/harvest repeatedly, verify sustained hydration, and compatibility with Harvest with Ease. Exact pinned 1.3.4 JAR and runtime still pending; source-branch code is not exact artifact proof. Four plants are sufficient as a starter seed stock, not guaranteed bulk yield.
