# Adventurer's Inn — original guildhall and brewery (V1 design proposal)
2026-11-19. Owner approved the combined inn/brewery/hunting guild as one of the 19 personal graduation choices, replacing the proposed separate Hunter's Lodge. This turn is a design pass; construction approval and acceptance still pending. Not a generated template, deployed grant, runtime dependency or release-ready declaration. Original architecture.

## Concept
Traditional medieval roadside inn and fantasy adventurers' guild: stone plinth, pale plaster/dark oak/spruce timber frames, layered steep roofs and dormers, a large chimney, communal fireplace and tables, serving bar, contract counter, trophy/map wall, guest accommodations, working kitchen, below-grade fermentation cellar and compact practice yard. The inn is a social hub for visitors but one player's graduation choice; no independent guild/faction membership or land permissions implied. Emphasize food/drink crafting and social play, not a second hunter progression engine. No new mod dependency or autonomous mobs/guests, loot displays, filled barrels/kegs, enchanted weapons, bounty prizes or faction grants.
