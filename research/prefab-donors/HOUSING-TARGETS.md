# Too Much Magic starter housing — approved targets and donor tracker
Updated 2026-11-18. Authority: issue #236; next comprehensive QA required for base Prefab (#287, PR #352). Research only; do not index raw donors or launch a build.

## Approved design
Seventeen target options (fourteen magical, generic, Hearthkeeper and Castaway's Retreat), available in either player mode. Flexible footprint 16–32 blocks on each horizontal axis; roughly 500–3,000 ordinary placed blocks, not a hard cap. Basic living quarters, dedicated expandable magical workspace, comparable ordinary material/EMC value, no generated loot or entities, no advanced progression equipment. Generally include first functional crafting station if obtainable with basic vanilla materials (iron, wood, stone) or earliest readily available magical resources (blood, Source Gems, Dark Gems). Minor stations where justified; audit exact recipes and IDs later. Never bypass research, initiation, dimension, boss or tier gates. Expansions inherit parent housing; exclude Summoning Rituals, late-game Draconic Evolution/ProjectE/Expansion/Replication, universal artifacts/skills/CC:Tweaked, species-specific Dragon Survival.
Prefab 1.0.9 NeoForge 1.21.1 approved for next test; custom addon + LDLib2 still research candidates, no new dependency approval inferred. Verify placement, claims, one-time per-player reward, EMC/recipe bypass, terrain/air, preview and rotation.

**Native palette and functional architecture (owner approved 2026-11-18).** Use each school’s own decorative/building families preferentially (including native planks, trims, pillars, stairs and stone variants); use vanilla only where appropriate or where native equivalents are unavailable. Approved decorative materials may incidentally support later construction; do not grant gated machines, functional progression components, consumables or research completion. Record material counts, native IDs/states and progression/EMC implications; explicit decorative exceptions (Astral marble and infused wood) are not blanket authorization for other gated resources. Audit all existing original and donor houses against this standard before acceptance; no blanket automatic palette conversion. Functional layout must match intended installation depth, sky and overhead clearance, subfloor access, fluid/automation access and independent multiblock upgrades, not just nominal bounding boxes. Test actual site, roof and vertical clearance in live QA.
