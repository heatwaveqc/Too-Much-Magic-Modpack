# Caliber Arms — Credits

Caliber Arms is made by the **Caliber Arms Team** and released under the **MIT License** (see `LICENSE.txt`).

All code, 3D models, item textures, particles, the scope overlay and the logo were made for this mod.
The models and textures are generated procedurally by the Python scripts in `tools/`.

All **sound effects** come from recordings in the **public domain (CC0 1.0)**.
CC0 does not require credit, but the people below deserve it.
Every sound was edited for the mod: trimmed, layered, converted to mono at 48 kHz, loudness-matched and saved as OGG Vorbis.
The "far" and "distant" gunshot versions add filtering and reverb on top of the original recordings.

- Python, NumPy, SciPy, Pillow and soundfile, used for the asset pipeline. FFmpeg was used for audio encoding.
