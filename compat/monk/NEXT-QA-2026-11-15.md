# Monk progression — next full-integration QA (2026-11-15)

**Source:** `fix/monk-tree-data-registry-2026-11-15`. Monk Gradle build and unit tests passed on SHA `627f6eb` after the code changes. **Client skill purchase, spells, TreeChop and harvest tier have NOT been player-tested.**

## Deliberate changes

1. Correct Vampirism 1.10 NeoForge tree resource folders to `vampirism/skill_trees` and `vampirism/skill_nodes`; correct rank-one faction unlock predicate.
2. Keep free root `monk_root` but remove automatic Step Assist. Monks receive one native skill point at faction rank 1; first purchasable branches are Open Palm (+1 empty-hand damage), Unarmored Poise (+2 natural armor), and Stone Fist (stone-tier barehand harvest, +4 efficiency). Stone Fist excludes `needs_iron_tool` and `needs_diamond_tool` blocks.
3. Fleet Footwork (+5% movement speed) follows Open Palm, and Step Assist (+0.5 step height) follows Fleet Footwork. All conditional modifiers turn off on invalid gear/faction and return when valid.
4. Monk mastery remains independent of global Tyz/MXP. Existing cumulative rank thresholds still 20 / 55 / 105; first-time food +8, direct hostile kill +4.
5. HT TreeChop 0.19.3: optional public event listener captures up to 256 candidate log positions before a final fell, credits only positions no longer containing logs after the fell, and avoids double credit from individual drop events. Partial chops and leaves do not count. This listener uses reflection to avoid mandatory TreeChop linkage; check that it logs successful registration.
6. Player-attributed `BlockDropsEvent`: empty-hand training-block breaks or compatible Roots / Ars Nouveau / Iron's magic focuses award +1 per completed block, not a conventional tool. A `BlockEvent.BreakEvent` magic-focus fallback checks the target became air before awarding and deduplicates drop credit. Synthetic owner-less magic destruction and other source mods are not yet covered: source-specific adapters will be needed for spells that bypass both events.

## Required disposable-world test

1. Complete First Sunset as a fresh character. At **rank 1**, open the native Monk tree: verify root is present, can purchase an initial choice, and **cannot Step Assist automatically**.
2. Take Open Palm, confirm +1 empty-hand damage; take Poise at next rank, confirm +2 armor; equip conventional weapon/armor and confirm suppression, then unequip.
3. On a new character purchase Stone Fist first. Break stone/iron ore by empty hand and confirm a dropped block item; test obsidian/diamond ore *must not* get higher-tier hand harvest. Confirm +4 hand mining efficiency only with skill/discipline.
4. At higher ranks purchase Fleet Footwork, then Step Assist; confirm Step Assist waits for these earlier purchases. Check relog, death, respec and equipment toggles.
5. Use TreeChop barehand on a 7-log tree: verify 7 removed log positions yield 7 native mastery, not 1 or 8; make a partial chop and confirm 0 completed-log credit. Then ordinary hand-break a standalone log: +1. Check leaves never grant log credit.
6. Player-cast mining from Roots, Ars Nouveau and Iron's: validate each exact spell and inspect the `/tmmmonk mastery` delta. Test regular pickaxe/axe/shovel: 0. Break protected/canceled blocks: 0. Owner-less spell adapters remain a follow-up if absent.
7. Rejoin and check native points, rank and food discovery still persist. Check global Tyz/MXP wallet unchanged by Monk mastery.

## Important caveats

- No world migration is required: all worlds are disposable pre-alpha; a formerly free Step Assist will be replaced by a rank-gated skill.
- Magic mining is *source-proven only when the event provides a ServerPlayer*. There is deliberately **no** nearby-block scan, fake-player fallback or free XP for spell attempts.
- The native skill point provider and tree unlock have compiled but need actual native Vampirism client/server purchase validation. Do not promote to alpha on CI alone.
- No new combined test build is requested by this change; import this branch when the existing full-integration workflow next runs.
