# Classic Monk vanilla benchmark — #721 / #722

2026-11-25 research checkpoint, Java Edition 1.21.1 survival. Source baseline 4a027c5f0e78af0cc08bb004e1bbb4ca3688a42a. This is a design/endpoint survey, not final balance approval or exact installed-runtime proof. Current live Monk fixes remain separate. No runtime changes.

## Controlling owner decisions — 2026-11-25 (supersedes conflicting preliminary targets below)

- Ordinary full-charge intrinsic barehand damage endpoint **20 HP** (10 hearts) before criticals, mitigation or external effects. Deliberate rounded target, not a claim about vanilla maximum. Exclude spear/mace and other specialized weapon combat from the Classic benchmark; no dedicated falling smash/specialist damage skill needed. Baseline fist 1 HP means +19 HP total purchased (exact rank allocation pending). Crit behavior and external stacking still to be reviewed.
- Intrinsic maximum health **40 HP** (20 hearts), replacing the earlier 26 HP proposal and optional absorption. No Monk-granted absorption mechanic; ordinary external consumables are unaffected unless separately decided. Baseline 20 HP means +20 HP total purchased.
- Retain natural armor/toughness and general protection as defensive targets. Put separate damage-type resistance/protection skill decisions on hold: do not allocate Classic ranks to separate Blast/Projectile Protection, Resistance, or other damage wards until reviewed alongside the endgame defense benchmark. General protection, full fire/lava immunity, full fall immunity and accelerated negative potion clearing are the intended foundation. Original blast mitigation parity can be satisfied via general protection if its actual damage-source coverage and effective endpoint suffice; verify, do not assume. Purification must preserve trial blindness and protected boss/system effects. Exact cleansing cadence, protection formula, layering/caps, and interactions with external effects pending.
- **49 effective mining speed** on appropriate blocks is accepted Classic endpoint; independent harvesting eligibility challenges remain unchanged. One-off wood training speed baseline remains outside budget.
- The 104-rank allocation below is historical feasibility evidence only and is now stale; recalculate when remaining stat inventory and protection choices are complete. No final split or skill rank allocation approved. Suggested illustrative flat increments: +1.9 damage over 10 ranks, +2 HP over 10 ranks, or fewer larger ranks; these are not approved and the meaning of the 10% guideline still needs resolution.

## Reference policy

Use legitimate survival armor, tools, enchantments, potions and beacons. Preserve original Monk where stronger. Command-only effects/enchantments, arbitrary damage exploits, Creative reach/flight excluded. Compare alternate valid loadouts instead of treating incompatible enchants as one item. Because Monk embodies equipment, proposals to combine best alternate loadouts or maintain temporary potion effects indefinitely require owner review. Do not double-grant the same vanilla potion or beacon effect on top of intrinsic equivalence without an explicit stacking decision. Additive numerical skill ranks preferred; distinct vanilla mitigation layers retain native formulas. Meaningful rank guideline measured against starting or terminal reference as appropriate; exact 10% interpretation TBD (late integer levels and finite endpoints may not permit relative 10% at every step). Do not use fixed 100/120 budget as excuse for filler. HP = half-heart.

## Martial

- Netherite sword 8 HP, 1.6 attack speed; Sharpness V +3, Strength II +6 = 17 raw HP on full-charge ordinary hit. Crit (8+6)*1.5+3=24. Netherite axe 10 + 3 + 6 =19, critical 27, slower .9 speed. Smite/Bane V +12.5 instead of Sharpness +3 on qualified targets. Not additive with Sharpness. Mace Density V is fall-dependent situational attack, not finite ordinary melee stat; distinct optional technique or benchmark, not flat fist requirement. Breach IV, Wind Burst III are situational/incompatible with Density where applicable. Bow/trident/crossbow mechanics excluded from basic fist benchmark unless owner approves separate abilities.
- Original Monk +16 fist =17. Propose this as everyday sword benchmark (not 19 axe peak); if all +16 encoded as flat attack attribute, crit 25.5 vs sword 24. User favors simple additive fist damage; document this difference. Current incoming-event melee bonus added after crit differs. Actual full-charge single hit vs DPS separate: empty hand base attack speed 4, Haste II adds 20% =>4.8; sword 1.92. Target hurt immunity means not every fully charged attack deals damage.
- Sweeping Edge III: 75% scaling plus base sweep damage under native conditions. Knockback II, Looting III. Fire Aspect II (8 seconds burn) optional ignition toggle/skill; no assumption that fist grants magic ranged attacks. Thorns III across four armor pieces separately discussed below.

## Body

- Full netherite 20 armor /12 toughness /0.4 knockback resistance. Java armor formula for raw incoming ordinary armor-reducible damage D: min(20,max(A/5,A-D/(2+T/4)))/25. Protection IV all four gives 16 EPF =64%, specialized valid mixes can reach cap 20 EPF=80%. EPF applies after armor where applicable. Resistance II beacon =40% further reduction, Turtle Master IV=80% with -90% movement; don't turn IV into unrestricted passive. For D=20, armor reduction 64%, remaining 7.2, Prot IV ->2.592, Resistance II ->1.5552 HP (92.224% total); IV ->0.5184 (97.408%). Damage bypass tags differ; never multiply an extra specialized 80% atop 64% general EPF. Armor/EPF/Resistance are distinct layers, additive ranks within each.
- Original fire/lava and fall full immunity as owner-defined challenge rewards. No bought fire/fall reduction. Original blast reduction 50% independent of armor; proposed native EPF model exceeds it late, ensure early original power available. General Protection and Resistance should be distinct stat owners or consolidated in one skill with both effects; avoid redundant overlapping skills. Native shield can completely block many frontal attacks; original Catch Arrows is narrower. General shield-like parry optional separate decision, not passive global immunity.
- Health 20 HP vanilla, preserve existing registered classic +6 HP =26. Enchanted golden apple Absorption IV =16 temporary HP; do not convert to permanent max health or instant refill. Optional rechargeable absorption with cooldown, separate from health. Totem of Undying death-save not passive immortality; reserve for optional late mechanic. Regeneration II 1 HP/25t=.8 HP/s; food saturation healing separate, not automatic infinite food. Original hunger discipline ~50% exhaustion reduction. Instant Health II restores 8 HP once; no unconditional per-tick grant. Thorns III each armor item independently 45% chance, native aggregate behavior and damage cap should be checked against exact JAR before implementing; proposed one rank per level across equivalent full set. Purification replaces ordinary harmful potion effects per owner; preserve trial blindness and protected boss effects.

## Movement and gathering

- Standard player movement attribute 0.1, Speed II +40%=0.14. Original Monk source MonkAbilitySpeed uses multiply-base amount 0.8 at max, doubled while sprinting, so 0.18 walk /0.26 sprint before ordinary sprint modifier. Preserve that original advantage, no additional separate sprint skill. Original jump ×1.2, Jump Boost II 0.42+0.2=0.62 blocks/tick, stronger. Native Jump Boost also adds safe fall distance, superseded by challenge immunity. Step Assist, water walk, glide, night vision, full breath as challenges. Invisibility, Frost Walker II and controlled slow falling are optional distinct/toggle choices; water walking and Frost Walker overlap, no mandatory world mutation. Elytra+rockets is sustained noncreative flight benchmark, retain strict barehand Wither gate. Original blink and undead gaze retain unique mechanics.
- Depth Strider III gives full water movement efficiency; Aqua Affinity removes underwater mining slowdown (0.2→1). Swift Sneak III 0.30→0.75. Soul Speed III +61.5% on soul terrain. Dolphin's Grace is environment-dependent not potion; conduit power grants underwater breathing/night vision/haste in range; no stacking Haste and conduit mining bonuses. Use native movement and terrain rules; not permanent dolphin-speed floor. Survival reach 4.5 blocks /3 entities; no Classic investment required. Vehicle, boat ice, Riptide/wind charge external travel not flat walking baseline.
- Wood challenge gives native wooden equivalent speed 2 on applicable tool blocks, no correct-for-drops entitlement; five stone grants wooden harvest. Independent material challenges grant native eligibility, no speed attached. Netherite appropriate tool speed 9 + Efficiency V (1+5²=26)=35; Haste II ×1.4=49 effective mining speed. Stone hardness1.5 needs45 for insta-mine with correct drops; deepslate hardness3 needs90, so not instant. Obsidian hardness50 takes ceil(1500/49)=31 ticks (~1.55s) before repeat delay. Native block/tool family and special shears/sword behavior not universal speed; don't make every block instant or drop incorrectly. Golden tool speed12 yields (12+26)*1.4=53.2 on valid blocks; 49 chosen as all-purpose netherite cap, not absolute material-specific fastest speed. Airborne penalty distinct. Fortune III, Looting III integer levels, mutually exclusive Silk Touch toggle, no fractional proxy drops.
- No purchased durability stats: Mending/Unbreaking irrelevant to bare hands. Equipment-specific mechanics such as crossbow multishot or trident channeling not included absent owner decision. Utility potions (night vision, fire resistance, water breathing, invisibility, slow falling) belong to challenge/toggle inventory; restorative and special consumables require specific design, not automatic permanent effects.
