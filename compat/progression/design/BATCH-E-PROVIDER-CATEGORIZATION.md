1. 2. MXP: actual successful personal paid expenditure, recognized completed meaningful rites, or existing one-time advancement/craft/boss outcome. Attribute registration does NOT mean successful payment is observed. Parent addon inheritance is not a separate payer or another reward. Preserve current approved MXP contract rather than obsolete numerical tables from historical design. Do not promote proposals automatically.
