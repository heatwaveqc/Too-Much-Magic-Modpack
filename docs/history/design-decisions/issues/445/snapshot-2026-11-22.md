## Deferred design candidate — NOT in current sprint or approved for implementation

**Owner direction (2026-11-21):** Keep adaptation of **Vampire's Delight** into the JAWR-only TMM pack **as a possible future integration**. Do **not** install TeamLapen Vampirism/Werewolves or the original Vampire's Delight, do not start a source port, do not add scope to the Nov 21 weekend preview or the Christmas alpha solely for this candidate, and do not assign it to hourly coding/integration work without fresh owner approval.

### Why defer the broad port

- Stock Vampire's Delight 1.21.1's `neoforge.mods.toml` **hard-requires** TeamLapen `vampirism` and Farmer's Delight. It imports Vampirism classes throughout, uses its `VampirePlayer.drinkBlood` API, and makes faction/registry assumptions incompatible with TMM's JAWR-primary blood system. The pack manifest explicitly retired `vampirism`, `werewolves`, and `vampires-delight` in favor of JAWR.
- It contains attractive cuisine (blood sausage, blood pie, blood wine, blood syrup, etc.), but a full fork would inherit many non-culinary systems and dependencies. A future first-party culinary feature should selectively adapt/reimagine only the desired foods, use JAWR native blood capacity, actual canonical blood inputs, Farmer's Delight infrastructure, and respect resource conservation/anti-arbitrage. Audit upstream code/art licenses individually and preserve credits.

### Narrower Blood Wine question — source feasibility only, not greenlight

TMM *already pins* **Brewin' and Chewin' NeoForge 4.5.0**, with a native **Aging Cask** that accepts a `WineItem` and up to eight `distillates`, including potion-bearing stacks with nonempty `POTION_CONTENTS`. It writes fermented potion effects into `BnCDataComponents.WINE_CONTENTS`; wine consumption applies those effects and returns a glass bottle. This is the actual native potion→wine system owner is referring to. It is independent of stock Vampire's Delight and can be extended **without adding Vampirism or forking either upstream mod**.

**Technical constraint:** A JAWR full blood bottle/wine glass is **not automatically a potion** and its authoritative blood content is not a `MobEffectInstance`. Simply placing it in the `brewinandchewin:distillates` item tag would admit it as a cask input but would **NOT** carry its blood into `WINE_CONTENTS`; `BnCWineUtils.age` only processes named `Distillate` bonuses or effects and cask clears consumed inputs. A tag-only/recipe-only approach can yield ordinary flavored wine, **not a JAWR blood-refilling wine**. Do not invent a fake generic potion effect as the vampire blood resource because such effects may be rebottled/amplified through cross-mod chains.

**Future recommended narrow design if owner separately approves:**
- Input: ordinary Brewin' and Chewin' **red wine** + a **verified real filled blood bottle** (accept canonical allowed cross-mod sources at an audited conversion rate). Process in the **native Aging Cask**, preserving its own aging duration and existing optional potion additions.
- Output: still based on native wine mechanics, but recognized/marked as **Blood Wine** with a small TMM server-authoritative provenance/paid-blood amount component; preserve existing `WINE_CONTENTS` effects and alcohol drawbacks, glass return. Possibly display a custom variant name on the existing wine item to avoid a dedicated new wine item/model.
- On legitimate consumption: preserve normal wine effects, **also restore JAWR's native blood meter** from the paid blood ingredient once (not player hunger, not a generic replicable status effect); no feeding credit to nonvampires or other blood meters. Determine exact dose/ingredients after JAWR native API verification; marker must survive cask processing and not be forgeable by ordinary potion rebottling. No free container/blood duplications.
- Engineering work: one cask validation/aging integration hook or equivalent narrow source-compatible adapter, one final wine-consumption hook and the blood provenance/storage component, plus exact pinned release checks. Estimated **small-to-moderate first-party compatibility task**, notably much less work than a whole Vampire's Delight port, but **more than tags/JSON** if the resulting wine should actually feed vampires. Vanilla potion fortification works natively and needs no adapter. QA: real blood debit vs delivered blood, slot/cask refund, wine effect preservation, stacks and serialization, cross-dimension/relog, vampire/human/dragon handling, creative and automation.

**Decision status:** Broad vampire culinary adaptation **DEFERRED**; Blood Wine **FEASIBILITY RESEARCH ONLY, NOT APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION**. No recipe/cost/value is locked; do not silently implement until owner decides whether Blood Wine should be a blood-feeding special cask product or a cosmetic/potion-only flavor.
