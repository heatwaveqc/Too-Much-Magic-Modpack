## Balance first pass — DRAFT, not owner-approved final values

Native source at 4d8d3c5df7c08abad6b019e8e4c53be38c94faaf: BountyCreator chooses initial rewards, samples their amount, filters filler by forbids, selects a filler within 25% variance or closest fallback, rounds its amount UP and clamps to its configured range. Its target is reward worth × (1 + filler difficulty %) × getDiscount(rep). BoardBlockEntity clamps board reputation to -30..30; getDiscount for this interval = 1 - rep/75. Source is not a verified exact beta.2 binary; confirm before enabling. Native expiry is around 749 seconds plus filler worth × 0.35 × timeMult, and a board update defaults to 45 seconds. Do not infer cadence or timer based on rarity alone.

Proposed: one reward, one objective preferred (no decree mixing); use +75% filler difficulty so target delivery cost is 2.45× underlying reward worth at rep -30, 1.75× at 0, 1.05× at 30. Existing pure-item arbitrage guard remains; leveling a board makes trading materially more attractive. Reward worth = max(effective unit EMC, tier minimum effort budget / minimum payout count) / 16; delivery worth = effective item EMC / 16. The /16 is only Bountiful matching scale, not an EMC exchange rate; reduces oversized expiry durations. Kill worth = reviewed effort budget /16; native kill tracker and explicit kill allowlist, 3× time multiplier. Set proposed minimum effort by increasing rarity: 64/256/1024/4096/8192 EMC-equivalent per minimum reward bundle; native rarity weights retained, proposed initial reward rep thresholds -30/0/5/15/25. The lowest enabled tier always available at -30; a single-output premium decree has premium effort cost at all reputation and does not claim rarity alone makes that output infrequent. No empty rep-only board. Gold ingots and diamonds are proposed high-value delivery candidates for expensive schools, not new payout routes. General payment may use emerald items, not a new currency or direct Quest Shop credits; amounts are provisional.

Compiler optional profile native_v1 generates native variable quantity ranges from reviewed inputs, validates all reward/objective pairs at the worst eligible board reputation and excludes unaffordable pairs with native forbids. It rejects any reward without at least one eligible objective. Proof: maximum allowed objective count covers the largest payout × target ratio at its minimum eligible rep (at most 2.45); ceil rounding then covers the real target at each reputation and target ≥ reward EMC at the best reputation. No claim that this prevents every gameplay exploit (native generator/add-on compatibility, unknown final EMC, kill farms, component-bearing items, dropped/copied slips remain tests). The fixed compiler path remains for backwards fixture support. Tests enumerate every rep in -30..30 for synthetic inputs; test outcomes not yet observed. No actual balance review input, runtime config or packs generated. Bountiful final config must use the native profile only after review.
