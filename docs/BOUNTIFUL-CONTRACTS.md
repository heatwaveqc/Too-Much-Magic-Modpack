## Owner-directed refinement — 2026-11-19

- Native redemption remains at the board. BoardBlockEntity source queues objectives for nearby villagers to collect after completion, simulates trade XP and restocks their trades. The Villager Retaliation compatibility report concerns this secondary mechanic; Villager Retaliation is not present in the examined Tyr mod manifest. Keep native visits unless exact-pack testing finds a problem.
- Thematic reward pools first: Iron's ink/arcane essence, Ars essences, etc. Currency may be appropriate but is not mandatory. Candidate IDs drawn from the existing quest/economy files where identified; descriptive themes are not registry IDs. All tiers and exact quantities remain subject to normal gate and value review. Repeatable jobs are allowed to produce useful value through time/effort; a pure item hand-in should not create a material arbitrage by itself.
- Native entity kill objectives instead of a Hunter hook. The source counts matching entities for carried bounties. Test normal native behavior, including multiple carried slips; no attempt to impose the Hunter attribution mechanics or suppress intended parallel Hunter skill progression. Explicit allowlist, no PvP or boss kill pool by default. Per-kill EMC-equivalent payout budget is a balancing review input, not a new exchange rate.
- Transformation decree acquisition: disable canSpawn/canReveal/canWanderBuy; craft a specific decree as the eligible form. Source registers bountiful:decree_data with fields ids (set of strings) and rank (integer), and default decree recipe returns bountiful:decree. Candidate output component is {"bountiful:decree_data":{"ids":["tmm_vampire"],"rank":1}}; confirm exact beta.2 codec before generating recipes. Prefer a genuinely form-exclusive acquisition recipe; if normal ingredients can be traded, a player-aware acquisition check is needed for literal form-only creation. Do not add claim-time gates. A crafted decree can be placed on a shared board and other players can accept its contracts; transferability is an explicit implication of acquisition-only policy, not a verified bound-item restriction. No form activation or gated item reward. Keep forms ungenerated until exact acquisition method and roster verified; any executable state check belongs to designated Progression/transformations owner.
