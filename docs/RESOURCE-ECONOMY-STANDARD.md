# TMM resource economy — owner-approved accounting baseline (2026-11-17)

Status: owner approved the accounting rates and audit targets; implementation and final item map remain pending. Ore hierarchy and Fortune/Philosopher's Stone exceptions are owner-approved; exact base prices below are recommended audit seeds, not a final deployed map. Tracks #47 (EMC coverage), #44 (ProjectE gates), #318/#319 (parchment). Source baseline: integration 2f662b9e8b73cee47a1e23bf6d1f3510dd41d8ae.

## Locked accounting constants

| Resource | Units per 1 EMC |
| --- | ---: |
| EMC | 1 |
| Botania Mana | 20 |
| Forge Energy / equivalent OP (verify native transport parity) | 200 |
| Ordinary canonical EvilCraft blood / compatible fluid and F&A blood essence | 5 mB or essence units (proposed 1:1 bridge approved) |
| Neo Vitae Raw Spiritus | 1 |
| Neo Vitae aspected Spiritus (Ruina, Nihilum, Vindicta, Invictus) | 0.5 |
| Forbidden & Arcanus Souls | 1/32 (32 EMC per Soul) |

These are valuation units, not an instruction to expose universal bidirectional exchange. Classify transactions as equivalent (conserve value), lossy (convenience), or productive (explicit renewable resource, time, infrastructure or progression investment). Record deliberate profitable paths and reject unintended unbounded conversion cycles. Compute net value from all inputs, outputs and container remainders; use final in-game EMC map, not assumed defaults. Do not nerf intended farms solely because they produce EMC.

## Implementation targets, not yet deployed

- Keep Mana Fluxfield at 10 FE/Mana; verify actual transfer, buffers and throttling.
- ProjectE Energy Converters 0.1.4: documented defaults 1,000 EMC/FE forward and 1,000 FE/EMC reverse. Eventual target 160 FE per EMC and 250 FE per EMC reverse (80% efficiency each direction; 64% round trip). Retain existing 1,000 FE/EMC reverse until major loops are audited. Verify exact config type/semantics, fractional conversion or batching support and source debit, integer conservation, tiers/throughput, gating, and OP support before implementation. Never change just one direction without checking the combined economy.
- Botania Conjuration Catalyst: price net item gain at 20 Mana per EMC, round up to 100 Mana and apply minimum 200 Mana. Example upstream EMC values (not final pack map): coal 2,600, redstone 1,300, glowstone dust 7,700, quartz 5,200, soul sand 1,000, netherrack 200 Mana. Audit every recipe, including addon/pack recipes; recompute if EMC changes.
- Mana-refined materials: baseline item EMC plus consumed Mana / 20; use integer rounding consistently. Existing native infusion costs: manasteel 3,000, mana pearl 6,000, mana diamond 10,000 Mana. Example upstream item values 406, 1,324, 8,692 respectively; these are illustrative and must follow final metal map. Parchment follows the standard in reverse: pin one fragment to seven times final paper EMC P, then price one-paper infusion at (7P − P) × 20 = 120P Mana. At P=32, fragment=224 EMC and infusion=3,840 Mana (supersedes 100,000); retain reverse seven-paper recipe. This is balanced, not a special economic exception. See #318/#319.
- Preserve approved Thermalily buff: 75 Mana/tick for 600 ticks = 45,000 Mana per lava source under sufficient transfer, zero cooldown. Intentional productive infrastructure; audit renewable lava and ProjectE/Volcanite loops, not a reason to revert the owner-approved buff automatically.
- Preserve native Draconic Generator efficiency differences pending audit. Coal: 80,000 / 24,000 / 16,000 / 12,800 / 8,000 FE/OP-equivalent by Eco Plus, Eco, Normal, Performance, Performance Plus (source calculation). Reactor is intended to be highly productive endgame infrastructure; measure real net power including containment and fuel.
- EvilCraft tier-3 bone + 2,500 mB blood -> blaze rod is an approved productive progression example (upstream 144 + 500 -> 1,536 EMC, net +892). Do not automatically reprice every inefficient convenience recipe. Ordinary blood is valued 50 EMC per 250 mB and 200 per 1,000 mB; maintain canonical blood boundaries.
- Fluid transmutation via filled containers is a candidate, not permission to bypass gates: container value plus fluid (ordinary blood 200/bucket, water 0, lava upstream mapping 64/bucket). Audit component-bearing filled items, hardened blood, ordinary vs special JAWR blood and every drain/fill loop before enabling synthesis. No generic direct fluid machine assumed.
- Botania/Ars machine bridge native default 15 Mana per Source implies 0.75 EMC/Source as a candidate derived valuation; player Ars mana is a separate resource, native directional personal bridge is asymmetric. Audit before extending standard.

## Development target: TMM Economy (first-party compatibility module)

Extend ProjectE's native calculation, not a fork or a hand-maintained per-item map. Verify API against pinned PE1.1.0; upstream 1.21.1 source supports `@RecipeTypeMapper`, `IRecipeTypeMapper`, `@EMCMapper`, `IMappingCollector.addConversion`/fixed values, `NSSFluid`, and abstract `NSSFake` mapping nodes. Start with Botania Revived mana infusion/Runic Altar and EvilCraft Blood Infuser, then add adapters by recipe family for Extra Botany, Neo Vitae, Draconic Evolution, Ars Nouveau, Embers, Theurgy, Replication and Iron's, and any other installed resource-using types. Read effective loaded recipe objects, including KubeJS/datapack adjustments; never hardcode individual outputs as a substitute for adapter coverage. Optional dependencies and explicit mapper priority; log unsupported recipe IDs rather than silently dropping costs.

One central resource-rate configuration, exact rational arithmetic through aggregate recipe costs; resource rates and units explicit. Abstract costs can be whole-EMC synthetic mapping contributions or exact batched/scaled nodes where supported, not integer-per-Mana or per-mB zero values; validate quantization, overflow, output counts, multiple consumed items, returned containers/catalysts, tags and component-aware identity. Use NSSFluid where its arithmetic represents the rate exactly; otherwise retain rational cost accounting at adapter boundary. No physical item or arbitrary new conversion mechanic required for an abstract resource. Unpriced energy remains explicitly unresolved until audited.

Valuation policy: (1) authoritative fixed/relative constraints for ore families and other approved anchors; (2) full resource-aware cost of ordinary magical recipes; (3) native recipe inference; (4) manually researched root values for unresolved items. Root values feed the graph before propagation; they are not a late override. ProjectE's cheapest-path and exploit detection are separate concerns: explicitly classify productive routes and prevent only those approved routes from collapsing established anchors or being incorrectly zeroed. Inspect fixed-before/fixed-from/after precedence, reverse recipes, mapper interactions and reload order with exact-pin tests; do not disable exploit safeguards globally. Export provenance per mapped recipe, cost breakdown, skipped productive routes, unresolved recipes/items, and final EMC map. Test downstream propagation, deterministic reload, modified recipe costs, and deliberately profitable Fortune/ore/Blaze Rod paths. No live server build without separate approval.

### Development target: EMC-to-Mana Pool catalyst

Owner-approved **EMC Catalyst** (final name): directly beneath a Mana Pool, draw from the placing player's personal EMC balance, at exactly 20 Mana/EMC (100% efficiency); one-way, no artificial throughput cap. Each loaded server tick fill as much of the actual pool deficit as the account can fund, subject to the pool's native capacity. Standard 1,000,000-Mana pool costs 50,000 EMC from empty. No spreader, source item slot, adjacent link, or intermediate energy buffer required. Crafting must require ProjectE; exact recipe remains open. Do not conflate economic equivalence with free generation.

Reference [Project Expansion 1.21.1 BlockEMCLink](https://github.com/DonovanDMC/ProjectExpansion/blob/1.21.1/src/main/java/cool/furry/mc/neoforge/projectexpansion/block/BlockEMCLink.java) placement and [BlockEntityEMCLink](https://github.com/DonovanDMC/ProjectExpansion/blob/1.21.1/src/main/java/cool/furry/mc/neoforge/projectexpansion/block/entity/BlockEntityEMCLink.java) item/fluid withdrawal: use equivalent persisted placer UUID and ProjectE IKnowledgeProvider BigInteger debit/sync via supported account access. Do NOT reuse Link EMCHandler.extractEmcBigInteger, which returns the requested amount without debiting in the inspected source (also blocked by the pinned energy converter). Implement independent block entity, using public API or permitted upstream approach rather than blindly copying code; inspect exact Project Expansion 1.0.6 and ProjectE PE1.1.0 binaries, license, offline account availability and FTB/owner permission behavior. Fail closed if no authorized account is available; no chunk forcing. Bound whole-EMC debit by deficit / 20 and balance; preserve exact conservation (up to 19 Mana residual deficit with whole-unit transfers, or accounted persistent credit if exact sub-unit refill is supported). Debit/credit on server thread, check actual accepted Mana, correct partial failures, avoid overflowing BigInteger/long/int; do not debit if full/missing/unloaded/unauthorized. Sync only on changes; ensure no owner impersonation, duplication, or stale account spend after block movement/removal. No arbitrary limit below pool capacity. QA: initial fill, per-tick refill at high drain, owner vs other player, offline/relogin (policy pending API proof), low/zero EMC, partial deficit, multiple catalysts same owner, full/absent/replaced pool, chunk unload and restart, cross-energy round trips. Preserve existing catalyst types and ordinary pools. Original Botania-compatible texture/model, localization and Lexica/quest integration. No gameplay implementation or full build authorized by this record.

Related QoL: already-pinned Extra Botany Revived 1.0.4 includes `extrabotany:mana_charger` (one livingrock slab above two livingwood twigs; block above pool, one real inventory slot, native Mana Item transfer up to 1,000 Mana/tick, pool mode controls charge vs discharge). Source [ManaChargerBlockEntity](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/java/velrondevs/extrabotany/common/block/block_entity/ManaChargerBlockEntity.java) and [ChargerBlockEntity](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/java/velrondevs/extrabotany/common/block/block_entity/ChargerBlockEntity.java). Verify in-game rather than add a new mod just for charging stands. Applied Botanical Machinery is available for 1.21.1 but depends on AE2 (excluded); no automatic roster change.

## Owner-approved scope refinement and Neo Vitae / Forbidden & Arcanus valuation (2026-11-17)

Do not rebalance native internal recipes or exhaustively audit internal mechanics. Focus on currencies that create/transform EMC-bearing items or create FE, cross-system exchange, mapping completeness, and explicit approved balance changes. Physical item reagents use item EMC; pure casting/progression pools with no material/power economy can remain unpriced. Previous full audit list is a candidate discovery checklist, not a mandate to reprice internal recipes.

- Neo Vitae: canonical altar fluid EV 5 units/EMC via existing 1:1 blood compatibility; personal Anima (player-bound network) unvalued, no direct converter or mapper energy debit. Crystallized Spiritus (harvested shard, not the four-shard placeable cluster): each shard yields 50 Spiritus according to pinned-version guide and SpiritusCrystalItem. Owner locks raw shard at 50 EMC; each of the four aspected shards at 100 EMC. Thus 1 EMC per Raw Spiritus, 2 EMC per aspected Spiritus; distinct aspect charges must be tracked rather than averaging. Derived costs for actual crystals/gems/charged variants must distinguish item components and stored quantities, not treat empty or placeable clusters as full fuel. Preserve native ritual productivity as intentional. [Guide source](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/src/datagen/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/datagen/book/spiritus/CrystallizedSpiritusEntry.java).
- Forbidden & Arcanus: Soul = 32 EMC, native Corrupt Soul supplies one soul unit and Enchanted Soul supplies ten (320 EMC reference); ordinary canonical blood essence 1 unit = 1 mB = 0.2 EMC, include in compatibility as far as native mechanics permit. Full Blood Test Tube supplies 3,000 essence = 600 EMC plus empty tube value; preserve components and actual container remainders. Essence is not a native fluid, so adapter needed, not merely a tag; no assumed existing conversion. Aureal is consumed by Forge; derive its EMC rate from computed Aureal Bottle value divided by 35 (account for any container remainder; source consumes the item). Experience deferred to pack-wide XP policy, XPetrified Orb supplies 91 XP. [Item registry source](https://github.com/stal111/Forbidden-Arcanus/blob/f62af610d550e0d033f6c5cd166e40062638c44b/neoforge/src/main/java/com/stal111/forbidden_arcanus/core/init/ModItems.java). Preserve native rituals, including costs.
- Candidate rates, NOT yet explicitly locked: Ember 2/EMC (100 FE/Ember native); Psi 10/EMC (Supply FE nominal 4,000 FE/200 Psi before CAD modifiers); Ars Source 4/3 per EMC (15 Mana/Source native bridge). Verify actual installed mechanics; separate ordinary vanilla XP from MXP/Hunter/relic/skill progression. Inventory XP carriers before selecting reference value.

## Ore hierarchy — owner-approved

Keep ore/raw/refined items in positive EMC coverage. Standard: metal ingot and canonical/equivalent dust base B; raw metal 6B; metal ore 18B (1 ore = 3 raw = 18 ingots); raw block 54B; ingot block 9B; nugget B/9. For direct-drop gems 1 ore = 3 gems nominally; multi-drop ores must be normalized to vanilla/native drop counts and Fortune limits separately. Equal ore variants for a family unless deliberate exception. No ore base prices approved yet.

Lower refined values instead of blindly increasing ore prices by 18x. ProjectE upstream 1.21.1 metals.json sets iron ingots to 256 EMC, derives copper at half iron, tin at iron, silver/lead at twice iron, nickel at four times iron, gold at eight times iron, and sets uranium to 4,096. Proposed base scale 9/128 (~7.03% of original refined values), equivalent to iron 18, copper 9, tin 18, silver/lead 36, nickel 72, gold 144, uranium 288; exact base values are audit seeds. This retains metal ratios and yields copper nuggets 1 and iron nuggets 2, with iron raw 108, ore 324, ingot block 162, raw block 972. All final registered nugget-bearing metal families must retain a positive integer value for nuggets; use bases divisible by nine, minimum nine, unless explicitly approved exception. Compare post-mapping values for all variants and gates. Gem values remain to be determined.

**Owner revision: remove upward Philosopher's Stone metal-to-gem crafting recipes.** Remove exactly `projecte:conversions/gold_ingot_to_diamond` (4 gold -> diamond) and `projecte:conversions/iron_ingot_to_ender_pearl` (4 iron -> pearl; upstream uses iron, not gold). Retain `projecte:conversions/diamond_to_gold_ingot` (diamond -> 4 gold, deliberately lossy), both iron/gold directions, and other native Stone recipes. Upstream has no matching pearl-to-metal crafting recipe; do not add one without approval. Keep diamond/emerald conversions unchanged for now and audit their final valuation together. This is a crafting-recipe restriction only, not a prohibition on transmuting learned diamonds/pearls via EMC or legitimate magic production. Audit mapping propagation and surviving world-transmutation paths for unintended bypasses; preserve boss-trophy Stone crafting and power flower gates. Isolated implementation: `kubejs/server_scripts/projecte_upward_material_conversions.js`; integration writer must reconcile packwiz/assembly ownership and verify exact IDs against the pinned PE1.1.0 JAR in live JEI, test both removed recipes and retained downward/iron-gold/gem conversions, and confirm final post-mapping EMC. Check smelting, alloys, dust unification, storage blocks, family intermediates and byproducts. Existing upstream ProjectE OreBlacklistMapper and RawMaterialsBlacklistMapper explicitly zero tagged items before/after mapping; disable/configure or replace their behavior and verify actual loaded overrides without collapsing the hierarchy through ordinary recipe propagation. Audit custom mappers and export actual post-map values. Do not assume vanilla defaults are final pack values.

**Owner exception: Fortune is explicitly intended to generate positive EMC**, particularly because manual mining takes effort. Do not cap or nerf Fortune simply to force conservation; audit ordinary and maximum enhanced Fortune and distinguish productive manual placement/mining from highly scalable automated variants. Fortune III single-drop ore averages 2.2; positive gain depends on family/roll and actual Fortune level; higher Fortune and multi-drop ores must be considered, not assumed capped at 3. Preserve special gated ores (e.g. uranium) and legitimate first acquisition. Ore generators/miners and Orechid receive increased valuations; audit output abundance. No arbitrary ceiling on other mods' ore processing, and no nerfs or tier rebalancing until native routes are measured.

**Neo Vitae priority:** exact version-aligned 1.1.53 guide source [OreProcessingEntry.java](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/src/datagen/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/datagen/book/spiritus/OreProcessingEntry.java) documents Tabula Vitae 2 dust/ore, Athanor 5 fragments/ore or 3/raw before Spiritus boost, saturated chunk 6/ore or 4/raw; fragments -> gravel -> dust -> ingot, with resonator byproducts, corrupted catalysts, better cutting fluids, and dynamic modded material registration. Audit actual generated recipes and pack materials.json, catalyst and advanced fluid bonus behavior, combined Fortune + Athanor yield, expected/max and automation before proposing changes. Three raw from a single mined ore can already become 12 ingots at saturated Athanor; higher Fortune/catalysts may rival or exceed 18. Preserve strong native processing as a legitimate specialty; ProjectE's advantage is convenient direct transmutation, not necessarily the highest possible yield.
