## 1. What a player can do, and where to begin

Neo Vitae offers an EV-powered altar ladder, personal Anima tied to Blood Orbs, Sigils, element/utility/crafting Alchemy Arrays, Spiritus harvesting and five aspect variants, Hellfire forging, Athanor ore processing, ritual construction, sentient weapons/armor, fluid/item/Spiritus routing, multi-recipe autocrafting, environmental alteration and a gated procedural Demon Realm. These are **separate actual systems**, not a rank or one-word tick per axis. The first usable loop is below. [S][W][R]

**New-player start (source-aligned, client not verified):**

1. Craft **Scriptura Vitae** (`neovitae:guide_book`) at a normal table with **one book and four redstone dust** in the cross/plus pattern: ` r /rbr/ r `. Right-click it. Consult **Utility → The Path of Ascension**, then **Ara Vitae**, **Orbs**, **Tabula Vitae**, **Alchemy Arrays**, **Spiritus**; JEI supplies per-recipe inputs. [S: `src/generated/resources/data/neovitae/recipe/guide_book.json`]
2. Obtain/craft **Ara Vitae** (`neovitae:ara_vitae`; exact Ara recipe in JEI). Place the **bare block**, with no rune ring (tier 0), into a safe work area with a consenting eligible player in range. Right-click a **diamond** on its basin. This tier-0 working costs **2,000 EV** and draws only the needed life for the first **Novicius Orb** without killing the source. Gray smoke / lost progress on depletion differs from a completed orb output. [W: `https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/wiki/Ara-Vitae-and-Runes.md`]
3. **Bind/charge the Orb.** Right-click the Novicius Orb (`neovitae:blood_orb_weak`); near an altar it supplies its basin, away from one it holds EV in the **item-local fluid store**. An Orb put into an altar (or later `orb_filling_link`) moves EV into the **player-linked Anima**. The two locations, capacities and actual transfer must be taught individually. Anima then powers Sigils and other bound instruments. [S: `BloodOrbStats.java`][W: `https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/wiki/Blood-Orbs-and-Anima.md`]
4. Use a Tier-0 Ara Vitae to make **Tabula Rasa** from Deepslate (1,000 EV), then build **Tabula Vitae** (`neovitae:tabula_vitae`). A bound **Novicius Orb (tier 0)** runs specific Tier-0 Tabula recipes, e.g. wool + flint → 4 String for **100 Anima EV**, or gravel + flint → 2 Flint for **50 Anima EV**. The Table itself is physically accessible earlier than its most important recipes. [S: generated Tabula recipes and `TabulaVitaeBlockEntity.java`] [W: `https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/wiki/Tabula-Vitae-Flasks-and-Anointments.md`, `https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/wiki/Alchemy-Arrays.md`]
5. **Upgrade the altar to Tier I first:** build an 8-Blank-Rune ring one layer down; transmute a Redstone Block (tag `c:storage_blocks/redstone`) for **5,000 EV** into a **Discipulus Orb** (`neovitae:blood_orb_apprentice`). Socket/bind it in Tabula Vitae. Only now can you use the exact tier-1 recipes for **Arcane Scribe Tool** (500 Anima EV) and **16 Iron Throwing Daggers** (500 Anima EV). The Scribe lays a two-offering Alchemy Array: actual `baseinput` then `addedinput`; effects are independent of any item carrying them. [W: `https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/wiki/Spiritus-Aspects-and-Crystals.md`, `https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/wiki/Hellfire-Forge-and-Sentient-Equipment.md`]
6. **Begin Spiritus** with a Dagger-marked hostile kill → Raw Spiritus, then forge the **Petty Spiritus Gem** (64-capacity, requires at least 1 Spiritus), which is a catalyst for transforming an iron sword into a Sentient Sword. Build stronger rings/Orbs through Tier V; ritual activation requires *both* a valid pattern and a crystal of sufficient level. Per-tier exact spatial previews remain in Scriptura Vitae; test recognized tier plus a tier-specific actual operation. [S: generated Forge, Altar, Ritual data] [W][R]

## 2. Native progression: all six altar tiers, separate branches

These are **formal native altar tiers**, not a comparative ranking. Roman numerals in the player guide correspond to machine tiers 0–5; source release notes mentioning "Tier 6" refer to counting tier 0 as the first of six and need visual-UI verification. Each tier requires a recognized multiblock and unlocks different recipe/perk routes. Do **not** assume every sigil/ritual on a guide page is unlocked by the same tier without inspecting its exact recipe. [S][W][R]

## 3. Resource-flow ledger — energy is not FE

A resource has an **identity, storage scope and typed path**. Neo Vitae contains player-linked, item-stored, and block-stored resources **at the same time**. No generic FE conversion is inferred from a network being able to move FE.

| Resource / kind | Actual storage, containers and scope | Producer → transfer → consumer | Capacity, important boundaries and visible proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Essentia Vitae (EV) — fluid/basin** | **Block:** `neovitae:ara_vitae` basin; internal transfer/input/output buffers; `neovitae:blood_tank` (fluid only). **Blood Battery is unrelated FE storage**; see separate row and storage audit | Source sacrifice/self-cut, orb deposit or Well → basin and intermediary buffer → altar transmutation or Vitae Link | Bare basin 10,000 EV; separate hidden reserve ~10% capacity; overflow holds up to 20× basin; guide describes input buffer 1,000 mB and **20 mB/20 ticks** default transfer; do not treat all as one display meter |
| **EV — portable fluid** | **Item:** six `blood_orb_*` items, stored via Orb fluid handler | Right-click sacrifice away from altar / credited offhand kills → item internal storage → deposit to altar, then Anima through altar/Link | Six item caps **4,000–14,000**; offhand kill base `int(maxHP×10)` before Bonus Sacrifice; excess above remaining capacity discarded; test actual tooltip and source-to-jar parity |
| **Anima — player-linked pool** | **Player / bound personal network**, with Orb defining maximum; it is *not* the item-fluid buffer | Orb in charged altar or Orb Vitae Link → bound player's Anima → sigils, ritual activation/upkeep, Sanguine Ward, Tabula costs, blood attributes | Six personal caps **5,000–10,000,000**. Separate attribute event transactions: Blood Siphon adds network points and Shield spends them; record death/logout/team persistence and GUI separately |
| **Spiritus — discrete items & item charge** | **Item:** Raw Spiritus essence, 5 Spiritus Gem tiers, Spiritus Crystal Shards, Spiritus-infused gear | Dagger-marked mob kills / Sentient Sword and Lost Souls → loot/Raw Spiritus → gem/Forge → Sentient effects, crafting or chunk deposit | Gem capacities: Petty 64, Lesser 256, Common 1,024, Greater 4,096, Grand 16,384. Infused equipment retains its own charge; gem Aspect matters |
| **Spiritus Aura — ambient world pool** | **World/chunk:** five aspect pools per chunk, not a player mana bar | Spiritus Crucible (`neovitae:vas_maleficum`) injects via items/gems/crystals; powered Crucible extracts back to gem; Conduit (`spira_infernalis`) spreads across chunks | Default **100 of each Aspect per chunk**; Aura Gauge (`neovitae:spiritus_gauge`) shows numbers while sneaking; required aura may gate Athanor recipes |
| **Spiritus Accumulator — block buffer** | **Block:** `neovitae:spirit_accumulator` stores **1,000 of one chosen Aspect**, held/locked/persisted in dropped item when broken | Attuned Accumulator skims up to 25/tick from aura above the local floor 30 → Output Routing Node target/aspect channel | Requires tiered Athanor setup and Animus Mote; locks to one Aspect until emptied; does not directly accept hand-filled Spiritus; not interchangeable with fluid EV |
| **FE / item/fluid movement interfaces** | **Routing capacity**; and distinct `neovitae:blood_battery` block FE storage. The Battery is creative/debug-style, refilling its FE store from redstone without a documented EV conversion | Routing Conduit/Nodes may move compatible FE; Battery holds 10,000,000 FE and allows max 100,000 transfer per call | Never infer regular survival FE generation or magic→FE conversion from the battery. Audit its availability as an anti-bypass issue: [storage audit](../inventories/neovitae-block-storage.md) |

**Spiritus production and upper bounds:** A newly scribed Spiritus Crucible can consume loose Spiritus, charged Gems or harvested crystal shards to raise the local Aura; power it with redstone to withdraw Aura into a gem. To seed a `neovitae:crystallarium_maleficum`, place it with air above and at least 99 Aura in the chunk; it produces a Spiritus Crystal which can grow up to seven spires. Matching-aspect growth costs about 45 Spiritus per additional spire and shard burning gives 50 (a possible net +5 per successful matched growth), but this is a source-wiki loop to verify in TMM—not free energy without setup. Aspected catalysts require special seed materials/Animus Motes, while the Accumulator can supply Routing nodes after being attuned and locked. [W: `https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/wiki/Spiritus-Aspects-and-Crystals.md`]

**Shield vs siphon vs Orb event, three different channels:** the Orb offhand kill handler directly fills an **item fluid**; the Blood Siphon attribute (damage dealt) credits **Anima network points**, and the Blood Shield attribute (damage taken) spends them to reduce damage. The earlier comprehension research reports source defaults of siphon cap 95%, mob/player multipliers 50/250 and Shield cap 95%, 100 network EV per prevented damage. The visual Blood Stream client option does not gate transactions. Never write a quest saying "kill a mob with an Orb to fill Anima" unless the subsequent transfer is explicitly taught. [S: `BloodSiphonHandler.java`; R]

**Source-level safe boundaries:** no verified generic resource bridge converts Anima to Botania mana, Ars Source or FE. The [JAWR integration](../../mod-comprehension/interactions/neovitae--witchery-remake--vampire-blood.md) offers conditional vampire/blood access; that is a version-paired compatibility question, not an unrestricted conversion. Keep blood/EV in the typed channels even when both share a lore name.

## 8. Player-facing quest handoff, acceptance and status

The complete [quest map](neovitae-quest-map.md) holds a proposed main spine, optional branches, explicit player instructions, FTB detection honesty, XP/fixed thematic rewards, and independent A/B/C verification. It crosswalks the present TMM chapter: `config/ftbquests/quests/chapters/neo_vitae.snbt` is currently **one optional checkmark quest** granting 2 XP + 1 paper (at time of inspection), not a functional walkthrough and not proof any altar operation is understood. This pilot proposes but **does not implement** its replacement.

### Research gaps and quest readiness

- **Not quest-source-ready:** the existing profile says Scriptura Vitae has **229 entries** (Alchemy Arrays 71; Altar 23; Dungeons 15; Rituals 45; Spiritus 24; Tabula Vitae 41; Utility 10). This pilot covers major families and selected concrete effects, **not all 71 arrays, 45 rituals, hundreds of individual recipe IDs, every Sentient modifier, or the exhaustive per-tier ingredients**. The registry index is an identity inventory, not a verified recipe catalog.
- **Exact binary parity pending:** 1.1.53 source and wiki are aligned by version, but no direct download/byte comparison with the mod's pinned SHA-512 was performed here. Source guides sometimes disagree even within the same commit (the Orb quick-reference names are a concrete example). Trust pinned data/registries first, then JEI/client verification.
- **TMM-specific configs:** current effective altar, rune, ward, farm, mob/portal/dimension, EMC and dust overrides are not comprehensively reconciled. Do not assert max throughput, cross-mod fuel, enemy whitelist or fluid handler operation from vanilla wiki alone.
- **Energy:** item, player and block EV storage have separate source fields; verify each fill/extract UI and saved persistence. Spiritus Aura is chunk-based, gems and infused gear are item storage, and Accumulator is block storage. Exact network conversion/FE endpoint audits remain open.
- **Gameplay acceptance:** **not run**. Server co-load in a prior B8 build is not a client/world PASS. Quest detectors for tier validation, ritual activation, custom Spiritus charges and dimension travel are not confirmed.
- **Axis gaps requiring owner research choices:** no proven native persistent summon, player resurrection, universal ore generation or economic trading. Retain these as `unknown`, not `absent`. TMM might disable or alter rituals: compare current source before quests.
- **Tier boundary acceptance:** builds for all 0–5 tiers need in-world structure validation including deliberately removed runes, source changes and multiplayer sync. Main/optional branch order and guidebook geometry require a disposable client run.

**Pilot verdict:** The 29-axis model is sufficient as a *navigation taxonomy* only because **object/operation inventories, typed energy stores, per-tier transitions, independent effect records and a truthful quest handoff are mandatory**. A one-row "has energy" or "has gear" summary would lose the central mechanics. The next research slice should be one exact-pin recipe/ritual exhaustive extraction (starting with Ara/Tabula tier gates) followed by in-world quest evidence; no silent claim of completion.

## 8A. Recipe and ritual deepening (2026-11-20 follow-up)

The first pilot used guide prose to stage some operations too early. **Generated 1.1.53 recipes supersede those assumptions**, and the quest map has been corrected:

- [All 31 Ara Vitae transmutation recipes](../inventories/neovitae-altar-recipes.md) list **actual input item or tag, minimum altar tier, total EV, per-tick craft speed, depletion loss and output**. This includes 6 Orb tiers, 5 Tabula grades, activation crystals, elemental inscribers, early utility and the four key gate material lines. The `#neovitae:vitae_stone` source item tag is currently Deepslate.
- [Core machine and array recipes](../inventories/neovitae-core-station-recipes.md) establish actual **Tabula Orb tier** and full Anima charge for first craft/Forge/Array examples: **tier-0 Tabula string/flint**, **tier-1 Scribe and Daggers**, **tier-3 Binding Reagent**, all **five Spiritus Gem** recipes, early dungeon keys, Crystal Clusters, and exact source Array base/catalyst ordering.
- [Ritual, sigil and Orb stats](../inventories/neovitae-resource-and-ritual-stats.md) inventories **46 explicit regular ritual stat overrides + 2 source-class dungeon ritual defaults** (from 48 registered regular rituals; 3 additional imperfect rituals have separate data), source-default startup/upkeep costs, crystal levels, 14 sigil overrides, and the Orb `fillRate` field. **Simple Dungeon: Weak Crystal, 80,000 EV. Standard/Endless Dungeon: Weak Crystal, 150,000 EV.**
- **Critical progression dependency:** Awakened Crystal (`activation_crystal_awakened`) is an Ara Vitae **tier-IV** recipe (40,000 EV). Therefore, tenebrae-type rituals with required `crystal_level:1` are **not automatically unlocked at Tier III** despite a Tenebrae Diviner being craftable there. Ordinary **Sentient Armor Binding Reagent** is Tabula `upgradeLevel:3`; armor operation should be taught at Tier III, while the first normal Sentient Evolution ritual belongs after Tier IV. This is first-known recipe access; dungeon loot or TMM overrides still require verification.
- **Concrete observer hooks:** [`RitualEvent.Activated`](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/2744123bb6e4f72c602178b23384be41956eef23/src/main/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/common/event/RitualEvent.java) carries the player and ritual after successful activation; `AnimaEvent.PostAdd/PostSyphon` carries owner UUID and actual debit; `NeoVitaeCraftedEvent` carries exact machine input/output but **no player in event payload**. These are implementation hooks to investigate, **not tested FTB tasks**. A good quest system must resolve player attribution rather than grant everyone progression from a single automated altar.

**Known source conflicts and edge cases:** the generated Array of Loyal Friends JSON lists 2,500 EV while upstream wiki says 5,000; use runtime/source-class implementation before a user-facing amount. A custom Orb-tier ingredient is used by Blank Rune and Ritual Stone recipes and may consume or return the Orb depending on crafting remainder semantics—**test, do not assume**. Tier-V Crystal Cluster has **2,048 minimum Spiritus available** but **1,024 consumed**; those are two distinct Forge fields. Basic first-operation successes remain tentative until actual pinned JAR/preset recipes are checked.

**Additional capability distinctions now source-proven:**

1. **Blood Tank is a 16-tier fluid system** (Tier 1 16 buckets through Tier 16 524,288 buckets), separate from Ara Vitae 0–5. Crafting two **same-tier** tanks with extra glass and Bloodstone yields one tier higher; if contents have mismatched fluids, the source preserves primary input and can discard secondary fluid. See [typed block storage](../inventories/neovitae-block-storage.md). Tank may be available early using bucket EV and Tier-I Spiritus.
2. **Blood Battery is a creative/debug FE battery**, not an EV tank. `BloodBatteryBlockEntity` stores 10 million FE, transfer limit 100k and self-refills on redstone ticks **without any EV or Anima debit**. It has creative tooltip and no identified normal survival recipe. Its reachability through loot/EMC/shop/datapacks is a **serious QA warning**; do not document it as a magic→FE converter or propose a normal survival quest until verified.
3. **Athanor can make a Nether Star** via `resonator/nether_star_from_diamond.json`: Diamond + Hellforged Ingot + **4,000 mB EV** and **50 Raw Spiritus**. This is typed *general material generation*, not world ore generation. **Owner lock 2026-11-20:** explicitly profitable EMC production; **preserve Nether Star's native EMC** rather than letting a cheap recipe collapse the Star's ProjectE valuation. Source survey: Star 139,264; calculated input 14,802; indicative +124,462 before maintenance. The Economy mapper already skips tool/fluid-dependent Athanor recipes, but **runtime map invariance is not yet tested**. [Full signed-off design and acceptance checklist](../inventories/neovitae-athanor-recipes.md#owner-locked-emc-exception--nether-star-transmutation-2026-11-20).
4. **Necromancy Sigil's actual craft is an Athanor Resonator recipe**, not an Alchemy Array recipe: Tabula Robur + Rotten Flesh + Bone, **1,000 mB EV** and **20 Raw Spiritus**. Thus the temporary pet ability has a real machine/resource prerequisite, not merely a sigil unlock.
5. **Spirit Accumulator's exact Athanor recipe**: Animus Mote + Common Spiritus Gem + Tabula Spiritus + Raw Crystal Shard + Blood Tank, requiring **2,000 mB EV** and **45 Raw Spiritus** with Resonator. It is a typed block Aura storage/transport node, not an Anima tank.
6. Three major **ritual observer types** are source available: player-attributed `RitualEvent.Activated`; owner-attributed `AnimaEvent.PostAdd/PostSyphon`; input/output `NeoVitaeCraftedEvent` without player. Native hook presence informs **possible future truthful FTB quest detection** but does not establish it in the current pack.

**What still stops `quest-source-ready`:** exact survival gameplay walkthrough and 3-D ritual constructions, real FTB task/advancement availability and correct per-player credit, native/pinned JAR equivalence, TMM datapack/EMC/gating compatibility, and positive/boundary gameplay acceptance. The recipe survey is *complete for six generated directories*, not for all other item forms, non-recipe world effects, dungeon loot, or all recipe JSON elsewhere.
