## Pass 2 — exact spell effects and operating boundaries (2026-11-25)
Same SHA1-verified JAR, reopened javap -p -c -constants output from magic.CastContext, MagicTargets, TimeFreeze, FlightPhysics, ActiveEffects, 26 concrete ability classes plus SustainedAbility, entity.UnicornEntity, menu.UnicornMenu$GemSlot, client.ClientKeys, world.TempBlocks, item.MagicalFerryItem and bundled tags. No loaded gameplay evidence asserted.
- CastContext.power = min(1.6, 1 + .12*max(0,bond - unicorn.unlockLevel(ability))). Actual wild assigned unlock, not canonical base. Energy discount uses Ability.energyCost separately. Damage multiplier native config default 1.
- Light Blast: 28-block ray, 10 HP times power times configured damage multiplier; doubles against inverted heal/harm entities; blindness60t, glowing120t, knockback .6*power.
- Light Flash: ordinary eligible enemies 4 HP*power, inverted heal/harm 9 HP*power + 4s ignition; blindness and Slowness amplifier2 for round(100*power) ticks; glowing400t. Friendly player branch grants night vision900t. Earlier undead-only shorthand is incorrect.
- Time Freeze: aim48, projectile selects direct eligible target or nearest eligible living within 2.5-block inflated hit box, duration round(100*power)t, persistent entity tag unicornacademy_frozen_until. Players, unicorns/riders, NeoForge boss-tagged and multipart entities excluded. No global time manipulation.
- Thunder Shockwave: base radius6, damage7 HP*power/config multiplier, Slowness IV and Weakness II 60t. Fireball aim48, Lightning Strike aim36; precise AoE and falloff remain to inventory.
- Rainbow Vision: round(24*power) radius, round(240*power) ticks, scan from -radius below to +radius/2 above, Euclidean radius cutoff, first 400 matches in scan order then sort; not guaranteed nearest 400. Sends block positions and at most 12 trails to rider, applies glowing to eligible nonfriendly living entities. Block tag unicornacademy:rainbow_vision_reveals explicitly includes #c:ores (optional tag), diamond/emerald and deepslate variants, ancient debris, chest/trapped chest/barrel/spawner/trial spawner/vault, suspicious sand/gravel, end portal frame, amethyst cluster/budding amethyst. No loaded-chunk guard in scan itself; performance/claimed treasure disclosure are TMM interaction considerations, not assumed defects. 
- Plant Growth: aimed28 blocks; radius2.5 + .35*clamp(bond-5,0,5) =>2.5–4.25. For each eligible plant, calls validBonemealTarget then performBonemeal up to six times, grass block once. Permission checks ServerLevel.mayInteract and player.mayUseItemAt at selection; not proof of FTB Chunks handling, nor all mod crops supporting interface. No automatic harvesting demonstrated.
- Water Creation: aimed16 blocks; base temporary-water duration600t*power. fillHeld scans both hands and consumes one empty bucket or glass bottle, outputs water bucket or water potion, replaces held item if depleted, else inventory insertion or drop. This creates real water items, unlike temporary terrain water. No arbitrary fluid capability. Temporary water spread cleanup is separately scheduled and includes loaded-position check; not assume all spill cleanup survives unload. 
- SustainedAbility: base ticks times power rounded; cast with effect active stops effect. Guiding Light1200t; Heated Hooves300t; Fiery Speed200t; Flying Lightning400t; Bubble1200t. Recast cancellation cost and per-tick behaviors need further source tracing.

- TempBlocks uses dimension SavedData unicornacademy_temp_blocks, stores original and replacement blockstates and expiry; direct setBlock, no common permission check. Per-spell protection must be traced; do not assume all terrain mutations respect FTB. No proof of block entity NBT restoration; do not treat temporary blocks as collectible resources.
## Pass 2 — fairy gemstone correction and controls
GemSlot only accepts unicornacademy:fairy_gemstone, maximum one; canWearGem checks actual player UUID bond level>=config wingsMinBond (default8) or creative. hasWings checks current DATA_GEM stack, not permanent unlock. setGem copies stack, extends wings on first equip, removing while flying applies Slow Falling200t to unicorn. dropEquipment calls superclass then spawns gem copy and clears gem. canFly checks wingsOut when gem present; otherwise falls back to SWEM canFly. toggleWings while in flight retracts wings and grants same Slow Falling.
