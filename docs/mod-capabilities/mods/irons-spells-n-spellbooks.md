## 2. Exact first-to-late game spell production and use

**Start (book + loot scroll):** Obtain a **Copper Spell Book** `irons_spellbooks:copper_spell_book` (native base **five spell slots**) or another actual Spellbook, and a usable **spell Scroll** `irons_spellbooks:scroll` from exploration/trades/crafting. Equip book in native Curios Spellbook slot; initialize/inspect its spell container. **A blank book or paper isn't a known spell.** Open **Inscription Table** `irons_spellbooks:inscription_table`. Input the book and a Scroll with a contained SpellData, select a valid slot, then *inscribe*. The server menu checks a real Spellbook+Scroll, adds spell data to book and **consumes the Scroll** only after the addition succeeds; it posts `InscribeSpellEvent` (can be canceled by integrations). Inspect the changed book SpellContainer. The table can **extract removable** book spells as Scrolls; locked unique-book spells are different. [Native Inscription menu](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/v1.21.1-3.16.3/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/gui/inscription_table/InscriptionTableMenu.java).

**Reliable chosen-scroll crafting:** Build **Scroll Forge** `irons_spellbooks:scroll_forge`. Supply **one `common/uncommon/rare/epic/legendary_ink`, one vanilla Paper, one item in `irons_spellbooks` School Focus tag**, and select an eligible school-compatible `AbstractSpell` in GUI. Native `ScrollForgeMenu.setupResultSlot` additionally checks **`spell.allowCrafting()`** and correct school from `SchoolRegistry.getSchoolsFromFocus`. It creates one `irons_spellbooks:scroll` containing the **minimum spell level for the ink's rarity**. Taking the output **consumes one Ink, one Paper and one Focus** (not just a reusable focus token). Higher ink doesn't universally mean “spell level +1”; the spell's rarity-level mapping and max level still govern result. **Exactly two native spells are Forge-disabled, but both deliberately have explicit special loot:** **`raise_hell`** from the keyed Citadel Vault and **`fang_swirl`** from ominous Dead King kills. Their `allowLooting() == false` stops generic random spell draws, **not** the explicit named `spell_filter.spells` entries—see [second-pass loot + boss audit](../inventories/irons-spells-special-acquisition-and-boss-closure.md). They are **player-obtainable boss-route Scrolls**, not general Forge recipes or generic mob-only abilities. [Native Scroll Forge menu](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/v1.21.1-3.16.3/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/gui/scroll_forge/ScrollForgeMenu.java).

**Scroll upgrade, equipment, rings:** Build **Arcane Anvil** `irons_spellbooks:arcane_anvil`. Source `ArcaneAnvilMenu.createResult` supports **Scroll + Ink whose rarity matches the scroll's next level** to advance one spell level when `scrollMerging` is enabled (default **true**); **weapon + Scroll** imbuement (not all weapons—`Utils.canImbue`); **eligible gear + Upgrade Orb** applying a stat-specific modifier with source config default **three upgrades**; **book + lesser spell-slot upgrade** (bounded by upgrade item's max slots); **Affinity Ring + Scroll** to attune a bonus-level spell; **Shriving Stone** to remove eligible upgrades, with return drops; and special unique gear upgrades. The anvil's preview does not spend inputs until result taken. [Native Arcane Anvil menu](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/v1.21.1-3.16.3/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/gui/arcane_anvil/ArcaneAnvilMenu.java) · [Server config](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/v1.21.1-3.16.3/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/config/ServerConfigs.java).

**Spellbook capacity versus effectiveness:** [Exact `ItemRegistry`](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/v1.21.1-3.16.3/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/registries/ItemRegistry.java) declares vanilla book capacities: **Copper 5 → Iron 6 → Gold 8 → Diamond 10 → Netherite 12**, while Legendary and Dragonskin also declare **12**. This is a **slot-capacity progression**, not five spell rarity levels, and specialty books need different material/trade/loot sources. Base attributes matter: Gold gives **+50 Max Mana and 15% Cast Time Reduction**; Diamond **+100 Max Mana**; Netherite **+200 Max Mana and 20% Cooldown Reduction**; Blaze/Dragonskin/Druidic school books specialize. Unique books `evoker_spell_book`, `necronomicon_spell_book`, `cursed_doll_spell_book` have preset/locked or exceptional behavior, not generic upgradeable empty books. A `wimpy_spell_book` in registry has **zero** base capacity: never recommend it as an ordinary starter upgrade.

**Casting cycle:** Spell selection uses actual SpellData in book/weapon/Scroll, native input binding and an **entity-owned MagicData Mana pool**, cooldown state, cast time and interrupts. `getManaCost(level)` source formula = **integer of (baseManaCost + per-levelMana × (level−1)) × per-spell configured Mana Multiplier**, before other event overrides. `SpellOnCastEvent` exposes requested cost to integrations; the native player cast handler deducts the final event cost from personal Mana. Source `MAX_MANA` attribute base **100**, `MANA_REGEN` base **1** (attribute multiplier), with specialized spell power, resist, cast speed, summon damage and movement while casting as distinct stats. Casts are not simply item durability use or vanilla XP. [Native AbstractSpell](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/v1.21.1-3.16.3/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/api/spells/AbstractSpell.java) · [MagicData](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/v1.21.1-3.16.3/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/api/magic/MagicData.java) · [Attributes](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/v1.21.1-3.16.3/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/api/registry/AttributeRegistry.java).

**Rarity economy:** Spell levels and rarity brackets are coupled **per spell**. `SpellRarity` source includes **COMMON, UNCOMMON, RARE, EPIC, LEGENDARY** (not creative/commented mythical tiers); default five-bracket population distribution in ServerConfigs `[.30,.25,.20,.15,.10]`. **Rarity is not player XP, book capacity, boss difficulty or universal level count**. TMM's five planned Occultism-to-Iron's ink rituals [draft PR #336](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/pull/336) are **not integrated** at inspected mainline: Common/Uncommon Foliot, Rare Djinni, Epic Afrit, Legendary Marid would be an **alternate material route**, not a change to each spell's actual `SpellRarity` or Focus requirement.

## 5. TMM overlays and source-versus-implementation boundary

1. **Ars 'n Spells:** TMM pins `ars_n_spells-3.3.5.jar`. It is a legitimate compatibility layer between Ars Nouveau and Iron's, and TMM's common player Magic/MXP model may rely on bridge events. **Do not infer one physical mana tank, a common item mana pool, or a successfully paid double-award merely from addon presence.** Native Iron's `MagicData` mana plus `SpellOnCastEvent` is source evidence; TMM-owned MXP adapter must count actual player-spent resource once (not menu previews, turrets, or non-player casts) under separately authorized Progression design. MXP belongs to shared progression engine, not a particular Path.
2. **Occultism ink:** [open draft PR #336](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/pull/336) proposes alternative ritual manufacture for `common_ink`, `uncommon_ink`, `rare_ink`, `epic_ink`, `legendary_ink` via Foliot/Djinni/Afrit/Marid. **Draft, not in installed runtime.** A lore/compat optional quest can note it *once integrated*; first spell chapter should always work using native loot/crafting. Do not silently mark Occultism quest complete from a staged ritual.
3. **Blood:** Iron's Blood Magic spells and `blood_cauldron` are distinct from **JAWR vampirism/witchery** and **EvilCraft's canonical liquid blood**. Native `blood_vial` and noop `blood` fluid do not prove TMM canonical conversion, nutrition healing, or bottle return. A compatibility recipe is pack-owned, not a native mod feature. Do not assume an item tagged “Blood” fulfills all transformation needs.
4. **Quest gates and rewards:** The Atlas is factual teaching research, not authorization to give boss keys `dead_king_phylactery`, `cinderous_soulcaller`, `pyrium_ingot`, `arcane_ingot`, highest-tier Ink, or unique Spellbooks/Spellbreaker. Safe rewards are normal XP and low-value materials, with rare parts only after actual discovery/fight milestone. Boss kill is an event, **not possession of a replicable trophy**.
5. **Protection/safety:** Source ServerConfigs `spellGriefing=false` by default, with separate blocks/portals restrictions. Permission checks and special spell targeting vary; `touch_dig` does call NeoForge protection hook, but this **does not prove every area spell respects every claim/cancel boundary**. Only TMM-authored adapters or observed concrete bugs require targeted pack-side QA. No general native-game test suite created as a documentation prerequisite.
