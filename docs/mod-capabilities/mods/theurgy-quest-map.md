# Theurgy — compact quest-source map (Atlas, 2026-11-21)

**This is a research artifact, not 20 live FTB Quests.** The integrated TMM Theurgy chapter has **one optional manual checkmark**, rewards **2 vanilla XP + 1 Paper**. Its GUID remains owned by the quest chapter implementation. No quest tree, scripts, mod pins, machine recipes or runtime rewards were changed.

**Source status:** pinned artifact 1.79.0, source investigation [release/v1.21.1-1.79.0](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/tree/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0) with metadata mismatch detailed in [29-axis dossier](theurgy.md). Use the exact installed Hermetica and JEI when writing final recipes. All quantities below are **source-default examples**, not asserted current loaded config. [Original preliminary Mod Comprehension](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/blob/research/mod-comprehension-local-jars-20261116/docs/mod-comprehension/mods/theurgy.md).

**Quest direction:** Teach one complete working material recreation first; everything after is optional: Niter/Exaltation/Reformation, independent rod scanning, separate Mercury-powered heating, item/fluid logistics, and high-cost rare material economy. Do **not** make player memorize every derivative item/recipe/rod; exact 29-axis Atlas dossier inventories the capability families. Distinguish item possession from actual operation, fluid count from item count, and Mercury Flux from FE.

## Research-to-implementation handoff

- **Code-ready quest prose:** TH-00…TH-08 is the complete first-item alchemy experience. TH-09…TH-19 are modular optional lessons. Exact machine IDs, salt names, rod tiers, Niter ranks, concrete input/outputs and energy carrier are in [dossier](theurgy.md); do not replace them with bare “craft device” labels.
- **Evidence-specific finalization:** Source ref's gradle.properties claims 1.47.1 while TMM JAR pin is 1.79.0; compare actual available 1.79.0 data or version-matched registry before selecting exact items, default fluid yields or FTB machine-action detectors. No native-only client QA required solely because the source wasn't directly tested.
- **Pack-owned material policy:** Uranium no special restriction; Occultism Draconium and Replica miner first tier Afrit, with other approved magic/fishing alternatives. Theurgy's Nether Star/Dragon Egg precursor chain is a *risk to audit*, not a proven effective TMM leak. Protect genuine first-kill progression tokens through their owners; don't invent new Theurgy nerfs or force Nether Star as an early reward.
- **QA queue:** Only after TMM-authored recipe/tag changes or new per-player machinery/scan trigger, test affected positive/boundary behaviors. Do not test unchanged native machine processing as a condition of documentation closure.

**Source count:** nine main + eleven optional research lessons, with a checked acyclic prerequisite graph and unique TH IDs. No runtime code or quest edits.
