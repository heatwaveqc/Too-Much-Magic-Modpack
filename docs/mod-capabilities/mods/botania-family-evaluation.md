## New focused source findings (supersede older unresolved death and gear wording)

- **Potato Chips:** [implementation](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/java/velrondevs/extrabotany/common/item/equipment/bauble/PotatoChipsItem.java), [event registration](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/java/velrondevs/extrabotany/event/ExtraBotanyEvents.java), [actual recipe](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/resources/data/extrabotany/recipe/runic_altar/potato_chips.json). Lowest-priority LivingDeathEvent listener skips already-canceled events, requires equipped Curio, no cooldown, 3,000 Mana. Cancels event, restores 5 HP, clears all effects, gives Regeneration II 900 ticks, Absorption II 100 ticks and Fire Resistance I 800 ticks. 600-tick cooldown ordinarily, 12,000 when damage source entity is in NeoForge bosses tag. Does not consume chips, one equipped instance. Runic Altar recipe uses gilded potato, Tiny Potato Mask, Tiny Potato, Rune of Mana, three Totems of Undying, campfire, blast furnace, 50,000 Mana and Livingrock reagent. Death avoidance is real, not resurrection after death. No new forced-death quest or runtime test.
- **Nature Orb:** [source](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/java/velrondevs/extrabotany/common/item/equipment/bauble/NatureOrbItem.java). Max 500k Aura; strict greater-than thresholds 100k/200k/300k/400k for tier1–4; up to 3 Mana/tick distributed to eligible stores; tier3 heals 1 HP every 60 ticks; tier4 clears harmful effects every 40 ticks, debits 200 Aura per removed effect. Ego summon consumes 200k on success, separate from Mana. Note strict > thresholds.
- **Achilles Shield:** [source](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/java/velrondevs/extrabotany/common/item/relic/AchillesShieldItem.java). Hits in unreleased mode add 2 absorption up to 20. Sneak-use main hand at >=10 absorption switches released mode; loses 1 absorption per 10 ticks, movement modifier +40% base, strikes use thunder-sword path; paid block reflects blocked damage for 500 Mana. Released mode cannot block normally. 
- **Death Ring:** [source](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/java/velrondevs/extrabotany/common/item/equipment/bauble/DeathRingItem.java). Offensive only: every 30 ticks searches radius-6 inflated AABB, visibility/passability checks, pays 80 Mana per target, applies Wither II/Unluck II for 60 ticks and 0.5 direct life loss. Not a death-protection ring.
- **Silent Eternity:** [source](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Extra-Botany-Revived/blob/b58857d3658479f6e8684d3b45f6d3138a294548/src/main/java/velrondevs/extrabotany/common/item/equipment/bauble/SilentEternityItem.java).
