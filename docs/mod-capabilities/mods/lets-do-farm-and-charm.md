## Progression and resources
No formal player rank or FE/mana network. Analyst stages: find wild seeds/abandoned fields or acquire crops from farmer; grow crops (barley, oat, corn, tomato, lettuce, onion, strawberry; wild emmer, nettle, ribwort and vanilla crops); build hand stations; grow/cook, improve soil and husbandry, operate carts; complete native meal collection and expand scarecrows. Separate optional industrial mincer and cleaver tiers. Teach via EMI recipe categories, native advancement tab, station feedback. No forced all-food collection in TMM core path; native completionist remains optional.

- Cutting board: 36 cutting and 9 assembly recipes; ordinary knives use recipe chops, iron/diamond/netherite cleaver defaults 5/3/1. Chop/strip and assemble feed, compost, rope and nests. Example one raw chicken -> 3 chicken_parts + 1 feather; poppy -> 2 red dye. Campfire/grill unique meats; mincing/smoking alternatives have distinct outputs. Cleaver head-drop toggle defaults true. Audit exact combat drops versus TMM Ender Dragon loot policy.
- Mincer: 53 recipe JSON. Insert and crank; grain tag -> four flour, iron ore -> 3 raw iron (genuine tripling), stone -> 2 cobble, diamond horse armor -> one diamond; also ore/horse-armor recovery, stripped logs, seeds, meat. Source 1.1.28 fixed duplicate ore recipes and copper-ingot reverse route. Repeated processing, no free fresh ore. Need full output/cost table, claim interactions and exact rotation counts.
- Crafting bowl: four recipes; manual whisking, one milk-tag input -> 4 butter; flour-tag + yeast + water bottle-tag -> 5 dough; salad and oatmeal. Pot: 10 recipes; heat from stove, soups/tea, sugar+wheat+water bottle tag + bowl container -> 12 yeast; no learned-recipe gate for yeast. Roaster nine recipes; stove nine, internal cooking 240 ticks, 3 ingredient slots, fuel, output; grill four campfire slots and flip cooldown 40 ticks. Grandmother's cake recipe dough+strawberry+sugar -> 2 cakes, requiresLearning false. Native normal XP recipe fields and configurable cookingExperienceMultiplier default1; not MXP. Full station times and consumed container returns need follow-up. Bakery, Brewery and Candlelight depend on shared resources; do not assume cross-mod station capabilities newly added in 1.1.30.
- Silo wood/copper: 19 drying recipes including wild crops -> bone meal, rotten flesh -> fertilizer, podzol -> fertilized soil. Multiblock controller 3x3x9 max=81 cells; two inventory regions each MAX_CAPACITY, processing timer 200t; exact per-cell stack/volume, accepted side transfer and connectivity behavior pending. Do not teach 1.1.30 hopper fix on pinned 1.1.29.
