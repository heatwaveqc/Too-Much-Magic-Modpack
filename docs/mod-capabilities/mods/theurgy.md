## Player progression in real operations

**Entry:** Craft **Hermetica** (a component-selected Modonomicon book) from one Book and two tagged Sand; follow its Getting Started/Spagyrics pages. Make **Pyromantic Brazier** and use ordinary furnace fuel to supply heat beneath **Mercury Distiller** and **Calcination Oven**. Distill accepted inputs into Mercury Shards; calcine Stone, Ore, plants and creature parts to four kinds of Salt. Make **Sal Ammoniac** from water using Accumulator + Tank. Heat a **Liquefaction Cauldron** and dissolve an eligible source item with Sal Ammoniac to obtain its identity-bearing Alchemical Sulfur. Then build the **two-block Incubator** with separate adjacent Mercury, Salt and Sulfur vessels, heat the machine, insert one matched ingredient of each category and retrieve a real item. Exact source example: `Mercury Shard + Plant Salt + Oak-Log Sulfur → Oak Log` (default incubation time 100 ticks). Output is a machine inventory slot, not a world-summoned mob. [Hermetica provider](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/ShapelessRecipeProvider.java) · [Incubation recipes](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/IncubationRecipeProvider.java).

**Advanced transmutation:** Ferment Sulfur with category-eligible organic inputs and Water in **Fermentation Vat** to make **Niter**, then use **Digestion Vat** (including Purified Gold + Sal Ammoniac) to change Niter rank. Build a **Reformation Array** of **Sulfuric Flux Emitter + Source, Target and Result Pedestals**; supply source items and **Mercury Flux** to change eligible Sulfurs directly or convert Niter into specified Sulfurs. **The target pedestal must already contain the requested output's Sulfur or Niter identity as a real nonconsumed template**, checked by `ReformationRecipe.matches`, while Reformation spends the source items and Flux. Incubate resulting Sulfur to produce actual material. This is the key late-game capability, not a generalized first-item creation table. Material types include Gems, Metals, Other Minerals, Earthen Matter, Logs, Crops, Animals and Mobs; available tier ranges differ. **Niter ranks** are Abundant → Common → Rare → Precious, a separate concept from **Divination Rod tool tiers** Stone → Iron → Diamond → Netherite. [Fermentation source](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/FermentationRecipeProvider.java) · [Digestion source](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/DigestionRecipeProvider.java) · [Reformation source](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/ReformationRecipeProvider.java).

**Parallel tools/automation:** Nine rod variants for ore/target scanning; Mercurial Wand configures native wire/frequency/item/fluid connectors and filters. This is a *native magical* material-routing system, independent of AE2/RS or Draconic FE networks. There is no demonstrated player mana, pet class or universal remote item bank.

## Matter/loot gates and precise limitations

**Verified source-level first-sample lock, no native first-kill bypass from the inspected chains:** Nether Star Sulfur is first liquefied from an **actual Nether Star + 100 mB Sal Ammoniac**; Dragon Egg Sulfur analogously from an **actual Dragon Egg + 100 mB**. Their explicit Reformation recipes require **the same existing Sulfur on a real target pedestal**, plus **2 Precious Mob Niter + 200 Mercury Flux** for a new Star Sulfur, or **4 Precious Mob Niter + 200 Mercury Flux** for a new Egg Sulfur. `ReformationRecipe.matches` checks the target-pedestal inventory and `ReformationArrayCraftingBehaviour` never consumes that retained template. Incubation then consumes a generated Sulfur + Creature Salt + Mercury Shard to produce a real Star or Egg. **The template must already exist; the standard source route obtains it by spending the original boss drop.** The actual [`SulfurMappings.noAutomaticRecipesFor`](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/SulfurMappings.java) *excludes* these special Sulfurs from general automatic conversions; the empty field initializer in the provider is overridden before generation. Even the 64 Precious Gem Niter→1 Precious Mob Niter Reformation requires an existing Mob Niter sample. [Exact seven-file recipe, matcher, data and tag audit](../inventories/theurgy-release-provenance-and-precious-reformation.md). Thus the checked **1.79.0 release-tag source** implements expensive **post-acquisition duplication**, consistent with the owner's approved boss-drop replication, **not a new first-kill bypass**. Real loaded 1.79.0 JAR/data parity was not byte-verified. Do not use *possession* of an output Star/Egg as proof of a player first-kill achievement; preserve event-specific QA separately.

**TMM policy retained:** Uranium is unrestricted (old reactor restrictions retired). **Draconium and Deepslate Replica Ore are Afrit/Marid Occultism-miner targets** with previously approved independent magic/fishing alternatives; this source survey does not establish a direct Theurgy recipe for them. The source has Iesnium/Demonite candidates; do not invent upstream Replication `replica` Sulfur. Normal sulfur/ore tag recipes must be checked against *effective TMM* tags. Occultism's optional guide entries in Theurgy are not proof of Source/EV/MXP currency interoperability.

**Resource and storage boundaries:** Theurgy outputs can be renewable by consuming common resources/Flux/solvent, but this is still a recipe-constrained economy. No automatic assumption of pre-acquisition item samples, unconditional unification, free water/crystal reuse, lossless machine insertion or chunk loading. Any native item/fluid logistics tool may be useful to other mods only if actual capability and loaded integrations expose it.
