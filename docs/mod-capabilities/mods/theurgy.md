## Player progression in real operations

**Entry:** Craft **Hermetica** (a component-selected Modonomicon book) from one Book and two tagged Sand; follow its Getting Started/Spagyrics pages. Make **Pyromantic Brazier** and use ordinary furnace fuel to supply heat beneath **Mercury Distiller** and **Calcination Oven**. Distill accepted inputs into Mercury Shards; calcine Stone, Ore, plants and creature parts to four kinds of Salt. Make **Sal Ammoniac** from water using Accumulator + Tank. Heat a **Liquefaction Cauldron** and dissolve an eligible source item with Sal Ammoniac to obtain its identity-bearing Alchemical Sulfur. Then build the **two-block Incubator** with separate adjacent Mercury, Salt and Sulfur vessels, heat the machine, insert one matched ingredient of each category and retrieve a real item. Exact source example: `Mercury Shard + Plant Salt + Oak-Log Sulfur → Oak Log` (default incubation time 100 ticks). Output is a machine inventory slot, not a world-summoned mob. [Hermetica provider](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/ShapelessRecipeProvider.java) · [Incubation recipes](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/IncubationRecipeProvider.java).

**Advanced transmutation:** Ferment Sulfur with category-eligible organic inputs and Water in **Fermentation Vat** to make **Niter**, then use **Digestion Vat** (including Purified Gold + Sal Ammoniac) to change Niter rank. Build a **Reformation Array** of **Sulfuric Flux Emitter + Source, Target and Result Pedestals**; supply source items and **Mercury Flux** to change eligible Sulfurs directly or convert Niter into specified Sulfurs. Incubate the resulting Sulfur to produce actual material. This is the key late-game capability, not a generalized free item transmutation table. Material types include Gems, Metals, Other Minerals, Earthen Matter, Logs, Crops, Animals and Mobs; available tier ranges differ. **Niter ranks** are Abundant → Common → Rare → Precious, a separate concept from **Divination Rod tool tiers** Stone → Iron → Diamond → Netherite. [Fermentation source](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/FermentationRecipeProvider.java) · [Digestion source](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/DigestionRecipeProvider.java) · [Reformation source](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/ReformationRecipeProvider.java).

**Parallel tools/automation:** Nine rod variants for ore/target scanning; Mercurial Wand configures native wire/frequency/item/fluid connectors and filters. This is a *native magical* material-routing system, independent of AE2/RS or Draconic FE networks. There is no demonstrated player mana, pet class or universal remote item bank.

**Mercury Flux is intentionally not Forge Energy.** The upstream [`MercuryFluxStorage` interface](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/content/capability/MercuryFluxStorage.java) specifically calls itself a **copy of IEnergyStorage kept separate to prevent FE conversion**; there is an independent `theurgy:mercury_flux_handler` capability in [`CapabilityRegistry`](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/registry/CapabilityRegistry.java). The method names `receiveEnergy`/`extractEnergy` are *not* evidence that Draconic Evolution FE, Psi-generated FE or other generic FE ports can power Theurgy. Heat is its own typed work permission.

## Matter/loot gates and precise limitations

**Potential first-acquisition issue, not a proven bypass:** The [source Sulfur registry](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/registry/SulfurRegistry.java) includes Nether Star, Dragon Egg, Elytra, Netherite, Iesnium, Demonite and Uranium derivative candidates; [IncubationRecipeProvider](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/IncubationRecipeProvider.java) declares corresponding reconstruction recipes. [Digestion](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/DigestionRecipeProvider.java) permits upgrading **16 Rare Mob Niter to 1 Precious Mob Niter**; [Reformation](https://github.com/klikli-dev/theurgy/blob/release/v1.21.1-1.79.0/src/main/java/com/klikli_dev/theurgy/datagen/recipe/ReformationRecipeProvider.java) contains Niter→Sulfur paths and initially sets `noAutomaticRecipesFor` empty. If all resulting recipe/tag conditions load, a boss-derived material might be reproducible through normal resource conversion. **Never assume a player killed the boss merely because a Nether Star/Dragon Egg item was obtained**; preserve TMM first-kill-only rewards and protected progression tokens as separately owned policies. Don't blacklist native high-tier Theurgy functionality without first confirming which exact loaded paths create a forbidden shortcut.

**TMM policy retained:** Uranium is unrestricted (old reactor restrictions retired). **Draconium and Deepslate Replica Ore are Afrit/Marid Occultism-miner targets** with previously approved independent magic/fishing alternatives; this source survey does not establish a direct Theurgy recipe for them. The source has Iesnium/Demonite candidates; do not invent upstream Replication `replica` Sulfur. Normal sulfur/ore tag recipes must be checked against *effective TMM* tags. Occultism's optional guide entries in Theurgy are not proof of Source/EV/MXP currency interoperability.

**Resource and storage boundaries:** Theurgy outputs can be renewable by consuming common resources/Flux/solvent, but this is still a recipe-constrained economy. No automatic assumption of pre-acquisition item samples, unconditional unification, free water/crystal reuse, lossless machine insertion or chunk loading. Any native item/fluid logistics tool may be useful to other mods only if actual capability and loaded integrations expose it.
