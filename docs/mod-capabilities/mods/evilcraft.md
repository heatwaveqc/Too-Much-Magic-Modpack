## 1. Orientation: actual player operations and native unlocks

**What EvilCraft offers:** (1) First Age dark-gem discovery and a dangerous **Vengeance Spirit Portal** to generate the **Origins of Darkness** book; (2) Vengeance Ring sight/attraction, Focus capture and Eternal Closure, protection against spirits; (3) Second Age **player-kill Blood Extractor** and world Blood Stains, multi-container **Dark Tank** storage, **Blood Infuser** with upgrade **Promises** tiers0→3; (4) paid renewable material production, repair and utility devices; (5) portable **Blood-powered broom flight**, Blood Pearl teleport, effects, weapons; (6) **Spirit Furnace** mob-drop processing and **Spirit Reanimator** conditional spawn-egg production; (7) source-defined weather and biome alteration, rare temple machinery and a Sanguinary variant; (8) **Rejuvenated Flesh** consuming fluid Blood instead of itself, plus mobs, worldgen, odd trading and undead materials. These are distinct resources and independent branches, not a generic magic mana school.

**First usable loop (actual game action, not item-only quest):** Mine `evilcraft:dark_ore` in Overworld with **iron pickaxe or better** and obtain `dark_gem`. Obtain/open `evilcraft:origins_of_darkness` **either** from eligible chest loot (native 20% loot-modifier condition for Simple Dungeon/Jungle Temple and several others) **or** by crafting `darkened_apple` (Apple+Dark Gem), applying Paling to an animal until its death creates temporary `evilcraft:spirit_portal`, then throwing `minecraft:book` into the portal. The animal/portal method is a **tutorial option, not the only legitimate source**. Craft `blood_extractor` (native Second Age criterion), gather actual Blood from player-credited kills or fall-death stains, throw a Dark Gem into a world pool of ≥five Blood source blocks to produce the **first** Dark Power Gem. Preserve/source a *separate* placed **source-level Blood** block, let it naturally harden in suitable dry/no-nearby-water conditions, smelt Hardened Blood → **nine Hardened Blood Shards** and use **eight** shards + the Power Gem for the `blood_infusion_core`. Craft the `blood_infuser` and run the repeat Dark Gem+**250mB Blood /200 ticks** infusion. Source Blood Stain is not Hardened Blood; holding a machine is not proof of its operation.

**Native advancement facts:** `first_age` needs Dark Gem inventory; `second_age` needs a Blood Extractor craft; `evil_source` needs guidebook possession, not reading. **Narrative reading is not necessarily an enforced unlock**. Spirit capture has a custom `box_of_eternal_closure_capture` criterion; distorted/necromancy milestones have separate custom triggers. First Age spirit/temple activities can be explored in parallel to the blood main line. No TMM Path or MXP wallet corresponds to these two native Ages.

**Native formal machine tiers:** Infuser tier0 (no Promise) → `promise_tier_1` → `promise_tier_2` → `promise_tier_3`. Machine fluid capacity at each = **10,000→40,000→160,000→640,000mB**, via [`getTankTierMultiplier() = 4^tier`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/core/blockentity/BlockEntityWorking.java). This is a **machine upgrade**, not player Age, Blood fluid potency or tier of Vengeance Spirit. Separate Speed and Efficiency upgrades modify machine operation. [Precise tier0→3 recipe/reagent route](../inventories/evilcraft-blood-tier-resource-ledger.md#native-promise-machine-capacity-ladder-not-player-class-levels).

**Optional branches:** Early Vengeance Ring, Gemstone Torch, Burning Gemstone, Focus and capture; an *already prefilled* Box of Eternal Closure can be crafted via Weakness Potions/Ender Chest tag or found in certain chest loot, without proving a Focus capture. **Spectral Glasses are late** (require Garmonbozia), and **make spirits visible rather than hiding the wearer**. Spirit Furnace needs tier2 Dark Blood Bricks and valid enclosure. A Broom can be crafted from material parts **or found precharged in eligible chest loot**. Entangled Chalice is tier3 Corrupted-Tear-gated world-shared fluid logistics; passive Effortless Ring needs Speed+Efficiency Promises. Temple operation uses source weather and world conditions. None of these are first-boss kill events.

## 4. TMM-specific integration boundaries and truthful quest-writing evidence

**Canonical Blood:** TMM selected **EvilCraft `evilcraft:blood`** as common fluid for planned/ongoing EvilCraft, Hexerei, Iron's and JAWR blood bottle/tank and food adaptations. EvilCraft has **its own** configurable name-based blood fluid conversions; don't claim they automatically convert *every* bottle, potion, siphon or JAWR vampire health. Check pack-owned integration PRs before writing a live compatibility task. The two particular planned Vampire's Delight Blood Sausage/Blood Pie recipes and 2×/5× Blood-return policy are **TMM custom content**, not native EvilCraft capabilities.

**Food tags:** The owner chose a common **Magical food** eligibility concept, with Gilded and Nether separate tags. `flesh_rejuvenated` is an important blood-costed candidate; do not turn magical access into free food, suppress blood drain, attach imaginary vanilla FoodProperties, or assume original hunger gate disappears. Dragon Survival/JAWR/werewolf/lich paths can differ in whether they dispatch the custom item-use method.

**Automated mob drops and boss policy:** Native Wither→Nether Star via Spirit Furnace and native Wither Skeleton Skull Infuser are **post-source / material syntheses**, not per-player first-boss credit. Existing TMM acceptance of high-magic boss-drop replication applies conceptually but **valid capture and boss eligibility** must be checked against shared spawner/capture policy rather than silently imposing a new owner gate. Native mob-drop FakePlayer can interact with external loot tables differently from real kills. Source Spirit Reanimator may *erase* box data even on invalid egg: warn before expensive/boss experiments.

**MXP and shared Progression:** Blood fluid/machine operation is an **independent magic resource economy**.
