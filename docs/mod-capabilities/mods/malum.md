## 1. Practical player entry and magic progression

The source-grounded accessible route begins by acquiring **Raw Soulstone**, smelting it into **two Refined Soulstone per one raw** (200 ticks), and opening **Encyclopedia Arcana** via **Book + Refined Soulstone**. This avoids relying on a rare mob-kill RNG drop. As a parallel guide acquisition path, native `enableCodexDrop` defaults true: the first eligible exposed Undead kill by a player can award a copy of the Encyclopedia. The Crude Scythe recipe consumes **3 iron ingots +2 wooden rods +1 Refined Soulstone** and exposes mob souls for a **200-tick (10s)** window; kill a non-soulless target while exposed for actual typed Spirit drops. [Source steps and exact shaped pattern](../inventories/malum-1.8.2-core-processes-and-progression.md), [SoulHarvestHandler](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/core/handlers/SoulHarvestHandler.java).

Craft the **Spirit Altar** with 4 tagged Runewood planks, 1 refined Soulstone, 2 tagged Gold ingots. Put one gunpowder and one Arcane Spirit in the altar to make **Hex Ash** (first concrete Spirit Infusion). The Altar has one central item plus Spirit inventory, captures optional extra inputs from pedestals within **±4 x/z and ±3 y**, and crafts after ~300 ticks before accelerators and extra-ingredient consumption delays, dropping output in the world. It doesn't store or spend FE/Mana/Neo EV.

The magic paths then branch: **Totem rites** by exact engraved 3/4/5-pole patterns and normal vs corrupted base; **Soul-Stained Steel / Scythe / rune equipment** and **24 runeworking recipes**; **Spirit Crucible** and wear-unknown/partial specialized Impetus material Nodes; **Soul Brazier** and permanent Geases; **Repair Pylon** using actual tool material and Spirits; **Umbral/Weeping Well** with conditional world structure, dangerous rejection and Void Favor. A player can pursue these in parallel once actual recipe requirements are available. Endgame ProjectE and other TMM magic alternatives need not be locked to Malum.

## 4. Important source confirmed hooks and exclusions

- **26 native Totem Rites** are pattern-defined, not JSON-shaped placed multiblocks. Three-pole minor, four-pole major, five-pole special exact ordered Spirit engravings; Soulwood base selects corrupted alternate. Base rite default cooldown 100 ticks; entity effects select up to two nearby LivingEntity targets, and no base per-pulse Spirit-item drain. [Full effect and pattern map](../inventories/malum-1.8.2-spirit-types-and-rites.md).
- **36 generated Soul Binding recipes + 1 nonrecipe Creed** are actual persistent effects. [Soul Brazier](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/common/block/curiosities/soul_brazier/SoulBrazierBlockEntity.java): use one Ether to start, takes 600 ticks, each target offers 25% max HP Karmic on joining; recipe ingredients/Spirits consumed once; failure deals an additional 50% max HP, then effect is attached on eligible target. The GeasItem right-click toggle is an *alternative special item path*, **not the standard result** from a Soul Binding recipe.
- **Weeping Well progression:** native structure has actual feature/structure JSONs. Primordial Soup rejection stacks to 60, first rejection forces the actor out (possibly damaging 4 HP and giving rare Umbral Spirit), Void Conduit applies `void_favor` recipes after 80 ticks, and qualifying well-streak unlocks `creed_of_the_blight_eater`. Preserve as significant optional late quest and warn about damage and world alteration.
- **No-Create rule:** 85 handwritten recipe files include Create crushing/washing/milling and compatibility for absent optional mods. They cannot be counted as pack-enabled just because upstream JSON is present. Native source has **metal-node production** using an Impetus; this is not Create automation and should be retained pending pack economy check.
- **Creative-class #596:** No creative-only infinite item was identified in the native initial Rites/Geas/major-machine registry scan. Scarce Spirit-fed production/cobblestone creation/ward recovery isn't automatically creative-class. Flag any future truly zero-input infinite material power item to owner policy rather than banning all output generation.
- **Portable-pouch policy:** both base pouches allow broad eligible item insertion with a bundle-like weight model. This is NOT a narrow sigil-only inventory like Neo Vitae Sigil Holding, nor Occultism-style dedicated magic storage. The pack's no-generic-backpack rule suggests preventing Ravenous/Soulwoven acquisition or explicitly approving an exception. No runtime restriction has been authored here.
- **Dust/EMC integration:** source lists Botania, Hexerei, Occultism repair support as handwritten compat recipes, and uranium Impetus/Node as conditional. The TMM dust canonicalization and source-to-EMC economic relationship are integration checks, not evidence of duplication exploits absent tested loops.
