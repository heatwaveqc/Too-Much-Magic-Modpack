**Before starting:** obtain Hint of Rebirth from spruce/jungle oven with Jar, Torn Pages from Graveyard Dirt archaeology or Fortune Teller, unlock current objective via Torn Page when necessary. Gather food/blood sources and shelter; native bloodline is separate from TMM class skills/MXP. Actual rank cap 10; pool maxima 900,1200,1500,1500,1800,2100,2400,2700,3000,3600. **Initiation**: craft Arthana (gold ingot top, gold nuggets flanking emerald, bone bottom), Wine Glass (glass block over bottle), Infernal Chalk (Crimson Fungus→Blaze Powder→Ritual Chalk heated cauldron), skull, eight candles. At night, kill chicken within two blocks of circle with Arthana main hand/Wine Glass or vanilla glass bottle offhand. Use special chicken blood on circle; summons Elle, skull consumed, vessel emptied. Follow/lead Elle to Nether through portal, wait300 ticks (conversion resets outside Nether); she becomes Lilith. Defeat to <=5% HP (lethal clamps1, heals while undefeated); use Wine Glass on defeated unused Lilith for marked vampire blood (one claim, then despawn). Drink at level0→rank1. No coffin gate in WineGlassItem inspected branch. TMM approved Lilith route; separate ring initiation for Sexy Demon #656 is not part of native vampire procedure or guaranteed integrated.
