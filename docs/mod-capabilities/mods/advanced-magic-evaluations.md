Protection5 DM/RM/Gem armor (native full-set baseline reductions 80/90/90%, subject to damage rules), shielding, hostile repulsion, fire protection; not universal immunity. Gathering5 charged area tools, excavation, Divining Rod value scans, material synthesis from learned/template EMC-positive resources, not arbitrary unknown or protected material creation. Construction5 Philosopher's Stone world transmutation, Mercurial Eye template building/replacement, charged excavation, fluids and material supply. Life5 harvest band and pedestal plant growth/harvest, animal product and plant synthesis for eligible templates, time acceleration; no universal mob-spawn system assumed. Death0 no distinct native lethal rescue, grave or item/XP recovery at death demonstrated; powerful mitigation/repair not death recovery. ## Apotheosis ecosystem — 38/55, 7 independent, Substantial

Combat5 affix/gem/enchantment systems, critical rolls, attack/penetration/current-health and elemental effects, high-tier invaders and gear. Protection5 strong armor/toughness/typed resistance, protection formula, dodge and gems. Survival4 lifesteal, overheal, vitality and healing_received, renewable XP-supported Life Mending, potion charms; no broadly independent food/resource system. Gathering5 Radial/Supermassive, mining speed, tool/loot modifiers, Fortune/Boon, chainsaw, spawner and Capturing outputs; no unrestricted ore source. Industry2 genuine advanced reforging, socketing, enchanting, infusion and alchemy, but specialized gear processing, not broad material refinement or independent industrial resource network. Construction2 area excavation, selective block transformation, no general placement/architecture system. Transportation4 large movement/swim/step/gravity bonuses, flight or elytra affixes and potion effects as native; not universal travel network. Logistics2 Gem Case, Enchantment Library and Alexandria, collection affixes and specialized storage only, no general routed multi-item storage system; consistent with Reliquary logistics reassessment. Life4 growth and breeding enchantment effects, Growth Serum, animal spawner variants, advanced renewable mob farming; less comprehensive plant cultivation and animal processing than independent farm traditions. Death0 no native resurrection or distinct death recovery evidenced. Advancement5 five World Tiers, five rarities, six gem purities, reforging/augmenting, Eterna progression to100, four spawner rune tiers.

The actual family includes 99 native affixes, 23 gems and 81 bonus entries, independent world-tier equip/kill gates, 26 Enchanting stat definitions, 19 infusion recipes, 37 Attributes brewing mixes. Native tier Eterna caps 30/45/60/75/100. Enchantment Library points preserve known enchantments, not a general warehouse; native capacity16, Alexandria31. Spawners module is substantial infrastructure: Frontier delay150–600 ticks/count7/nearby cap12/range24; Ascent100–400/10/18/32; Summit50–200/13/24/40; Pinnacle20–20/16/32/48. Actual spawn output remains bounded by eligible mob, nearby cap, available space and conditions; ignore players does not chunk-load. Apotheosis disables all 32 standalone modifier IDs in its packaged data and uses rune modifiers; do not teach the old sugar/nether-star system. Existing blacklist and replication policy remain binding.

Classification: the family is exceptionally strong at improving equipment and adventuring, with real independent gathering and mob agriculture. It does not provide independent general industry, construction, storage/logistics or death recovery. No bespoke Major criterion needed. High-quality gear may enhance another mod's activity, but that other mod's independent capabilities are not assigned to Apotheosis. Preserve exact numerical research for Monk #721/#722; this editorial classification does not constrain max bonuses, grant armor-less access, or decide effective pack balance.
