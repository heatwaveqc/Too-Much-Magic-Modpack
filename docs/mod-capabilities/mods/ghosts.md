- Enchantment processing: GhostEntity.tick starts animation cd82, processes at cd10 when shouldUnenchant or fallback when cd0 and flag true. removeEnchants copies stack, awards ordinary XP based on sum of minimum enchanting costs of all non-curse enchantments in ENCHANTMENTS and STORED_ENCHANTMENTS. Chooses one uniformly random ordinary enchantment, removes it (selection does not exclude curse tag); removes entire stored enchantment component; increments REPAIR_COST via AnvilMenu.calculateIncreasedRepairCost, drops output stack, clears held item. No book conversion in method. Trigger is ItemStack.isEnchanted, so do not promise enchanted book eligibility or guaranteed curse removal. Potential XP over-award for partial removal because XP sum includes retained enchantments; review for pack balance and anvil/grindstone policy. No patch proposed as settled requirement.
