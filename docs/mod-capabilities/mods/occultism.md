## 1. Player experience and real first operation

Occultism combines **ritual craft/summoning**, specialized **owned spirit labor**, four familiar **spirit tiers**, **tier-sensitive ore processing**, **renewable dimensional mining**, item-only **dimensional storage and remote access**, **Otherworld perception/harvesting**, optional **familiars and special equipment**, plus **mob possession, capture, virtual Battlefield loot, wormholes, trader and utility routes**. A Book of Binding supplies a **tier and named spirit**, but does not automatically grant every job; ritual recipe/factory chooses the actual summoned worker, item, wild encounter or craft output. Literal ingredients, chalk patterns, world-event gates and worker control matter as much as the Book.

**First real loop — do this, not merely acquire an icon:** collect Demon's Dream seed by breaking grass; craft/open Dictionary (`#c:seeds/datura` + `#c:books`), grow renewable Datura; place Datura as an item entity and ignite it with flint and steel to form Spirit Fire; convert a registered input; make and purify White Chalk, craft Sacrificial and Golden Bowls, prepare Taboo Book/Purified Ink/Awakened Feather, make and bind a Foliot Book with the **reusable** Dictionary; build native `pentacles/summon_foliot` and put **Iron, Gold, Copper, Silver ingots** in bowls within 8; activate `ritual/summon_foliot_crusher` with the bound Foliot Book. Feed eligible raw iron to the actual summoned worker and collect **2 iron dust** under the default recipe/tier formula. Binding-item possession is not evidence of a successful summoning; a summon advancement is not evidence of processing.

**Main teaching spine:** Dictionary/veil → Spirit Fire → chalk/bowls → Foliot book/circle/first worker. **Formal tiers:** Foliot → Djinni → Afrit → Marid, each with parallel **summon, possession and craft pentacles** and separately selected jobs. **Material branches:** Otherworld perception/harvest; lime/purple/orange/red/black/blue and specialist chalk; named unbound Afrit/Marid essence encounters. **Major optional continuations:** mining, storage, familiars, farmer/transporter/machine manager, trading, devices, weather, wormholes, ritual satchels and late eldritch contact. Not every ritual, chalk color, boss or familiar is needed to unlock every other branch.

## 5. Concrete capability families and usable ceilings

### `veil-tools` — Third Eye, Otherworld materials and resource detection

Datura fruit (native 70% chance Third Eye, guaranteed Hunger) reveals **client tier ONE**; guaranteed Dream Essence has additional potential negative effects while purified Otherworld Essence provides safer Third Eye. Third Eye does not grant a permanent power stat. Otherworld Goggles and staff can reveal tier TWO, but the server harvest check counts **active Third Eye tier ONE**, **staff** or appropriate **Otherworld/Infused tool tier TWO**; goggles alone are not automatically tier-two mining authority. Natural Iesnium Ore is visually disguised as Netherrack until uncovered; with required tier and correct mining/tool, it can yield Raw Iesnium or ore under Silk Touch, whereas covered default loot can be ordinary Netherrack. Use [exact visibility/loot source comparison](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/blob/research/mod-comprehension-local-jars-20261116/docs/mod-comprehension/mods/occultism-pinned-storage-harvest.md#otherworld-visibility-harvest-tier-and-divination).

Link Divination Rod by sneak-use on a *specific analogue* (Andesite→natural Otherstone, Diorite→Otherrock, Netherrack→Iesnium, Oak/Leaves→natural Otherworld wood, End Stone→Otherglass, flowers→Otherflower) then hold use **40 ticks** to scan with cooldown40. Default distance is **horizontal bands**, not exact 3D range and not ore acquisition. Any-ore option/staff can broaden it conditionally. Quest action: find a genuine natural target, uncover and mine, not just craft a rod. **Bottom:** Datura+first Spirit Fire. **Middle:** Goggles/infused pickaxe and rod. **Upper:** Iesnium+special materials/late chalk; no magic flight or permanent ore x-ray inferred.

### `ritual-contract` — native ritual geometry and actual output

At each recipe, verify **chosen named pentacle**, color/foundation tags and props, one Golden Bowl activation, offerings in bowls range8, any **sacrifice death** or **item-use event**, and duration/interrupt behavior. The 158 ritual payloads use eleven factories: **47 craft, 9 named craft, 5 miner item craft, 4 repair, 3 upgrade, 29 summon, 17 tamed summon, 26 job-spirit summon, 15 wild summon, 1 chance-of-chicken summon, 2 familiar resurrection**. This sum is 158. Different factories deliver an **item, named item, miner, owned or wild entity, or resurrection**. Viewer spawn eggs or essence icons are not necessarily output; unbound Afrit/Marid rituals summon actual hostile mobs to defeat.

Ritual duration JSON is a **counter**, not always ticks: standard tier1 increments at native ~20-tick intervals (depending on multiplier), tier2 at ~5-tick intervals, advanced classes may finish differently. Ingredients can be consumed gradually; interrupt and reload rules matter. Overhead Sacrificial Bowls may catch item outputs; Automation Flame is not a loose item fallback. **Bottom:** first Foliot job. **Middle:** selected Djinni/Afrit craft/possession and specialized job. **Upper:** Marid/Contact Eldritch/Trinity/late chalk, without mandating all 158 rites.

### `processing-jobs` — tiered ore multiplication, crystallization and fuel-free smelting

**Crusher:** accepts exactly configured crushing recipes and inclusive min/max worker tiers, not arbitrary raw material. For `c:raw_materials/iron`→2×`c:dusts/iron` generated recipe, source-config default Foliot/Djinni/Afrit/Marid yields **2/3/4/6 dust**, with counter thresholds **400/200/100/60 ticks** at operationCount1. Formula is floor(base output count × min(held stack, operations count) × worker output multiplier), unless recipe ignores multiplier. Some recipes use anti-loop `ignore_crushing_multiplier`; cannot claim all ore/ingot conversions multiply. [All 240 crushing recipes](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/blob/research/mod-comprehension-local-jars-20261116/docs/mod-comprehension/mods/occultism-pinned-processing.md#crushing240-records).

**Crystallizer:** 54 bundled source recipes, four tiered workers; defaults same output multipliers1/1.5/2/3 and time factors1/0.5/0.3/0.1, but actual material recipe determines possible products. **Smelter:** tiered workers resolve **smelting→blasting→smoking→campfire cooking** in that order, fuel-free in inspected worker method, no claimed furnace recipe XP. All three have real held-item pickup and job-output semantics. **Critical**: a Dimensional Extractor under processor can route output to another inventory but can **discard rejected remainders** after consuming source input. **Bottom:** Foliot iron. **Middle:** Djinni/Afrit and side-specific processing. **Upper:** Marid, eligible high tier recipes, reliable storage arrangement; don't assert losslessness or game-tested throughput.

### `worker-control` — labor, machine management, agriculture and handling

Native `OccultismSpiritJobs` includes **farmer, lumberjack, cleaner, transport_items, manage_machine, four-tier crush/smelt/crystallize, four material traders+gambler, clear/rain/thunder weather and day/night**. Every worker needs its *specific* summon-with-job route and usually a job-specific Calling Book; generic summoning isn't a free role swap. Sneak-use on **owned** spirit with correct Calling Book links; nonsneak book opens mode GUI; select Set Base/Deposit/Extract/Managed Machine and sneak-use a valid faced inventory block to apply. Re-interacting the same linked worker can **capture it into the book** rather than merely update binding. Native Calling Book release clears saved entity regardless of spawn-success; do not promise safe pet transport without checking actual placement.

**Farmer:** finds mature `CropBlock` over `FarmBlock` within selected area, then harvests mature crops in a **5×5 target-centered square** and sets each to default crop state without spending new seed in this method; some neighboring blocks can lie beyond scan area. Not arbitrary modded plants/farmer's-delight stoves. **Lumberjack:** initial stump recognition uses log/soil/leaf; then breadth-first felling can follow **connected logs beyond the configured initial work-area**, and replant only where there is compatible dirt + a sapling item. Keep buildings isolated from grove; no 256-block cap inferred. **Cleaner:** filtered nearby ItemEntity pickup with capacity simulation and deposits; **Transporter:** physical extract→walk→deposit to sided inventory. Its extractor currently reads using **deposit-facing** rather than configured extract-facing on the actual extraction call; sided mismatches are an observed *source* defect, not automatically a confirmed client issue. **Machine Manager** queues machine orders against loaded controller/storage/proxy; queueing does not reserve inputs. **Weather/time** jobs perform bounded one-shot world operations and expire; a constructed visual lightning object is not a real spawned lightning strike. **Bottom:** configure one Foliot worker deposit. **Middle:** safe farm/grove and basic transport. **Top:** managed machine orders and advanced tier processors with actual output confirmation.

### `dimensional-mining` — generated ore, not ore doubling

Weighted selection uses **relative active recipe weights** and actual loaded condition/tag membership, not 142% success. The basic ores tag includes Foliot unspecialized; all four ordinary worker tiers share that tag. **Specific TMM gate warning, not a new gameplay vote:** `miner/ores/draconium_ore.json` weight142 requires `#c:ores/draconium`; version-aligned selected Draconic Evolution 3.1.4 source supplies this tag and Occultism's ore-miner tag includes Foliot. The Eldritch Draconium storage-block generator is commented out in this release. **No bundled miner recipe directly names Replica Ore, Chaos Shard, Nether Star, Dragon Egg, Gaia/Wilden trophy or Mine Key**; this negative statement is restricted to the 194 scanned JSON outputs, not all recursive tags or TMM loot. ### `storage-network` — item-only Dimensional Matrix, stabilizers and remote

### `death-recovery` — finite protections and actual-death separation
