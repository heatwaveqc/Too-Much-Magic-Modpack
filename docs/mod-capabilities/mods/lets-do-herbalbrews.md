## Seven custom effects
All radius10 inflated AABB (not true sphere); most calculate number of nearby players minus1, rounded max(0,1-0.1*n) amplifier, generally amp1 for n<=5 (float rounding boundary check), amp0 thereafter. Some exclude creative/alive, some count all players; not universally scaling with allies or actual party/team. Native drink amp0 often ignored by per-tick calculations.
- Balanced (green): applies absorption to drinker only every200t, duration200t, computed amplifier0/1; level I or II (2/4 absorption hearts nominal), not party buff.
- Bonding (yerba): each tick grants nearby alive noncreative players OTHER than drinker absorption II and regen II for50t if computed amp>0, no self buff; nearby count includes eligible player list, subtract1. More drinkers may mutually buff, no party filter.
- Deeprush (coffee): every tick affects other eligible players only, base amplifier by drinker Y>=50:0, >=30:1, >=0:2, >=-20:3, below:4; integer divide by other player count, if >0 apply Haste level amp+1, duration200t; does not refresh identical current amplifier until absent, replaces different Haste, no self effect. Therefore no effect on companions at Y>=50, and Haste V possible below -20 for one companion; not guaranteed personal mining bonus.
- Fortune (oolong): applies Luck attribute (not Fortune enchantment). With computed amp1, +2 luck via permanent modifier minecraft:effect.luck, removes modifier if crowd reduces amp to0. No explicit removal override in class, potential expiry persistence and collision with vanilla Luck/Grandma blessing; verify lifecycle rather than assert exploit.
- Feral (lavender): registers +3 attack damage and +4 maxHP base using vanilla IDs minecraft:effect.strength and minecraft:effect.health_boost; each tick reapplies modifiers at computed amp1 when positive, nominal +6 damage/+8 HP. No explicit reset when amp falls0; lifecycle and collision test vs vanilla Strength/Health Boost and pack attributes. Not guaranteed stacking.
- Tough (black): neutral-category effect; every100t if health<=8 HP and amp>0 applies Regen II and Resistance II for40t. Not continuous resistance.
- Lifeleech (hibiscus): every tick propagates itself to nearby eligible players (other than drinker) for200t amp1 if count permits; every160 entity ticks directly subtracts1 HP from every Monster within10, adds1 HP per enemy to drinker capped max. Uses setHealth, not standard hurt damage source; no owner/claim/hostility target distinction, may bypass defenses and kill attribution. Mutual propagation can potentially sustain effect indefinitely with multiple players; verify vanilla reapplication/expiry. This is a real multiplayer integration test target, not an approved nerf. 
Rooibos uses vanilla Regeneration I recipe effect.

## Potion blending and communal drinks
Cauldron five slots: 3 PotionItem input0–2, output3, herbal infusion catalyst4. No recipe-type limitation beyond class, accepts even potion without effects; reads all PotionContents including modded custom effects. Deduplicates by effect holder, strongest amplifier wins, EQUAL amplifier keeps first encountered (not longest); no summing duration, no potency boost, no reduction. Consumes one each of three potions and one infusion, produces one Flask with random model1–6, PotionContents retained. Infusion exact recipe three each dried black/green/oolong leaves (nine total). Config brewingDuration1200t=60s. Flask stack16, drink32t, exact return/application and PotionItem subclass edge cases next; not arbitrary effect injection from drinks (HerbalBrews drinks BlockItem).
