## Hexcasting — 36/55, 8 independent, Substantial Magic

- Combat 4: programmable target selection, explosion, lightning, potion attacks, motion and circle execution; no general summons or high-tier direct weapon system. Protection 3: programmable mobility/absorption/effects, but no comprehensive ward/shield or innate save. Survival 4: regeneration, night vision, useful effects, fluid/light spells and rechargeable devices; overcast can kill.
- Gathering 4: real Break Block with default diamond harvest tier, place and world actions, renewable amethyst after Flay Mind, Quenched Allay high-density Media. No generic ore multiplier/generator. Industrial 2: powerful programmable automation/circles, media production, edification, block transformation, no native broad manufacturing/refining ladder or cross-energy generation. Do not equate general-purpose programming with a physical production operation.
- Construction 5: player-authored geometry and list/loop operations, real block placement and conjure solids/light, break/water/lava, reusable program devices. Not arbitrary block creation or unlimited range; actual block placement consumes a held block item. Circle environment cannot supply all held-item operations.
- Transport 4: blink, Greater Teleport same-world with inventory-loss risk for self, paid range/time flight and Altiora. No cross-dimensional gateway; temporary flight only.
- Logistics 2: movement/teleport of eligible entities, item placement, Akashic program/data storage and read/write, no item warehouse or conventional pipes. Library key→iota is not a resource inventory.
- Life 4: bonemeal, edified saplings, programmable growth and redstone circles, renewable Media. No native dedicated breeding/spawning pipeline or pet system. Death 0: no death save; Enlightenment is near-death overcast with survival required, not a resurrection. Advancement 4: exploration of world-specific Great Spell signatures, Enlightenment and Great Work, rechargeable devices, libraries, circles and persistent player access; no innate stat tree.
- Existing exact port concerns retained: duplicate Toolsmith/Fletcher Flay Mind inputs, config circle length1024 lacks observed execution consumer, circle default edit permission true, possible unbounded/repeated operations and actual runtime/claim acceptance unresolved. Do not require broken Fletcher acquisition or deliberate death in quests.
