## Audit closure and tasks still separate

**Algorithm census:** 14 generating +27 functional +2 special = **43 distinct native named flower-algorithm families** (not counting cosmetic, Chibi or floating variants). Crosschecked against the `BlockEntityType<...>` registry: Orechid Ignem and port `Wither Mana Rose` included, as are standalone utility Pure Daisy/Manastar. Extra Botany and optional Asgard module flowers are independent and not included in 43.

**Native mod interaction caution:** this exact pinned source demonstrates that *name-based* mechanical guesses are dangerous: Dreadthorn deliberately attacks adult animals; Spectranthemum refuses Mana-holding item drops; Solegnolia doesn't spend its own Mana; Bergamute handles occlusion rather than electricity; Pure Daisy is recipe-driven; several redstone or Chibi costs differ. The Atlas is for real player instruction, not a quest for mere possession of every flower.

**Not yet Atlas closure for the entire Botania family:** per-flower recipe-first acquisition/ingredient grids, Mana storage equipment and named major relics/gauntlets, standalone devices/lenses, port module flags, and a separate Extra Botany 1.0.4 boss/energy/flower/crafting graph. Those require source investigation and/or effective pack registry as specified; native unchanged flower mechanics do not demand a full new client regression run just to be researched.
