## Spell package cost, cooldown and modifier semantics

[`SpellPackage.java`](https://github.com/daedalus4096/PrimalMagick/blob/5977468bda968b994e54066236ec5719295ac9c3/common/src/main/java/com/verdantartifice/primalmagick/common/spells/SpellPackage.java) computes **single typed-Source** base cost as `(base payload Mana + vehicle flat Mana + primary/secondary modifier flat Mana) × vehicle multiplier × primary modifier multiplier × secondary modifier multiplier`; it stores the result as **centimana**. The source cost is the **payload's Source**, not automatically a mix of five Sources. [`IManaContainer`](https://github.com/daedalus4096/PrimalMagick/blob/5977468bda968b994e54066236ec5719295ac9c3/common/src/main/java/com/verdantartifice/primalmagick/common/wands/IManaContainer.java) applies the holder's Wand cap, attunement, equipped gear and discounts to the cost **on consumption**, so this builder cost is not identical to every player's final deducted amount.

Standard **spell cooldown = max(0, 30 − 5×Quicken Haste) ticks**, clamped to 30. The actual `HASTE` property is 1–5. `Amplify` cost multiplier = **1+Power**; `Burst` multiplier = **1+radius²** with additive **Burst Power**; `Quicken` multiplier = **1+Haste**; `Fork` multiplier = **1+forks²+precision²**; `Mine` adds Duration to base Mana, with `4×Duration` minutes of persistent spell duration. Values in each SpellProperty must be checked for legal ranges and same-type duplicate mod handling; do not treat alternate modifier permutations as free.

## High-impact player-facing and TMM safety implications

- **Flight** is a timed `EffectsPM.FLYING` effect applied on a successful living-entity hit. Its base Mana cost = **20 × duration rank**, while duration = **3 seconds × duration rank** before additional modifier interactions. It is **not** a permanent creative flight item; verify temporary ability removal and interaction with TMM transformation/Monk passive flight restrictions before accepting as pack gameplay.
- **Healing** scales as **2 × Power** HP on living entities; on inverted-heal/harm targets, including vanilla undead, it instead damages for **1.5×heal amount**. Excess healing may grant **200-tick Absorption** based on each **four points** of overheal. Source uses **Sun Mana**. Cross-mod undead detection (lich/vampire and similar) is a **target-specific QA concern**, not an assumed interoperability PASS.
- **Break** builds a scheduled [`BlockBreaker`](https://github.com/daedalus4096/PrimalMagick/blob/5977468bda968b994e54066236ec5719295ac9c3/common/src/main/java/com/verdantartifice/primalmagick/common/spells/payloads/BreakSpellPayload.java) operation with Power, optional Silk Touch, `Treasure` (Fortune) enchantment, player and actual Wand tool. Its base Earth cost = **Power +5 if Silk Touch enabled**. **Do not claim that land-claim, protected-block or block-break event hooks are respected** until the `BlockBreaker` scheduled implementation and actual claimed-block QA are verified.
- **Teleport** has **Void Mana cost 10**, rejects a burst target and self-targeted entity but forwards other target coordinates to `EntityUtils.teleportEntity`. It is **not** documented unlimited interdimensional travel; target/permission/collision safety requires test.
- **Conjure Animal** costs **100 Blood Mana** and spawns one random terrestrial or water animal when targeting a valid block/entity location. The source selects candidate by whether spawn fluid is water and calls the server mob-spawn routine. **Not** guaranteed player-owned permanent pets.
- **Conjure Lava/Water/Stone/Light**, **Polymorph** and **Shear** can manipulate entities or blocks in ways that may affect claims, Worldgen and farming. Confirm actual `mobGriefing`/claims/event behavior and loop exploit resistance in owner-authorized client/integrated QA.
- **Creative policy:** a source-registered timed Flight effect is not itself a creative-tier infinite resource item. Conversely, an all-research or infinite-Mana *item* cannot become a normal survival drop, recipe, EMC output, shop item or FTB reward under TMM's locked policy.
