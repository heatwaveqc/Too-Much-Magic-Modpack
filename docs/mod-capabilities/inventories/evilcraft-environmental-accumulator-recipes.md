**Operating context:** Native **Dark Temple** generation exposes the standard Environmental Accumulator, which can change its biome/degrade and cause penalties when overused. The crafted **Sanguinary Environmental Accumulator** requires the documented ring of **eight Blood tanks** to operate without standard negative side effects and consumes Blood; it does not dispense an unlimited weather/biome bottle without the native recipe and world context. **Biome Extract Bottle** is a component-bearing item that can be filled at native Accumulator and thrown to alter biome state; the output isn't necessarily a new item ID.
