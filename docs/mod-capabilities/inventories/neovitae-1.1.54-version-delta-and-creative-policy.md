## Creative-class policy #596 — Neo-specific audit

The pinned source [`BloodBatteryBlockEntity`](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21/src/main/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/common/blockentity/BloodBatteryBlockEntity.java) stores **10,000,000 FE**, transfers max **100,000 FE per extraction/receipt**, and **self-fills whenever redstone-powered without requiring EV, Mana, Spiritus or consumable input**. The [Block tooltip](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21/src/main/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/common/block/BloodBatteryBlock.java) labels it **Creative Only**. Upstream 1.1.54 recursive generated recipe tree contains **no `blood_battery` craft recipe**; its ordinary block-break loot table self-drops only an already-existing placed block. Thus **no native generated recipe proven**, yet this is **exactly a creative-class unlimited power generator** under [owner policy #596](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/issues/596). Do not include it in any survival quest/shop, EMC learning, loot or reward. Integrator must cross-check effective loaded recipes, loot, KubeJS, admins, trades and ProjectE mapping before accepting. **Source audit itself changes no access path.** Normal 16-tier EV Blood Tank is unrelated and allowed.

Also distinguish **productive resource infrastructure** (Torment Nexus, Endless Quarry, Nether Star-resonator recipe, water generator) from creative-debug blocks: generation with actual mechanisms, local inputs and planned rates is *not* prohibited merely because it is useful. The existing source-backed owner's Nether Star EMC exception remains intentional.
