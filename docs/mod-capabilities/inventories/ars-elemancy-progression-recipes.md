The **Source cost is 3,000** in the seven source Imbuement recipes, separate from the four elemental Essence item catalyst stacks. The central reagent is consumed, pedestal Essences are reusable catalysts in the underlying Ars imbuement format. The six pairwise affinities are **choices**, not a mandatory six-step research ladder. For the four-element Elemancer, plan a separate advanced goal with appropriately scarce gear inputs instead of pretending it automatically unlocks from the Novice book.

**Tier conclusion:** Six dual families each contain **two** numbered equipment recipe variants, whereas four-element Elemancer includes **six** numbered armor/caster recipe variants and **three** focus recipe variants. These are **recipe alternatives/upgrades**, not guaranteed six separate gameplay level gates; inspect central reagent/gear dependencies and preserve item NBT/enchantments in exact upgrade semantics before asserting a linear progression for every armor type.

**Quest coverage:** The current 49-node Ars chapter has only a Cinder Essence and a Cindermancer Hat step. A single how-to can explain the six pairwise options and the separate optional Elemancer multi-element capstone with JEI references, without demanding 250 acquire-item quests.

**QA boundary:** only a TMM-authored armor modifier, gear retention change, recipe override, or quest detector needs focused pack QA. Do not regression-test native Elemental/Elemancy recipe mechanics merely because a survey found them.
