# Botania Revived 2.0.2 — pinned-source generating-flora operations (14 base flowers)

**Evidence:** immutable [Botania Revived 2.0.2 source commit](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Botania-Revived/tree/5f803227d484598da1f1093a91d5d7cf5cf9600d), matching TMM [Botania Revived Packwiz pin](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/blob/test/full-integration/mods/botania-revived.pw.toml), plus earlier independently [hash-verified JAR research](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/blob/research/mod-comprehension-local-jars-20261116/docs/mod-comprehension/mods/botania-revived.md). **Scope:** registered named generating species and their native operating algorithms, not floating/potted duplicate entity types, recipe/JEI integration, installed TMM effective configuration or client gameplay evidence. **No native standalone Minecraft QA prescribed for unchanged upstream behavior.**

Source provenance for the common lifecycle: [`GeneratingFlowerBlockEntity`](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Botania-Revived/blob/5f803227d484598da1f1093a91d5d7cf5cf9600d/src/main/java/velrondevs/botania/api/block_entity/GeneratingFlowerBlockEntity.java) buffers Mana in the flower, automatically seeks a compatible collector within **6 blocks**, and hands off as much as the collector accepts. This is **not** an unlimited direct pipe into every Pool; generators normally bind into Mana Spreaders. A saturated flower can interrupt input, waste already-burning ticks, or limit the next event depending on its own code.

## Three important source/pack distinctions

1. **Thermalily owner design is already approved and authored, but exact runtime coverage is not implied.** Native 45 Mana/t + random cooldown differs materially from TMM's 75 Mana/t, zero-cooldown intent. [PR #147](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/pull/147) specified two isolated Botania Revived 2.0.2 mixins; [#251](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/issues/251) reconciles them into first-party compatibility. Do not propose a third balance system. Handoff to current compatibility/integration owner for exact module head and retest.
2. **Food and mob throughput cannot be inferred from names.** Gourmaryllis food property and dropped-item destruction, Spectrolus wrong-color wool discard, Narslimmus natural-spawn discrimination, and Shulk Me Not's dual-mob predicate all matter to real farms and to truthful quest criteria. These are source-backed warnings, not permission to modify them.
3. **Thermalily, Wither Mana Rose, conversion bridges and Entropinnyum have different sinks/caps.** A configured source rate assumes a sufficiently drained flower/collector; output cannot be called continuous when full. Cross-resource/resource-balance tests belong to TMM-owned overrides and actual adapters, not a generic unchanged-native regression suite.
