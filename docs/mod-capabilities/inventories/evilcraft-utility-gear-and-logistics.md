## C. Spirit safety, ore extraction and permanence corrections

- **Sources of early protection:** [\`gem_stone_torch\`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/resources/data/evilcraft/recipe/crafting/gem_stone_torch.json) and [\`burning_gem_stone\`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/resources/data/evilcraft/recipe/smelting/burning_gem_stone.json) are accessible through Dark Gems/ordinary smelting, unlike Spectral Glasses. The Torch intercepts **eligible spirit spawning nearby**, does not erase spirits spawned outside its coverage or turn them friendly.
- **Source Hardened Blood availability:** [\`BlockFluidBlood.randomTick\`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/block/BlockFluidBlood.java) hardens **placed source-level Blood blocks** on random ticks (1-in-3 conditioned chance on each random tick) if not exposed to applicable rain and no Water within a four-block area; [smelt Hardened Blood](evilcraft-special-recipes-world-loot.md) into **nine Hardened Blood Shards**. A **Blood Stain is not the Hardened Blood block**. These shards are specifically needed (**eight**) around the first Dark Power Gem in \`blood_infusion_core\` ordinary crafting recipe. Have players generate Hardened Blood **without destroying all source pool Blood** needed for their first manual gem, and use a separate source block or staged pool.
- **Native Vengeance Spirit blacklist:** [\`EntityVengeanceSpiritConfig\`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/entity/monster/EntityVengeanceSpiritConfig.java) forbids certain entity types including EvilCraft Werewolf, Controlled Zombie, Vanilla Ender Dragon, and merchant. This is specifically the **random Vengeance spirit spawn** blacklist, not a general classification of all spirits or immunity to other TMM capture methods.
- **Rare gear acquisition:** [Dungeon chest modifier](evilcraft-special-recipes-world-loot.md) can produce an **already charged random Broom** or **prefilled Box of Eternal Closure** much earlier than the crafted part/Box recipe chain. Quests must accept actual operation with a legitimate native loot item, rather than gating flight until someone needlessly crafts an arbitrary Broom from parts.
- **No player Path attachment:** Native passive ring effects, blood-spending pendants, machine tiers and spirit toys are **ordinary items**; neither tied to TMM Newbie/Normie specialist Path state nor an alternate player MXP pool. The eventual shared MXP attribution is a pack integration decision, not a property of this source.
