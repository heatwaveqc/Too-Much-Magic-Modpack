## Quest-source sequencing / crossings

The addon is **not** just three bosses: it offers early passive temporary generators (Moonlight/Sunshine, Bellflower), applied-resource generators (books, lightning, food, sound, movement), special flower machines (remote Mana, fishing, villager discounts, fast farm and enchanted soil), and late mechanics (Stardust Lotus creature teleport, guardian-class boss chains). These are *optional parallel paths* in an Atlas/Questbook, not requirements before base Botania Gaia.

**TMM-specific collision leads, not automatically blockers:** Stardust Lotus provides native, **paid** creature teleportation but user's scope allows teleport; confirm behavior in pack. Annoying Flower can fish items through a separate source; include it in fishing-parchment acceptance if drops flow through shared fishing loot. Serenitian extends temporary flower lifespan and can make passive loops permanent by design. Necrofleur may harm players/pets; label combat risk but do not unilaterally rebalance.

**Research remaining after flower algorithm census:** exact addon recipe inputs and outputs, Relics/Curios/airships gear properties, resource caps item by item, and live data override proof. No quests or pack runtime changed.
