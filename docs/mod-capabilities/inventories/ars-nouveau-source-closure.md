This is pack code/QA, not an unanswered native Ars mechanic or gameplay vote.
- **Ars 'n Spells:** owner reports mana bridge already operational. Read the effective current enum and performance when implementing/configuring a changed bridge, but **do not require a fresh mana-mode vote** to write native quests or close this Atlas.
- **Ars Ocultas and Siren:** approved Source-for-sacrifice fulfillment and legitimate synthetic fishing treasure output are already documented, not design holds. Source Spawner is similarly an approved behavior with outstanding technical enforcement.
- **Arcane Permanence:** Additions' recipe is **disabled by default**. Its optional lesson AR-41 remains out of survival scope unless TMM owner enables it; excluded/disabled content is not an incomplete mandatory progression tier.
- **Future user-facing FTB work:** quest text/optional tasks, per-player credit and task-trigger limits remain owned by the chapter writer; [PR #395](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/pull/395) owns chapter layout. TMM-only code/config/loot changes receive narrow QA when implemented.
- **Source/JAR/live verification:** native mechanics, client GUI, exact addon costume/gear variants and all optional generated recipes need not be blindly replayed in Minecraft. Never call this a runtime PASS or exact assembled-pack configuration proof.
