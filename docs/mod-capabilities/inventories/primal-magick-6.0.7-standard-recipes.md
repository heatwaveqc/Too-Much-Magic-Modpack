# Primal Magick 6.0.7 — 169 standard, stonecutting, cooking and special recipe callsites

**Evidence:** pinned official [6.0.7 `Recipes.java` source](https://github.com/daedalus4096/PrimalMagick/blob/5977468bda968b994e54066236ec5719295ac9c3/common/src/main/java/com/verdantartifice/primalmagick/datagen/recipes/Recipes.java) (immutable commit `5977468bda968b994e54066236ec5719295ac9c3`). This covers the **remaining 169 literal standard/special builder invocations** after the separately indexed 610 Arcane/Concocting/Runecarving/Dissolution and 76 Ritual calls. The entire **855-literal-recipe-builder callsite census** is now covered by three source inventory files, **not** yet proof of 855 distinct runtime JSON outputs; for loops and conditionally generated recipes can change materialized counts.

The categories and numbers here are **35 Vanilla Shaped, 58 Vanilla Shapeless, 54 Stonecutting, 7 Smelting, 4 Blasting, 10 Special Craft recipe classes, 1 Smithing Trim**. Keep separate their **vanilla recipe advancement unlock** fields (`unlockedBy`/`unlocks`) from Primal Magick's Grimoire `ResearchEntry` stages; the same player action does not always increment both. `minecraft:<id>` and `primalmagick:<id>` map direct source item registrations, while unexpanded tag constant expressions remain symbolic. The factory arguments are left literal (category, count, ingredient, XP, burn/smelt time, etc.) because positional meaning varies by vanilla builder.

**Especially significant:** `minecraft` crafting constructs the original **Mundane Wand from a wooden rod plus one terrestrial Essence Dust**, so the player does **not** need an Arcane Workbench to craft that first Wand. The ten **special recipe classes** include Wand Assembly and Wand Inscription and are not generic potion/workbench shapeless crafts; these custom recipe classes must be reviewed separately for operation UI and ownership, rather than reducing them to a grid of ordinary inputs. Stonecutting produces numerous marble-family material-conversion options with output counts; verify TMM effective recipe count and balance before counting them as separate progression gates.
