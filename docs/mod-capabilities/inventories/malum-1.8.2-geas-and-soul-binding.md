# Malum 1.8.2 — registered Geases and persistent Soul Binding

**Native immutable source:** [Geas registry](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/registry/common/magic/MalumGeasEffectTypes.java). Only active (uncommented) registration statements counted; commented multiplayer Bonds and Crushing Melancholy are excluded. The source has **36 generated Soul Binding recipes**, and one special non-craft Geas from Weeping Well. The table links every registered ID to its Java implementation and native generated recipe when present. Registry names do not prove numerical effect rules; in-pack acceptance has not been run.

## How Geases are bound

**Correct native survival path:** a `malum:soul_binding` JSON's `result` field selects a **registered Geas type applied to a living target**, not an output ItemStack. Load the [Soul Brazier](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/common/block/curiosities/soul_brazier/SoulBrazierBlockEntity.java) with the **central and extra items and typed Spirits** from that recipe; right-click the ready Brazier with **one `malum:ether`** to begin BINDING, or **one `malum:paracausal_flame`** to begin UNBINDING. During the 600-tick (30s) work period, right-click the active Brazier to offer yourself as a target (or the machine periodically accepts nearby `MobEffects.WEAKNESS` targets within a radius-4 AABB). Each newly accepted target is hit for **25% maximum health** Karmic damage. After the 600 ticks, the rite debits the recipe's central/extra items/Spirits **once**, applies/removes the selected Geas to/from each captured eligible LivingEntity, and inflicts **another 50% maximum-health damage** on a target whose binding/removal fails. This is not a harmless instant craft. The optional [GeasItem.use](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/common/item/GeasItem.java) can also toggle that specific effect if such a special Geas item was separately obtained, but **do not teach item acquisition/use as the native Soul Binding recipe's output**. [GeasSoulData](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/common/data/attachment/soul_data/GeasSoulData.java) is a **persistent attached list of effect types** rather than a conventional inventory; it prevents duplicate types and limits the total by the target's **MalumAttributes.GEAS_LIMIT** attribute. The list synchronizes across server/client and displays bound Geases as extra Curios slots; [GeasEffectHandler](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/core/handlers/GeasEffectHandler.java) dispatches their per-tick attributes/effects. The displayed Curios stacks cannot simply be unequipped; **normal Geas acquisition/removal is the Soul Brazier ritual**, while separately obtained GeasItem instances can toggle their type. These are genuine player progression effects, not ordinary wearable Curios and not FE/MXP. TMM should not tie Geas persistence to the player's current Path without an explicit separate design.

## Quest and safety limits

**No crafted Geas item from the standard Soul Binding rite exists to collect**. The quest should instruct: prepare the Soul Brazier recipe, offer Ether, sacrifice the intended player (only when safe), wait 30 seconds, observe the persistent Geas and demonstrate an actual effect. For removal, prepare another appropriate rite and offer Paracausal Flame. Use a player-attachment detector only if TMM creates one, otherwise a clearly labeled manual checkmark; an item possession task would be misleading. Attribute-determined active count and source effects need not depend on TMM Progression Paths. The boolean isCreative metadata on some Geas ItemStacks is not evidence that normal survival craftable types are banned creative-class goods. Gaze's separate two additional Geases **are not included** in this base-source list.
