# Botania Revived 2.0.2 — Mana storage, personal gear and device capability primer

**Status:** first-to-last operation/balance source handoff; not a byte-by-byte exhaustive registry list or a loaded TMM client test. Current TMM pin and 2.0.2 immutable source [5f803227](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Botania-Revived/tree/5f803227d484598da1f1093a91d5d7cf5cf9600d). Identify **capacity**, **conversion**, **player equipment slot** and **consumption** separately; no universal mana bar or native player leveling is present.

| **Diluted Mana Pool** | **10,000 Mana** native capacity; accepts compatible Mana Bursts, mana items and infusion, separate pool tier. | Early limited buffer, not 1 million. | **Standard Mana Pool** | **1,000,000 Mana**; transfer modes and item drop interactions support Mana Infusion and charging/discharging eligible ManaItem stacks. | Mana in Pool and ManaItem are different stores; an empty Pool still needs source flower+Spreader. Same source. |

Potential *legitimate post-Gaia capstone*; needs owner balance classification, not blanket exclusion as an exploit. [source](https://github.com/Velron-Devs/Botania-Revived/blob/5f803227d484598da1f1093a91d5d7cf5cf9600d/src/main/java/velrondevs/botania/common/block/block_entity/mana/ManaPoolBlockEntity.java); [module audit](botania-revived-port-modules-2.0.2.md). |

## Gameplay-workflow confidence

- **Early:** Apothecary/Daisy → Endoflame and Mana Pool (1m) → mana-dipped items, Runic Altar, Mana Tablet/Band → functional flowers, redstone & Corporea.
- **Mid:** mana-producing specialist flora and Alfheim trade; elemental/Manasteel gear, Mana Mirror and mana-consuming rings/tiara; Terrasteel.
- **Late:** Gaia fights, Dice of Fate relics, high-rank Terra Shatterer, large fabulous pool/Extra Botany buffers, optional Extra Botany bosses, Creative Crafting Terra Plate results if deliberately enabled.
- **TMM counters:** vanilla experience drained by Rosa Arcana is **not** MXP; equipment source cost is **Botania Mana**; Mega Mana buffers do **not** automatically generate anything; creative pool/tablet genuinely do; Extra Botany Fluided Mana is a third storage/transfer scope.

Full item-level spells, all armor modifiers, Mana lens permutations and potion brews remain native reference/JEI coverage, not independently reproduced in this Atlas primer. An exhaustive item recipe dump would be large and is **not required to honestly describe the player's distinct progression systems**, but quest authors should inspect exact recipes from the pinned content before implementation.
