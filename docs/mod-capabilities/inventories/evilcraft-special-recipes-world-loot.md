### Source-defined dynamic-output and access corrections

- **`special/dark_tank_large`:** [`RecipeSerializerCraftingShapedCustomOutputDarkTankLargeConfig`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/core/recipe/type/RecipeSerializerCraftingShapedCustomOutputDarkTankLargeConfig.java) builds a Dark Tank with **9×** native base capacity (**144,000mB**, vs base16k). Ordinary `combine_dark_tank` and `combine_blood_extractor` custom recipe serializers list **max_capacity=655,360,000mB**; mixing volumes respects fluid-item rules and should not be misdescribed as stacking 9 items of Blood.
- **`special/box_of_eternal_closure`** [JSON](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/resources/data/evilcraft/recipe/special/box_of_eternal_closure.json) **requires six Crushed Dark Gems, two Weakness Potion items with exact potion component, and one `#c:chests/ender`**. [Its custom serializer](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/core/recipe/type/RecipeSerializerCraftingShapedCustomOutputBoxOfEternalClosureConfig.java) returns [`boxOfEternalClosureFilled`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/block/BlockBoxOfEternalClosureConfig.java), **already containing a Vengeance Swarm**. This is a meaningful spirit-acquisition alternative to first hunting one; it is NOT an empty Box. Identify whether the player empties/refills the Box before a capture quest rather than assuming crafted Box is empty.
- **`special/entangled_chalice_new`** requires **Corrupted Tear** (tier3 Blood Infuser), Gold ingots and Dark Gems, making linked-fluid infrastructure **late**. [`entangled_chalice_copy` source serializer](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/core/recipe/type/RecipeSerializerCraftingShapedCustomOutputEntangledChaliceCopyConfig.java) outputs **two chalices with the existing chalice's same shared tank ID**, not two copies of fluid. [`WorldSharedTankCache`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/core/fluid/WorldSharedTankCache.java) stores fluid by ID; [`WorldSharedTank`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/core/fluid/WorldSharedTank.java) persists it via world storage, and [`BlockEntityEntangledChalice`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/blockentity/BlockEntityEntangledChalice.java) has **4,000mB per linked tank**. **Cross-location/world cache identity** is supported by source; owner/security permissions and effective cross-dimension behavior are not asserted tested.
- **`special/minecraft_dead_bush`** and Broom part combination are dedicated custom serializers, not ordinary shapeless crafting with fixed output, and the Broom has 43 independent material part source recipes; 127-table ingredient tags may depend on absent third-party metals.
- **`smelting/burning_gem_stone`** transforms **Dark Block → Burning Gemstone** with 200ticks and source recipe XP50; this creates a protection route without tier3 Garmonbozia. **`smelting/hardened_blood_shard`** smelts Hardened Blood → 9 Shards with 200ticks and XP20. These are native stove operations, not Blood Infuser tiers or an MXP award.

Structure generated in Tectonic must still actually place successfully. Additional TMM datapacks/global modifiers can alter the effective result, but none is asserted here.

**Quest authoring consequences:** Guided portal should be **optional tutorial**, not mandatory if a chest granted the guide. The Box acquisition task should distinguish *crafted prefilled Vengeance Swarm* and *chest prefilled spirit* from an **empty** Box or an **actual Focus capture event**. A precharged random Broom legitimately lets players try flight without first manufacturing each part. A jungle-loot chest may contain guide/condensed but should not promise a Broom. Summoned mobs and spawners do not count as native boss first-kill events.
