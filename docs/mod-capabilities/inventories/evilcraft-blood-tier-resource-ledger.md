## 1. Resource identities — don't collapse into other mods' energy

### Native Promise machine capacity ladder (not player class levels)

[`BlockEntityWorking.getTankTierMultiplier(tier)`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/core/blockentity/BlockEntityWorking.java) returns **`1 << (2×tier)` = 4^tier**, and after a real Promise slot change sets tank capacity to base tank×multiplier. For the 10,000mB base Infuser/Furnace/Reanimator:

| Machine tier | Blood tank capacity | Infuser recipes | First canonical Promise progression |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 0 | **10,000mB** | No-tier/tier0 only | Empty Bowl craft from **3 Dark Power Gems**; Dust Bowl = Empty Bowl + **2 Crushed Dark Gems**; infuse Dusted Bowl with **5,000mB** → tier0 Bowl; Infuse **Iron Block +10,000mB** (tier0) → Iron Promise Acceptor; then shapeless **tier0–3 Bowl + Iron Acceptor + Spider Eye** → `promise_tier_1` |
| 1 | **40,000mB** | tier≤1 | Make tier1 Bowl from Dusted Bowl with 5,000mB; infuse **Gold Block +40,000mB** at tier1 → Gold Promise Acceptor; shapeless **tier1–3 Bowl + Gold Acceptor + `#c:ender_pearls`** → `promise_tier_2` |
| 2 | **160,000mB** | tier≤2 | Make tier2 Bowl from Dusted Bowl with 5,000mB; infuse **Diamond Block +160,000mB** at tier2 → Diamond Acceptor; shapeless **tier2/3 Bowl + Diamond Acceptor + Eye of Ender** → `promise_tier_3` |
| 3 | **640,000mB** | tier≤3, including 50,000mB Garmonbozia | Tier3 Bowl from Dusted Bowl +5,000mB exists at tier3; **it is not a prerequisite to first obtaining tier3**, since Promise3 recipe accepts tier2 Bowl |

**Upgrade implementation constraints:** [`BlockBloodInfuserConfig`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/block/BlockBloodInfuserConfig.java) enables five Promise types: tier1/2/3, Speed, Efficiency. [`BlockEntityBloodInfuser`](https://github.com/CyclopsMC/EvilCraft/blob/1.21.1-1.2.100/src/main/java/org/cyclops/evilcraft/blockentity/BlockEntityBloodInfuser.java) applies efficiency factor to Blood cost, Speed to operation time and container fill, with four physical upgrade slots and tier-dependent availability. Recipe JSON's listed **minimum fluid input** is the native static recipe match: an in-machine Promise can change effective draw on processing, so don't claim exact measured final usage without applicable active slot values. Speed/Efficiency Promises are shapeless craft with **tier1–3 Bowl + Iron Acceptor + `#c:storage_blocks/redstone`/`#c:storage_blocks/lapis`** respectively; their separate effects are not machine-tier gates.

## 4. Native vs TMM integration

- **EvilCraft canonical Blood:** This is the selected **common fluid identity for TMM's cross-mod blood unification**. Native `bloodConverters` has legacy alias strings and an implicit converter used by selected machines, but does **not** by itself establish JAWR/Hexerei/Iron's bottle or tank transfer, healing, or crafting return. Describe planned KubeJS/bucket/bottle bridges with their actual integration PRs, not as existing native functionality.
- **Player global MXP:** Source Blood consumption in **machines**, passive blood generation, potion/food effects, and vein-sword extraction aren't interchangeable with a *player spell expenditure* event. MXP award policy belongs to common TMM Progression and needs a per-action owner ledger. No Path-owned MXP pool should be inferred from these fluids.
- **Species/food:** `flesh_rejuvenated` has a custom eater and was identified for broad *magical food* use. Don't replace its FoodData custom handler with vanilla fixed nutrition or give it free/bypassing blood. Real dragon/JAWR/werewolf outcomes require consumer-specific runtime verification if a TMM adapter changes their eater rules.
- **Ownership/QA:** Every number here is **source-default** at pinned 1.2.100, not evidence of effective loaded config or tested cross-mod conversion. No source-only client regression suite required. Any actual TMM gameplay modification to recipes, mob gates, food consumption, blood networking or quest event acceptance gets separate targeted integration QA.
