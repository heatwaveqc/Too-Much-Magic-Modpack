# Neo Vitae 1.1.54 — operational ritual effects and limits (48 regular + 3 imperfect)

**Source:** [Neo Vitae MC 1.21.1 at `501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21`](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/tree/501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21), source-declared **1.1.54** matching TMM current version. This is a **semantic class survey of all 48 registered regular rituals**; exact individual startup EV, refresh EV, tick period, activation crystal are in the existing complete [46 data-map + two class-only dungeon contracts](neovitae-resource-and-ritual-stats.md). Geometry for the **48 exact ritual rune layouts** is in [A](neovitae-1.1.54-ritual-layouts-a.md), [B](neovitae-1.1.54-ritual-layouts-b.md) and [C](neovitae-1.1.54-ritual-layouts-c.md). This is not a claim that every item/ritual operated in TMM's actual assembled client; effective loaded datapack and game QA remain distinct.

**Activation:** [MasterRitualStoneBlockEntity](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21/src/main/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/common/blockentity/MasterRitualStoneBlockEntity.java) checks `RitualStats.enabled`, crystal level, exact loaded rune structure and ritual-specific validation, then **charges the activating player's own UUID-linked Anima network**; it records actual actor in `RitualEvent.Activated` and the `RITUAL_ACTIVATED` criterion. These are truthful lesson triggers (stronger than possession proxy), if the quest owner builds an adapter; no FTB hook is claimed integrated. Ongoing work checks the owner's Anima and deducts on actual operations in individual classes. A nominal `refresh_cost` is **not a universal per-second upkeep**.

## Three imperfect rituals (distinct activation system)

These use an [Imperfect Ritual Stone](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21/src/main/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/ritual/ImperfectRitual.java) and a required material above it, **not the 48 Master-Stone rune circles**:

| Imperfect ID | Required block matched by class | Source action | Per-ritual constructor EV |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `neovitae:rain` | water | Rain/thunder for 6,000 game ticks on ServerLevel | 5,000 |
| `neovitae:zombie` | coal block | Spawns persistent, leather-chestplate-equipped zombie two blocks above ritual | 5,000 |
| `neovitae:resistance` | bedrock | Grants player Fire Resistance (amplifier 1) for 1,200 ticks | 5,000 |

Evidence: [Rain](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21/src/main/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/ritual/imperfect/ImperfectRitualRain.java), [Zombie](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21/src/main/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/ritual/imperfect/ImperfectRitualZombie.java), [Resistance](https://github.com/breakinblocks/NeoVitae/blob/501a8d6a0431ed8aeb19e18be2e83f1856f29a21/src/main/java/com/breakinblocks/neovitae/ritual/imperfect/ImperfectRitualResistance.java). Normal activation handler determines actual EV source/structure validation and any additional checks; these constructor figures alone are **not** proof that a 5,000 EV account succeeds.

## Source corrections over earlier Atlas and guide summaries

1. **Misleading registry name:** `RitualMagnetism` **is ore quarrying**, not loose-item pickup; `RitualDeusMagnetism` is a more powerful, configurable quarry. **Zephyr** collects loose items / ordinary XP, while the **Sigil of Magnetism** magnetizes inventory drops. Quest prose must distinguish these three functions.
