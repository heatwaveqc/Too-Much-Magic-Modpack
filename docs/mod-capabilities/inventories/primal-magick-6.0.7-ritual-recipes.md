Certain enchanted outputs are direct source ItemStack expressions like `ItemsPM.PRIMAL_SHOVEL.get().getDefaultEnchantedInstance(lookupProvider)`; their **registry item identity alone does not encode their enchantments or custom data**. Preserve these expressions rather than falsely treating them as a normal plain output.
- `Items.X` and `primalmagick:<id>` indicate actual vanilla / source-registered items; unexpanded `CommonTags`/`ItemTagsPM` references represent **tag ingredients**, whose installed membership can change. E.g. Manafruit Earth 5 + Sea 5 + Sky 5 + Sun 5 + Moon 5 = **25 displayed total Mana**, but **all five types must be available**.
- Props are placed in the actual **salt-powered connected** ritual arrangement (not additional consumed items). Instability is a recipe-specific contribution to stability drift, *not* a probability of success or a static danger level; symmetry and props can offset it. No ritual is labeled guaranteed safe.
- `RitualRecipeBuilder` validates at least one ingredient, one research requirement, and bounded instability; all 76 source calls met the extraction minimum. The builder defaults recipe ID to output item ID unless a custom save ID is explicitly provided.
