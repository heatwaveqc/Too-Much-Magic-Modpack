# Malum 1.8.2 — all 37 native Geas *effect-class* pros, cons and exact identity audit

**Evidence:** **all 37 registered Geas behavior classes** separately inspected at exact [Malum 1.8.2 commit](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/tree/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408), matching the 37 active registrations in [Geas registry](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/registry/common/magic/MalumGeasEffectTypes.java). Source-level semantic summary, not a complete simulator of all events, item compatibility or the published release JAR. The [crafting/access + real Soul Brazier binding rules](malum-1.8.2-geas-and-soul-binding.md) are separate. Gaze's 2 advertised additional Geases are **not counted** here.

**Mechanics:** All normal Geases are player/entity-attached passives bound via a 30-second Soul Brazier rite with Ether and a Karmic health sacrifice, and limited by the `GEAS_LIMIT` attribute (native default 2). They are **not FTB/Progression Path skills** and do not consume MXP. Many respond to LivingDamage, Spirit collection, hunger, Curios, staff charge, malignant crit or potion events, rather than simply giving a static attribute boost. These notes include negative consequences and required conditions so the questbook cannot misleadingly present an effect as unconditional.

## High-risk integration findings

1. **Possible upstream constructor bug — Berserker type identity:** [BerserkerGeas.java](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/common/geas/pact/wicked/BerserkerGeas.java) in 1.8.2 has `super(MalumGeasEffectTypes.PACT_OF_THE_REAPER.get())`, even though the [registry](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/registry/common/magic/MalumGeasEffectTypes.java) registers `PACT_OF_THE_BERSERKER -> new BerserkerGeas()`. Other inspected Geas constructors use their intended registered holder. The class's *own damage behavior* appears Berserker-specific, but its **base Geas identity is wrong**, potentially affecting tooltip, effect cache, registry checks, Curios item display or binding; the actual outcome must be tested against pinned JAR and TMM runtime before declaring an observed bug or authoring an active Berserker quest. This is a source warning, no runtime patch made.
2. **Player death protection is truly present:** `pact_of_wyrd_reconstruction` cancels an eligible `LivingDeathEvent` once, restores randomized health, and triggers 12 Soul-collection pulses followed by **36,000 ticks** of Wyrd Exhaustion; technical and Malum Void damage types bypass it. **Correct the Atlas death_respawn axis: native conditional death-prevention is present**, although this does not provide a grave, spawnpoint or respawn system. [Exact class](https://github.com/SammySemicolon/Malum-Mod/blob/03b743a37f3eeb0cc7f4364f0730e1f135f78408/src/main/java/com/sammy/malum/common/geas/pact/infernal/WyrdReconstructionGeas.java). Distinguish from TMM Bare Zerker's independent fury-gated Totem effect and the native Soul Ward defensive buffer.
3. **Food Paths and diets:** `pact_of_self_care`, `pact_of_contentedness`, `pact_of_the_profane_ascetic`, and `pact_of_the_profane_glutton` change hunger, legitimate food safety, damage, recovery and saturation. `Profane Ascetic` directly punishes ordinary foods while rewarding tagged `GROSS_FOODS`, potentially conflicting with TMM dragon diets, all-food census and universal magical foods. **Do not hard-code exclusions or quest directions** before the data-driven food bridge reconciles intended diet and Malum Geas effects.
4. **No-armor alternate builds:** `pact_of_the_lone_druid` grants armor/healing when not wearing armor, with a severe movement/Karmic consequence otherwise. This can coexist with TMM's Monk and Bare Zerker Paths as an intentional magic route; the Geas itself must not be mistakenly tied to a single Path or considered a new active skill.
5. **Source outputs and events:** Some Geas classes have no direct `update` method because they contribute persistent attribute modifiers; others rely on static/global event listeners in the parent mod. The individual class links are a **behavior summary**, not proof that the listener is wired in the exact assembled JAR; test only discovered TMM-owned bridge issues or the identified constructor mismatch.
