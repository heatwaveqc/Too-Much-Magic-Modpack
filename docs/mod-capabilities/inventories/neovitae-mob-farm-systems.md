**Correction to previous Atlas:** `RitualZephyr` was mistakenly classified as movement in the registration catalog and only listed as `logistics` in the 29-axis crosswalk. Its primary function is **item/XP collection**; as a mob-farm downstream collector it belongs in `mob_farming` as well, while Vortex, Repulsion, Containment and Expulsion belong in `mob_farming` as **transport/control**, not merely unrelated debuffs.

## Teachable first-to-endgame farm chain

1. **Early, no ritual required:** Inscribe Repulsion (hostile mobs only) or Collection Array (drops in 2 blocks); demonstrate physically moving one safe target and collecting one dropped item without attributing either to a kill.
2. **Tier-I array movement:** Acquire Tabula Robur and Blood Pearl; make the Vortex Sigil (base before catalyst). Pull an eligible hostile toward a protected collection/kill area. Show redstone pause, the player's immunity with an Orb, and the optional **Tabula Rasa player exclusion** without asserting all creatures are affected identically.
3. **Tier-II ritual loot/XP:** Build and activate **The Gathering** (`zephyr`) with a chest directly atop its Master Ritual Stone. Drop a test item at near and far points inside the actual bounds; verify insertion and overflow. Then kill a valid basic mob; distinguish item drops from **vanilla XP orbs**. Try a Tome of Peritia in first slot of supported chest to see XP absorption versus orb movement to player. Verify no movement when outside range, on lost owner context or with insufficient Anima.
4. **Combine with actual harvest:** Build a safe mob generator, align Vortex/Repulsion/Containment to route entities into a legitimate killing or Spiritus/EV-harvest mechanism (Spike Array or ritual Well of Suffering). The Gathering handles **item drops/XP after a real death**, not the creatures. Distinguish Well's EV into network/altar from dropped Spiritus, ordinary items and player MXP; the Gathering's vanilla XP/Tome is **not** proof it earns Tyz's MXP.
5. **Upper end:** Contrast the entity-heavy Well/Spike/collection arrangement with **Torment Nexus**, which can simulate spawner harvest for EV *without spawning actual mobs*. A no-entity simulation does **not** provide ordinary physical drop/XP entities for The Gathering to collect unless a separate source explicitly creates them. Check anti-boss-farm, chunk and spawner exclusions in TMM.

## Name and cross-mod disambiguation

- Neo Vitae's **Vortex Sigil** and **Repulsion Array** are the *native persistent push/pull glyph-like effects* in this dossier.
- **Ars Nouveau** separately has `ars_nouveau:glyph_pull` (a cast effect, not the same as a passive Vortex Array). A third-party **Ars Zero** addon exposes a Push glyph in some 1.21.1 reference catalogs, but it was **not found in TMM's `manifest/mods.json`** at the inspected baseline. Do not attribute that addon glyph to Neo Vitae or promise its presence in the pack. The actual installed Ars glyph/spell list and cross-mod farming interactions should be verified in the independent Ars dossier.

## Pack acceptance and boundaries

- Test nonplayer entity eligibility: hostile/passive, boss or immune mob, Endermen, spawner vs naturally spawned, modded protected mobs, FTB claims, PvP/player safety and loaded chunks. If a mob is redirected but lacks eligible farm drops, movement is not evidence of an approved EV exploit.
- Test Vortex on/off with redstone, Tabula Rasa player-exclusion, Orb player-exclusion, return of slate on removal, unknown owner/network after reload, and whether spending actually matches chance-rate expectations across sufficient ticks. Verify Repulsion mob categorization and unintended escape routes.
- Test **The Gathering** with chest, overflow, absent chest, online owner, supported Tome first slot, modded inventory handlers and no items or XP to collect. Its collection happens **after** loot entities exist, so preserving the originating mob's proper loot/XP/drop logic is separate work.
- Verify whether TMM's XP/MXP bridge converts experience or blocks vanilla XP storage; the source only shows vanilla `ExperienceOrb` and `ExperienceTomeItem` operations, not Tyz MXP.
- All sourced numbers are version-aligned declarations, **not** a proof that these six mechanics are pack-installed, runtime-tested or quest-detector-supported.
