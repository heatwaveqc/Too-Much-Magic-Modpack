# Enigmatic Legacy+ 1.21.1-1.1.2 — artifact abilities, native attributes and death compatibility

**Source audit:** 2026-11-14 (Phoenix time). **Pinned pack JAR:** `enigmaticlegacyplus-1.21.1-1.1.2`. **Upstream:** [Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus @ `9093d40` (2026-10-12)](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/tree/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022); [official release](https://modrinth.com/mod/enigmaticlegacy-plus/version/1.21.1-1.1.2). Source `gradle.properties` identifies `mod_version=1.21.1-1.1.2`. The exact downloaded JAR has **not** been independently decompiled or gameplay-tested; upstream source may differ from packaged build in fine details.

## Conclusion

Enigmatic Legacy+ is **predominantly artifact/Curios-driven**: accessories, cursed progression, scrolls, spellstone crafting, powerful tools, loot, potion/enchantment manipulation, exploration, and substantial death/inventory mechanics. It is **not** a new freeform mana/spell-school system.

However, certain Curios accessories called **spellstones** have **activated abilities triggered by a dedicated keybind**, and the mod **registers three real living-entity attributes**: `enigmaticlegacyplus:etherium_shield`, `enigmaticlegacyplus:projectile_deflect`, `enigmaticlegacyplus:lifesteal`. It adds them to **all living entity types** in `EnigmaticLegacy.attributeSetup(EntityAttributeModificationEvent)`, so they can be read/modified on players without a new source modification, subject to runtime confirmation.

**Matrix category: Confirmed (native attributes, source-backed).** **Design recommendation: no dedicated Enigmatic-specific Pufferfish tree or new code bridge**. Consider existing universal survivability / offense skills only if they need these distinct attributes; avoid powerful additional boosts to already high-tier gear. This is a recommendation, not a user-approved rule. Existing artifacts retain all their vanilla Enigmatic abilities.

## What spellstones actually do

- Curios [`ISpellstone`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/api/item/ISpellstone.java) supports one equipped spellstone in an `enigmaticlegacy:spellstone` slot (checked by `SpellstoneItem.canEquip`). Pressing the spellstone key sends `SpellstoneKeyPacket` to the server; applicable items run `triggerActiveAbility` and add **shared item-based cooldown** to every registered spellstone item. No cost in common player mana was found.
- Most spellstones supply **passive** boosts too; `Spelltuner` can retain a tuned passive secondary spellstone benefit, and `CosmicScroll` reduces spellstone cooldown by **32% default** vs **10%** from Spelltuner (mutually exclusive if/else in `ISpellstone.triggerActiveAbility`). Vanilla item cooldowns govern activation; projectiles/effects can be generated by an active ability.
- [`EyeOfNebula`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/contents/item/spellstones/EyeOfNebula.java): targeted teleport, magical resistance and dodge-related features.
- [`AngelBlessing`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/contents/item/spellstones/AngelBlessing.java): mobility and projectile deflection.
- [`RevivalLeaf`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/contents/item/spellstones/RevivalLeaf.java): passive regeneration, beneficial nature/area effect and optional plant-adjacent flight.
- [`GolemHeart`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/contents/item/spellstones/GolemHeart.java): heavy armor and knockback resistance.
- [`CreationHeart`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/contents/item/spellstones/CreationHeart.java): creative flight, resistance to harmful effects, active **ultimate wither skull** attack, and extremely strong death-cancel behavior.
- [`TheCube`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/contents/item/spellstones/TheCube.java): substantial mobility, random/other teleportation and a **lethal-hit rescue when off cooldown**, plus defensive effects. Config `autoTrigger` defaults **true**. [`VoidPearl`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/contents/item/spellstones/VoidPearl.java) gives **30% default** chance to cancel ordinary death.
- [`LostEngine`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/contents/item/spellstones/LostEngine.java): mobility / critical effects, and **on every third server tick calls `player.getCooldowns().tick()`** while equipped. Vanilla also advances this timer normally every tick. **Likely speeds up all item-based cooldowns, including those from other mods, not only Enigmatic.** Must test on Scriptor/Goety/other item cooldowns before deciding if any compatibility patch is warranted; this is *not proof* all spell systems use vanilla cooldowns.

## Native attributes and whether a Pufferfish node would matter

| Registered player attribute (source key namespace `enigmaticlegacyplus`) | Source behavior | Pufferfish notes |
|---|---|---|
| `etherium_shield` | Percent-style shield threshold (0–1); used by Etherium gear and attachments, affecting damage reduction when health falls under threshold | A small additive increase could increase survivability but may scale very strongly with high-max-HP builds; **do not blindly grant positive base to everyone**. |
| `projectile_deflect` | Percent chance (0–1) for incoming projectiles to be reversed/reflected; `ProjectileImpactEvent` reads player attribute directly | Easily scalable with generic defensive skill; beware stacking to 100% deflection and compat with modded projectiles / claim protections. |
| `lifesteal` | `LivingDamageEvent.Post` reads attacker's attribute and heals proportional to actual damage; cap 64, default 0 | Easily scalable with generic offensive skill; **very high raw ceiling and scaling with modded high damage**; avoid blindly adding multiples. |

Registered in [`EnigmaticAttributes`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/registries/EnigmaticAttributes.java); present on **every living entity** through [`EnigmaticLegacy.attributeSetup`](https://github.com/Auviotre/Enigmatic-Legacy-Plus/blob/9093d40910e66f7e58217d994222c310f62f3022/src/main/java/auviotre/enigmatic/legacy/EnigmaticLegacy.java). These are unlike Enchanted's block-owned altar power, and can be targetable by Pufferfish attribute modifiers *without* a custom upstream source bridge. Whether to include any of these in **existing generic** perks is a separate balance decision; do not introduce a standalone Enigmatic branch just because a native attribute exists.

## Death, soul loss, items, inventory — serious cross-mod integration priority

1. **Creation Heart immortality:** at `LivingDeathEvent` **HIGHEST with `receiveCanceled=true`**, if it is the equipped spellstone **or present as an item**, cancels death (except technical damage). If death wasn't already canceled, sets health to 1. **Inventory presence matters**, not simply Curios slot occupancy. Its potion immunity also may conflict with morph/transform states and negative effects.
2. **The Cube:** at `LivingDeathEvent` **HIGHEST**, conditional rescue based on its tracked pre-damage health; separately at **LOW** priority, if equipped and off item cooldown, **cancels death**, restores 30% max HP and grants multiple buffs; auto-trigger teleport default TRUE. Test changes to death location and multi-handler priorities.
3. **Void Pearl:** `LivingDeathEvent` **HIGHEST**, default **30%** chance to cancel death and restore 1 HP (excluding technical damage).
4. **Cosmic Scroll:** independently runs a `LivingDeathEvent` handler for revival on its own cooldown, creating *another* death interception path.
5. **Ring of the Seven Curses:** optional high-risk, high-reward Curios ring. At **HIGHEST** death priority records probable curse state; at **LOWEST (receiveCanceled=true)** distinguishes actual vs canceled death, and at `LivingDropsEvent` creates **Soul Crystals / Storage Crystals** and potentially relocates/deletes player inventory drops via its own `SoulArchive` and persistent item entities. With default `soulCrystalsMode=NEED_CURSE_RING`, a death wearing the cursed ring with `keepInventory=false` can increase lost-soul count. Each lost soul applies **–10% maximum health multiplicatively**. Soul Crystals can recover lost souls when used. `maxSoulCrystalLoss` defaults **9**, so **true permanent death (requires loss ≥10 AND config maximum ≥10)** is **not enabled by default**, but 9 lost souls is still a severe debuff. Configurable `ultraHardcore` defaults false, `autoEquip` defaults false, `giveStarterGear` defaults true — ring typically given on first login but not automatically forced equipped.
6. **Inventory/drop mechanics:** `EldritchAmulet.keepInventory` defaults true for the amulet's special inventory preservation; Escape Scroll and Ascension/Storage Crystal may move death drops to spawn, store them or create nonstandard persistent entities. These can conflict with **Corail Tombstone / other grave systems** and any single-authority death handler in the pack.
7. **Event priority hazards:** `CursedRing` tracks state at HIGHEST and decides final handling at LOWEST. Any mod canceling death between those points must be verified to suppress unwanted soul loss, while survival from pre-death poppets/totems may prevent a LivingDeathEvent entirely. **No guarantee** all installed mods' priorities compose. Test the actual B6 JAR and custom transformation provider together.
8. **Other systemic effects:** Cursed Ring defaults include `experienceBonus=300`, `enchantingBonus=10`, and additional Fortune/Looting. Audit actual XP reward multiplicative behavior relative to Pufferfish XP/FTB Quests and other high XP systems. A Cursed Ring may enhance monster AI with `forTheWorthy=true` default and has player-related item restrictions; do not confuse it with a generic character class.
9. **Special caution:** Lost Engine's extra `ItemCooldowns.tick` may shorten **The Cube's own fatal rescue interval** as well as *other mods'* item cooldowns, but only one standard spellstone can normally be equipped. Test via Spelltuner/related interactions and when mixing effects.

### Suggested death test matrix extensions (disposable B6 world)

- For each of **Creation Heart**, **Cube off/on cooldown**, **Void Pearl**, **Cosmic Scroll**, **Enchanted poppet (inventory/shelf)**, **vanilla Totem**, **Tombstone grave** and each transformation/phylactery provider: pre-lethal damage → canceled death? actual `LivingDeathEvent`? tomb generated? inventory/soul crystal moved? player respawn? item consumed? cooldown and health state?
- Pair with **JAWR vampire/lycan/lich**, **Goety lich**, **Vampirism** vampire/werewolf, and Dragon Survival only if later added. Verify that a canceled death does **not** trigger phylactery consumption, grave generation, inventory duplication or soul-loss increment.
- Test Cursed Ring with keepInventory **false and true**, **not equipped** vs cursed, default 9-soul ceiling, storage crystal with full Curios inventory, ESCAPE scroll, amulet keep-inventory. Back up world and use sacrificial test characters.
- Confirm whether **Creation Heart just held** truly prevents death and whether technical damage / admin `/kill` bypasses it; ensure there is a reliable admin reset/escape from unplanned immortality.
- Test Lost Engine cooldown speed on vanilla ender pearls, all spellstones, any known other mod item, and client-visible cooldown HUD vs authoritative server.
- Test high lifesteal + large magic damage and Projectile Deflection with magic/projectile entities. Do not assume attributes enforce their own balanced multipliers.
- Confirm pinned 1.1.2 release's behavior, not only matching-version source.

## Design choices and actions

- **User approved for Enchanted: Witchcraft:** *no custom bridge/skills planned*, leaving its ritual, poppet and altar mechanics intact. This is a distinct user decision from **Enigmatic**.
- **User decision for Enigmatic (2026-11-14):** source hooks **Confirmed**, **no bespoke Pufferfish track or custom spellstone bridge**. Native `lifesteal`, `projectile_deflect`, `etherium_shield` may optionally participate in broad existing Pufferfish passive skills after balance testing. Do not touch spellstone cooldowns, alter immortality rules, or add new mana/caster attributes without explicit design approval.
- **Priority:** death-handlers + graves + cooldown interoperability tests, then gameplay ability spot checks.
- **No mod additions/removals, config modifications, KubeJS integration, datapack patches or gameplay changes made** during this audit.
