## Uranium source gate

**Approved natural source:** `alexscaves:radrock_uranium_ore` generated by `alexscaves:radrock_uranium_ore` placed feature in `alexscaves:toxic_caves` *in the Overworld only*, on `alexscaves:radrock`. Alex's Caves already embeds this feature in its toxic biome data. Do not add a second, competing uranium ore vein.

**Disabled broad source:** BiggerReactors Rework 0.1.6's `biggerreactorsrework:overworld_ores` NeoForge biome modifier originally adds `biggerreactorsrework:uranium_ore` to all `#is_overworld` biomes. We replace **that exact ID** with `{"type":"neoforge:none"}` in:

`kubejs/data/biggerreactorsrework/neoforge/biome_modifier/overworld_ores.json`.

This prevents the default reactor uranium source from bypassing the Toxic Caves exploration gate. It does **not** alter ordinary iron/copper/etc ores or mutate old chunks. Check that no other generators introduce these blocks, including alternate dimensions, custom biome injections, custom magic drills, loot or miners.

**One-way bridge:** `kubejs/server_scripts/toxic_caves_reactor_uranium.js` adds `1 alexscaves:uranium -> 1 biggerreactorsrework:uranium_ingot` (crafting grid). Alex's native shards, uranium, blocks and recipes remain available. No reverse recipe and no extra world generation are introduced. Keep alternative extraction/EMC closed until the larger resource allowlist audit.

All IDs above are source-inspected. **The specific pinned 1.1.2 JAR must still be runtime-registry checked**: upstream main may have diverged from the released JAR, and static KubeJS parsing is not proof that any worldgen override or ingredient loads in game.
