## Important correction to shortcut wording

**Not all Path dietary rules are binary allowlists.** Share **food classification tags**, but consume them through distinct policy types:

1. **Hard permit/deny**: Gilded and Dryad.
2. **Nutritional affinity/penalty/override, often still edible**: Cave/Forest/Sea Dragons. Their mixed/off-diet foods remain edible but receive reduced nutrition/saturation, under #444; do not turn the shared tag into a blanket denial list.
3. **Native feeding/resource compatibility and native penalty exemption**: JAWR Vampire. Food permission, ordinary-hunger penalty, and actual **blood** reward are different decisions.
4. **Modifiers only**: Monk's Hunger Discipline/exhaustion; Gilded Golden 2× hunger/actual saturation POINTS; other Paths if subsequently designed.
5. **No diet policy**: all Paths lacking a specific owner-approved food restriction.

Recipe classifier generates/merges tags; Path engine chooses a declared policy function. Species-specific **numeric food overrides**, recipe counts/thermal stage, rare item effects, source-specific resource debit and item stack provenance remain independent from tag membership. Never mutate ordinary human food components globally.

## Distinctive exceptions that static item tags CANNOT express

1. **Gilded lava catches:** Existing Gilded exploratory design permits an item normally forbidden (e.g. ordinary cooked chicken/cod) *only when the actual stack* bears a verified `minecraft:custom_data` marker `tmm_progression:lava_caught=true` from lava fishing. A global item tag for cooked chicken would over-permit every cooked chicken. Keep a **stack-component predicate in the Gilded eligibility resolver**, in addition to normal item tags. Source: archived Gilded [Lava Fishing feasibility](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/blob/main/docs/archive/historical-branches-2026-11-19/gilded/docs/explorations/gilded/LAVA-FISHING-FEASIBILITY.md); actual mod/mark integration unverified.
2. **JAWR vampire:** Pinned Jar `VampireEvents.isSolidFood` checks its native `FOODS_TAG` **as a trigger** for the ordinary-food penalty, NOT as a safe-food allowlist. Separate positive TMM blood-safe classification and a narrow suppression of native penalty for approved foods. Bloody meals must restore *blood*, not arbitrary hunger; item tags don't encode underfilled bottles or the approved 5×/2× economics.
3. **Dryad + Forest Dragon (explicitly approved pair #452):** Combined policies must be evaluated deliberately. Dryad's **ordinary-food veto** should not be silently overridden by Forest Dragon plant affinity. Magical exceptions universally apply; drinks/potions permitted by Dryad must still traverse any relevant source physiology checks. Environmental photosynthesis remains functioning while in Forest Dragon form. Exact handling of an ordinary Forest-approved fruit while Dryad is active is a **clear precedence acceptance case**; default conservative interpretation is **Dryad veto**, not unrestricted Forest eating.
4. **Recreational beverages:** [#334](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/issues/334) preserves vanilla/required potions, magical items and native intoxication; Gilded and JAWR Werewolf have baseline alcohol permission. Other incompatible recreational drinks use the approved **Acquired Taste** unlock, without granting otherwise-incompatible food/blood/saturation. A common drink tag must distinguish **recreational drinks vs therapeutic potions vs food-backed juices**; `useAnimation=DRINK` is not reliable enough.
5. **Prepared foods:** A single food item can be produced from multiple recipes. Generated common tags identify eligibility/affinity, but per-species mixed-food penalties still require source ingredient ratios, cooked-vs-raw stage, source values, recipe yield and origin confidence. Preserve source-input uncertainty and native FD cutting economics. Another mod's tag contribution must not cause hardcoded per-item whitelist conflicts, yet its numeric source value may require an explicit fallback rather than automatically giving 6/2.0 to every item in a category.
6. **Magical:** `#tmm:foods/magical` gives universal **permission**, not universal nutritional amount. Retain native effects and 0/0 adapter for EvilCraft Rejuvenated Flesh and Botania Fruit of Grisaia; no item-use duplication. `#c:foods/golden` remains distinct, only Gilded adds 2× when Golden-qualified.

## Proposed minimal shared tag additions (NOT new owner-approved mechanics)

- **Use existing** `#c:foods/raw_meat`, `#c:foods/raw_fish`, `#c:foods/fruit`, `#c:foods/vegetable`, `#c:foods/golden`; classify more plant/mushroom and raw/cooked family distinctions where the effective mod registry supports them, rather than a Cartesian product of tags.
- **Use the new approved** `#c:foods/nether_only`, `#c:foods/with_nether` (strict every-recipe requirement), `#c:foods/raw_nether`, and **universal** `#tmm:foods/magical`.
- **Propose one positive vampire-compatible blood-nourishment category** (e.g. `#tmm:foods/vampire_nourishment`; final name must not collide with native JAWR `FOODS_TAG` penalty). In addition to *permission*, custom items carry verifiable blood resource values and special on-use logic.
- **Propose one drink classification** (reuse existing common beverage tags where possible; a first-party `#tmm:drinks/recreational` overlay if required). It must distinguish medicine/potions from recreational food-backed drinks; relevant to Dryad and Acquired Taste. An item-only tag cannot encode native intoxication, container effects or a special potion case.
- **No automatic diet tags** for Monk, Hunter, Bare Zerker, Newbie/Normie, Sapient Slime, Sexy Demon, Spawn, Werewolf or either lich without a grounded owner decision.

## Implementation order and test contract

1. Produce canonical shared food provenance/family/preparation tags from recipe/loot classifier **without destructive replacement of mod-provided tag entries**. Verify `/reload` resolves mod/data pack contributions; preserve traceability of overrides and ambiguous alternate recipes. Revisit Gilded candidate because the old 50-item `#tmm:foods/nether` is NOT automatically an accurate `nether_only` tag.
2. Implement a **generic food-policy evaluator** with modes: `strict_allow`, `diet_affinity`, `native_penalty_exemption`, `post_consumption_nourishment_modifier`, `no_policy`, plus explicit `stack_origin_predicate` and `special_resource_feeding`. Keep generic executable code in Progression engine; keep Path-specific rules as declarative config/data and native species bridge.
3. Migrate Gilded and the three Dragons first, then Dryad and the narrow JAWR Vampire exemptions; leave other Paths unrestricted. Preserve alcohol/Acquired Taste through the same typed consumable classifier.
4. Test mod tag extensions/removals after reload, candidate classification of raw Strider Slice/Ghasta for Sea Dragon, Gilded Golden ×2 exactly once, Gilded lava-caught special item stacks, ordinary human food untouched, Dryad + Forest Dragon, blood meals 5×/2× and native effects once, and mixed food still edible to Dragons with an affinity penalty.
5. **Open owner-level choice:** exact rule precedence/consumption for Dryad + Forest Dragon when tags overlap, and optional additional nutrition rules for undeath or speculative new Paths. **Not an implicit greenlight:** do not invent those behaviors or launch Minecraft runtime/QA from this audit. Numeric cooking/mixed-food coefficients remain as separately tracked in #444.

**Technical caution:** `replace:false` merges additive generated tag contributions; it does **not** automatically implement user-configurable removals from a tag that another provider has also added. Removing a native member requires supported datapack removals/explicit deny overlay with well-defined precedence and resolved tag caches. Likewise recipe classifier regeneration and live `/reload` must not drift apart. This is engineering reconciliation, not evidence that the solution is already working.
