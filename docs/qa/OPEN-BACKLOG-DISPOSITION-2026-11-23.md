## What the categories mean

- **C — Closable:** redundant source donor / completed narrow administrative issue; close only after verifying successor and leaving a provenance comment. It does **not** mean gameplay QA passed or historical branch deletion is authorized.
- **R — Ready for review/integration:** source patch, config, research, or completed narrow code slice can be reviewed by its designated owner now; stacked or artifact/ABI dependencies are identified in the row. It is NOT necessarily mergeable directly to `test/full-integration` and does not imply game-test authorization.
- **N — Needs code/engineering:** meaningful runnable behavior, fresh evidence generation, targeted adapter, or data migration remains before a finished integration candidate.
- **D — Needs design/owner decision:** substantive choices, research selection, detailed balance/effect signoff, or approved-but-deferred future plans block the full scope. Some draft documentation can be preserved without implementing it.
- **X — Conflicts with current build/owner decision:** naive replay would overwrite newer selected behavior, use stale namespaces, create incompatible starters/food/quests, or publish misleading snapshot. Preserve unique ideas and reconcile; do not mass-merge.
- **Q — QA/operations, keep open:** gameplay-only acceptance or standing governance/automation tracker has no outstanding implementation that can fairly be inferred from the issue itself. Added sixth category to avoid calling a test obligation “closable” or “needs code.”
