## The Graveyard 4.0.1 — actual NeoForge 1.21.1 source review

- Previously owner-approved [#386](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/issues/386), candidate pinned **source-only** by another writer in [#399](https://github.com/heatwaveqc/Too-Much-Magic-Modpack/pull/399): CF **514468/9346778**, `graveyard-NEOFORGE-4.0.1+mc1.21.1.jar`; requires [VillagersTradingPlus 1.1.1](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/villagerstradingplus/files/9346744), GeckoLib >=4.9.3 already installed. Do not duplicate the third-party pin branch files.
- [Exact 1.21.1 public source](https://github.com/finallion/The_Graveyard/tree/1.21.1) has `gradle.properties` version 4.0.1, NeoForge 21.1.251, VTP 1.1.1 and GeckoLib 4.9.3. Source review, **not** downloaded exact JAR or game test.
- **Boss is a major system, not a decorative mob.** The Corrupted Champion is Java `LichEntity` with default **400-health spellcasting + 200-health hunting phases** (not 600 single-phase HP); casting damage 30, hunting additional 40, invulnerable attack windows, darkness/fog, up to 30 minions. In the healing spell he **teleports nearby noncreative players to his own Y minus 15**, using a raw `teleportTo` call. Position safety at that exact call is unguarded in source; **test suffocation/terrain bypass and void hazard**, especially at structures/default sky prison Y210, Tectonic high mountains and unusual arena floors. Don't disable the boss or its spells preemptively. Tests also cover spells/Monk/Dragon damage attribution, Hunter categories and first true boss MXP vs minion kills.
- **Unique blood-vial progression:** Blood Dagger requires **eight villager kills** to fill `graveyard:vial_of_blood`; item keeps **progress as a custom data float** and is consumed by the full staff-fragment pattern at the altar at night. This is a *boss summons key*, not an EvilCraft generic blood fluid. **Preserve vial progress/identity** and do not substitute full vials with bulk fluid buckets, just as Sons of Sins typed creature blood is also outside bulk-fluid canonicalization. Test loot/storing/relog/stack transfer, altar pattern, phase summoning and claim permissions.
- **Hordes:** default enabled, up to **40 spawn attempts** after check every ~24,000–25,200 ticks, with **20% chance per check**, night-only, ~24–48 blocks away, avoidance of villages, requiring `doMobSpawning`. Attempts do not guarantee 40 mobs. Could be meaningful early-game challenge and mob tick burden; leave default until observed.
- **Magpie:** picks up one **dropped shiny item**, not player inventory, gated by native `mobGriefing`, `behaviour.magpiesStealItems`, normal treasure item tag. Guaranteed gear drop + persistence, and drops item immediately if damaged. No broad anti-theft configuration justified. Test item components/FTB Chunks item owners, item-despawn edges.
- **Ghouling:** themed ownable combat pet and coffin item collector, not prohibited generic backpacks. Standard source inventory copy/add and ground-item decrement, owner UUID binding across dimension and save/load. Test item conservation with full inventory, relog/death/dimension shifts and claims. Default collection enabled.
- **Wraith:** native light manipulation (candle extinguishing, torch/lantern swaps to soul variants) performs direct block replacements, gated by `mobGriefing` and `behaviour.wraithGriefing`, but **FTB Chunks claim protection needs live test**. Do not disable absent failure. Skeleton Creeper block destruction and Champion skull block destruction are both **disabled by default** and should stay that way initially.
- **Urns:** conventional block-entity item storage (54 slots by source default), contents saved in NBT, drops contents on breaking; not player-grave mechanics or remote logistics. Basic break/relog/claims inventory conservation test.
- **Ambient/worldgen:** graveyards, crypts, haunted house, altar, boss prison default Y210, multiple small variants, five overrun village biomes, decorative blocks and 36-page Chronicle. Test Tectonic+YUNG structures, large overrun village effect on early village/trade access. **Reaper natural spawning is disabled by default**; retain this default, no unreviewed forced override. Nameless Hanged trades at night through VTP; 4.0.1 fixes earlier offers reroll and server stall, verify native trade reload without duplicating stock.
- **Disposition:** retain full Graveyard with native defaults for first comprehensive QA. Nontrivial but *bounded* issues: boss relocation, permission-safe Wraith griefing and owner-bound Ghouling collection, nocturnal horde load, blood-vial key and worldgen overlap. No observed runtime defect and no justification for removal/nerf now.
