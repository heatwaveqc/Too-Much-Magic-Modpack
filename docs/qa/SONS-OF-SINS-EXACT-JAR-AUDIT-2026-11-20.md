## A. Typed blood is real: do not normalize bottles

Observed in `procedures/DamagesHurtProcedure.execute` and `BottleOfBloodSpecialInformationProcedure.execute`:

- A player using an item in tag `sons_of_sins:is_slicer`, carrying a **glass bottle**, can harvest `sons_of_sins:bottle_of_blood` in an entity damage event. Harvested bottle gets a **`DataComponents.CUSTOM_DATA` numeric double** named exactly **`originEntityCapaity`** (preserve developer typo).
- Branch priority: **4** if `EntityTypeTags.AQUATIC` or is native Nibbler; **3** if `#sons_of_sins:is_humanoid` or victim Player; **2** if `#sons_of_sins:is_large`; **1** if `#sons_of_sins:has_wing`; **0** default (primary/other). Native tooltip translations label 4 aquatic, 3 human-like, 2 wide, 1 winged, 0 primary. Inspect `data/sons_of_sins/tags/entity_type/{is_large,is_humanoid,has_wing}.json` and `data/sons_of_sins/tags/item/is_slicer.json` in the binary.
- `data/sons_of_sins/recipe/blood_bucket_recipe.json` is generic **4** bottled blood + **4** Ether Ash + bucket -> `sons_of_sins:blood_bucket`, intentionally disregarding origin categories for this generic recipe. Distinguish crafting a generic blood fluid bucket from replacing a species-specific specimen.
- **Compatibility requirement:** keep the actual native `sons_of_sins:bottle_of_blood` `ItemStack` and `CUSTOM_DATA` unchanged through storage, transfers, recipe conversions, crafting, consumption and UI. EvilCraft canonical **bulk fluid** bridging may operate on native Sons blood bucket/fluid only after a checked recipe/transfer transformation. Do not replace typed specimens with plain EvilCraft blood bottles; a KubeJS recipe with `item ID` alone would conflate 5 body categories. Exact fluid registration includes `BloodFluid` and `BloodFluidType`.
- **Test:** one of each 0..4 blood types, including double-tagged or transformed sources; verify type after stacking, storage/reload, cross-mod transfer, pack conversions and feeding carcass. Verify generic bucket conversion yields **one** bucket while consuming four bottles and preserving their glass-bottle crafting remainders.

## B. Cell Division is bounded in *number of split generations*, but multiplies organ material

Observed in `procedures/GulberEntityDiesProcedure.execute`, and parallel `DevourerEntityDiesProcedure`, `GrubEntityDiesProcedure`, `GuzzlerEntityDiesProcedure`, `NibblerEntityDiesProcedure`:
- A dying flesh creature with `CELL_DIVISION` spawns **exactly two** same-type offspring through `EntityType.spawn(...MOB_SUMMONED)`, sets them as **babies** where supported, attempts to transfer tame owner/target and copies other component organs/appearance slots.
- **Explicit terminal control:** both new offspring have `SLIME_REAR` in FEET replaced with ordinary `RIBS`. That disarms the Slime Rear source of Cell Division; no native automatic exponential 2→4→8 generations via the same organ. Important correction to prior high-risk hypothetical.
- **Subtler resource question:** other organ slots `LEGS` / `CHEST` are inherited by **both** offspring. If these offspring later die normally, non-Cell-Division branch creates an item `FLESH_CARCASS`, fills slots 3/4/5 with their held organs (`ItemStack.copy()`) and slot 9 with a new blood bottle with `originEntityCapaity=0`, then drops the carcass. This can produce **two reclaimable carcasses bearing two copies of the non-Slime organ components** after one parent splits. That is a *code-visible pathway for material multiplication*, potentially by design; inventory UI/extraction and exact resulting counts require runtime verification.
- New offspring do not get `SLIME_REAR` without an external source reinserting that organ. Avoid broad creature-number nerfs; **test rather than ban**. If packs add rare or boss-gated modded organ drops later, this multiplication has progression implications (prevent off-label boss organ copies unless owner wants them). Existing JAR supports a finite set of common vanilla organ types, not an arbitrary import of every modded boss organ.
- **Test:** one adult Gublèr with Slime Rear and two distinct rare organ components; kill, verify exactly two babies and same owner, inspect their FEET/LEGS/CHEST, let mature, kill, recover carcasses and extract contents; ensure deaths/ghost/reloads do not duplicate or drop both vanilla equipment and custom carcass. Repeat for all 5 specialized flesh species.

## C. Equipment disarming is real; native recovery exists; normal-drop duplication remains a test gate

`BetrayedGuzzlerEntityIsHurtProcedure.execute`:
- On qualifying `DamageTypes.PLAYER_ATTACK` or `MOB_ATTACK`, with the Betrayed Guzzler's **MAIN_HAND empty** and attacker's MAIN_HAND an instance of vanilla `SwordItem`, `AxeItem`, or `TridentItem`: copies the attacker's **full `ItemStack` including components**, equips the Guzzler main hand, empties the attacker's MAIN_HAND, and calls `Mob.setPersistenceRequired()`. It does **not** match all arbitrary Ars/Iron's custom base items or spell projectiles.
- `BetrayedGuzzlerEntity.die` calls `Monster.die(source)` **first**, then `BetrayedGuzzlerEntityDiesProcedure.execute`, which **spawns an `ItemEntity` of the current main-hand stack with 10-tick pickup delay and clears the main hand**. Therefore there is an explicit intended recovery path after death.
- **Suspected edge:** because superclass `Monster.die` has already run standard mob death/equipment processing, and the custom procedure then drops that same slot, a normal eligible equipment-drop roll *may* spawn a duplicate before the guaranteed drop. **Not claimed as proven without actual gameplay/native MC 1.21.1 superclass and drop-chance inspection.** Test >100 stolen-gear deaths, count gear before and after, including full enchant/Curios/Attribute/DataComponents, Looting, kill credit, chunk reload, void/death, confiscation by another entity. Test ordinary living death vs entity removal/despawn, plus PersistenceRequired behavior.
- `CursedHeadLivingEntityIsHitWithItemProcedure` selects an occupied random armor slot, directly spawns an `ItemEntity` with that full equipped `ItemStack`, **60-tick pickup delay**, and clears the armor slot. No explicit owner-lock is visible in this path; ordinary item-entity loss/theft/despawn/environment exposure should be tested. The Cursed Head itself is **consumed on hit** for noncreative attacker. No need to abolish the mechanic; potential narrow prevention should target any verified vanish/dupe.
- In related world interaction `CursePlayerCollidesWithThisEntityProcedure`, collision just adds velocity/knockback (not a hidden armor delete).

## D. Entity terrain destruction is governed by `mobGriefing`, but FTB claims still require testing

- `BludOnEntityTickUpdateProcedure`, `ButcherOnEntityTickUpdateProcedure`, `SinOnEntityTickUpdateProcedure`, `WalkingBedOnEntityTickUpdateProcedure`, and `WistiverOnEntityTickUpdateProcedure` all contain calls to `LevelAccessor.destroyBlock(pos,false)` **with explicit checks** of vanilla `GameRules.RULE_MOBGRIEFING`. Wistiver/Blud code checks hardness and/or wither-immune tags in some branches, but **does not exhibit an FTB Chunks claim-check API call** in the inspected paths. This honors native global mobGriefing but is **not proof of safe claimed chunks**.
- `WhenEntityDieProcedure` triggers a `LAST_BREATH` death explosion using `Level$ExplosionInteraction.MOB`, so its terrain effect also depends on normal game explosion/griefing behavior; verify claim protection.
- `WhenEntityDie2Procedure` contains direct `LevelAccessor.setBlock(pos, AIR,...)` when consuming native blood ritual blocks and summoning Ether Ghost. It is *not* automatically secured by general mobGriefing; a ritual/ownership permission test is warranted.
- **Pack policy already established:** mod entities may grief **unclaimed** chunks but may not alter **claimed** chunks without appropriate player/claim permission. Test claimed and unclaimed worlds with mobGriefing both true and false before any compatibility patch; use narrow claim-aware fix if necessary.

## E. Ether progression and organ harvesting: existing intended controls

- `EtherEngineItem` exists as a native item. `PuppetOfStrifeItemInInventoryTickProcedure`, `EtherSwordLivingEntityIsHitWithToolProcedure`, `ShapeShiftingToolItemInHandTickProcedure`, etc., explicitly check `SonsOfSinsModItems.ETHER_ENGINE` in player inventory as an **alternate resource-payment path**. So unlimited Ether support is intentional; no default nerf justified.
- `WhenEntityDieProcedure.onEntityDeath` listens for `LivingDeathEvent`; when killer is credited and wields an `#sons_of_sins:is_osseous` tagged tool, Ether Ash amount is **ceil(victim.max_health × 0.1)**; source requires nonnull attacker entity and mob/PathfinderMob victim. This is coupled to legitimate kill attribution, though modded pet/spawner/mob farms and very-high-health bosses require pack-level XP/economy verification.
- `#sons_of_sins:is_slicer` contains the Sickle of Struggle and Butcher Cleaver; `WhenEntityDieProcedure` obtains organs from `<victim_namespace>:slicing/<victim_name>` loot table, with built-in `data/minecraft/loot_table/slicing/*.json` covering Blaze, Cave Spider, Creeper, Enderman, Iron Golem, Ravager, Slime, Snow Golem, Spider and Strider. When no applicable slicing loot is found, the native procedure can give basic anatomy instead. **Do not add arbitrary named mod-boss organ tables until clone material multiplication is assessed.**
- Gamelimit `jawsDamageLimit` is registered with default **666** through `SonsOfSinsModGameRules`. Damage/power for Wistiver's Jaws has a native server-side game rule cap (verify exact semantics after integrated test), so do not assert unbounded growth.
- Native material recipes, Sin relics, Essence Ghost ritual flows and Sinful Church loot/structure are present and should remain intact for first test. Normal EMC/replication policies still apply, but no inherent reason to disable unlimited Ether Engine.
