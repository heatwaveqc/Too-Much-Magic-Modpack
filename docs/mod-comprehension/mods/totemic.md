## Player-facing identity and value

Totemic centers on carved wooden poles, passive local buffs and timed musical ceremonies. It uses neither a common mana pool nor FE. Playing several different instrument types before a deadline powers ceremonies; instrument-specific caps prevent replacing the orchestra with unlimited copies of one instrument. Carvings work passively outside ceremony states, and a charged Medicine Bag can carry one eligible carving away from the pole.

Five standout capabilities are configurable local carving buffs, timed growth/animal ceremonies, renewable Buffalo and Bald Eagle material chains, portable charged effects, and the summonable Baykok boss and bow. Early practical unlocks are a carved mining/speed pole, War Dance's combat buffs and Fertility's cedar saplings. Candidate integrations are tagged crop growth, Weakness-backed mob cleansing and charged portable utility. These are source-supported native capabilities; cross-mod targets and loaded-pack behavior need testing.

## Totem construction, carvings and passive economics

Craft `totemic:totem_whittling_knife` using pattern `  I/ SF/S  `, I=iron ingot tag, F=Flint, S=wooden rod tag (two rods). It has 250 durability. Sneak right-click in air or sneak-scroll cycles the knife's selected carving; no carving component means Base mode. Stop sneaking and right-click a burnable log to replace it with the selected base/pole, costing one tool durability for a player. Nine native wood types are oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, cherry, dark oak, mangrove and cedar; other `#minecraft:logs_that_burn` logs fall back to oak appearance. No separate base or pole crafting recipe is required. Disabled-carving config rejects carving.

Place/carve a base at the bottom and up to **five contiguous carved poles directly above**, six blocks total. A gap or nonpole ends scanning; additional height does not contribute. Wood is appearance, not a tier/fuel gate. The base can select ceremonies without a minimum carving count in the state-machine implementation. Carvings are stored on each pole. Pick-block constructs an appearance/component-bearing pole item, but the bundled base/pole block loot tables have no pools: do not promise survival break-and-recover behavior. Carve replacement logs when relocating.

`none` is the empty registry carving. Ordinary potion effects refresh every 80 ticks for 100 ticks; Night Vision lasts 290. Scaling amplifier is integer `(repetition−1)/2 + (passiveMusic>=5760 ? 1 : 0)`; displayed level is amplifier+1. Repetition counts identical effect instances on one pole, up to five contributing pieces; separate poles apply normal potion resolution, not additive amplifier sums. Player effects exclude spectators. Default range is `5 + passiveMusic/1920 + (five contributing poles ? 1 : 0)` in a block-centered AABB: 5–10 under native bounds, not a sphere. Passive music caps at 7680 and every 20 ticks drains `clamp(music/96,10,60)` while positive. Passive effects need no music to operate; music increases range and selected amplifiers. During selection/startup/effect, passive ticking pauses.

Spider effect every tick when horizontal collision sets fall distance zero, clamps X/Z to ±0.15 and Y to 0.2, or zero when suppressing ladder sliding. Ocelot is periodic fuse suppression, not a universal explosion-cancellation guarantee.

## Medicine Bag exact contract

One carving component per bag. Sneak-use on a carved pole copies its carving without consuming the pole; Ocelot fails portability and server `medicineBagBlacklist` can reject others. Changing selection resets ordinary bag charge to zero. Right-click toggles open/closed once selected. Inventory ticking charges even closed: every 20 ticks, matching carving on a passive base within block scan range 6 adds 400 up to 4800. It does not consume the pole's stored music. Open charged bags apply effects on each effect interval at amplifier zero, then drain default 80 every 80 ticks: approximately 4800 ticks / four minutes from full if continuously open away from charging, with phase/lingering effects affecting endpoints. Closed stops application/drain and preserves charge; removing from ticking player inventory stops active refresh, while prior potion can linger. No Curios equip requirement or slot GUI exists. Multiple bags independently tick; identical potions resolve by normal potion rules. Native body casts holder to Player; do not claim arbitrary mob inventory support. Creative bag has no survival recipe and is not a progression reward.

## Renewable materials, Baykok and support content

Buffalo inherits Cow food/breeding behavior, explicitly tempts with Wheat, has 25 health and breeds Buffalo offspring. Adult native loot rolls hide 0–4, tooth 0–2, meat 1–4, with Looting adjustments; fire/smelting-loot equipment can cook meat. No tooth guarantee per kill. Bald Eagle has 10 health, salmon breeding, fish taming; offspring inherits tamed parent's owner. Adult loot supplies bone 1–2 and feather 1–3 before Looting. Maintain renewable pairs rather than consuming all conversion targets.

Baykok has 200 health, movement speed 0.4, follow range 40 and bow-attack goal interval parameter 12; projectile speed 2–3 based on range and difficulty-adjusted accuracy/damage. Its equipped Baykok Bow is marked guaranteed-drop in equipment initialization (not listed in its JSON loot). It awards native XP reward 65; ordinary loot gives bones 2–8, rotten flesh 0–2, arrows 3–10 and a skull alternative (Wither Skeleton Skull base chance 25%, otherwise Skeleton Skull), with enchantment modifiers. It does not ordinarily despawn for inactivity; switching Peaceful discards it. Bow durability 576; normal untipped arrow entity with nonpositive color becomes Invisible Arrow, copying pickup origin, weapon, owner and base damage. It is not native infinite ammunition. Invisible Arrow adds Slowness II 20 ticks only when its owner is Baykok, not every player bow shot.

Tipi requires sturdy dirt-tag ground, replaceable 3×3×3 space at its bottom and center column for the next three blocks, within world height. Sleeping additionally needs sky visibility above base+6 and normal bed conditions. It acts as a bed/respawn point; in dimensions where beds cannot set spawn, use destroys it and causes strength-5 bad-respawn explosion. Totem Torch is light/decorative TorchBlock behavior; do not infer a magical fuel network. Cedar has the ordinary wood building/crafting family; exact 31 recipe table includes all variants and cooking routes. Spawn eggs, Ceremony Cheat and Creative Medicine Bag have no native survival progression recipes and are excluded from normal walkthrough/rewards.

## Configuration, APIs and current pack behavior

Server defaults: disabledCeremonies, disabledTotemCarvings and medicineBagBlacklist are empty; startup multipliers Easy/Peaceful 1.1, Normal 1, Hard 0.875. Startup customTotemWoodTypes can register log-tag/color appearances; client HUD offsets default X=0/Y=−70. These are native defaults, not a claim about an existing world's config. Read-only `rg` sweep of this checkout's config/defaultconfigs/kubejs found only Totemic quest placeholders, no Totemic overrides. No config, script or quest is changed by research.

Pinned KubeJS plugin registers default builders for instrument/carving/ceremony registries and bindings `TotemicAPI`, `TotemEffect`. Instrument builder requires baseOutput/musicMaximum; displayItem is display-only and does not make an item play music. Carving builder accepts effects/potions and Medicine Bag drain; ceremony builder requires music target, startup time, two selectors and effect callback, optional effectDuration (zero executes once on both sides). Registration of content and executable callbacks requires future authorized engine/API ownership review; this document creates none.

`TotemicEvents` has startup modifyMusicInstruments/modifyTotemCarvings/modifyCeremonies; server ceremonySelection, ceremonyStartupFail, ceremonyStartupSuccess; common ceremonyStartupTick/ceremonyEffectTick, targeted by ceremony key. Selection/startup/effect result handlers can change/cancel native behavior. Mod setup registers event handling and posts modification events only when KubeJS is loaded, then builds selector map. Native APIs/capability expose music acceptance and ceremony events, not a generic FE/item logistics interface. A loaded custom script can change the documented defaults; runtime plugin operation is untested.
