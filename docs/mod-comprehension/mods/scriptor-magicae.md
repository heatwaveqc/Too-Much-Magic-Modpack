## Executive gameplay map

**What the player actually does:** obtain one of four tiers of Ancient Tome from injected loot or the Scriptor villager, then use it to reveal a save-specific spellbook. Compare spellbooks to learn how that world’s words and sentence structure work. Craft a Dictionary (the item the in-game help calls a Scriptionary), open its Guide screen, and use a Writing Table to record definitions or author spellbooks and scrolls. Spellbooks and scrolls trigger spells from saved text; verbal casting can test phrases without preparing an item but carries hunger, health and cooldown costs. Lecterns automate one stored book, crystals retarget it, and Engravings provide a separate written trigger path. The four tome tiers are random discovery tiers, not a linear crafting ladder; tier advancements track learning all entries within each tier, not merely owning one of each tome.

**Why it matters in TMM:** Scriptor is a standalone utility-magic system driven by a world-generated vocabulary. It can turn ordinary smelting recipes into spell effects, automate them with a Lectern, route casts through item/entity/coordinate targets, and supply healing, movement, block, weather, time, combat and bound-tool actions. The pack does not include the optional `scriptor_mana` integration; default player casting is cooldown-based, not Ars Source or another pack mana pool. Several configured spell-cost ceilings are not enforced by the pinned item-casting code, so do not teach them as effective gates.

**Quick start:** get any Ancient Tome from early tier-1 loot or buy one from a Nominomist, then right-click it to reveal a spellbook. Craft `scriptor:dictionary` from 3 leather, 3 paper, 1 ink sac, 1 gold ingot and 1 feather; right-click it and choose **Guide** for the embedded first-use help. Make a Writing Table from 3 wooden slabs, a glass bottle, paper and purple wool. Compare or inspect spellbooks, save definitions in the Dictionary and use it with the table to write a low-risk spell. Identify Scrolls are registered but have no normal survival recipe/loot/trade route in this pin. [pin] [jar: SHA512] [source: `v2.0.14-1.21.1`]

The Guide screen loads bundled Unfocused Markdown; its default entries are **Verbal Casting**, **Tomes and Spellbooks**, and **Writing Tomes**, with additional notes unlocked as the player encounters Scriptor items. [jar: SHA512] [source: Dictionary.kt, DictionaryScreen.kt]

[jar: SHA512] [source: `Tomes.kt`, `AncientSpellbook.kt`, `ScriptorTrades.kt`]

### Acquisition, renewability and output economics

- Four tome tiers are discovery pools with 47 bundled definitions total (12 / 16 / 14 / 5). They are not a sequential crafting ladder. Tier-specific loot and trades offer different ways to discover entries; exact world frequency depends on generating the relevant loot tables and is not measured here.
- T1 tome purchase from a Nominomist is renewable through village trading, subject to the trade's finite restock/use count. A villager house is injected into five vanilla village pools, but no individual village is guaranteed to contain one. Higher-tier conversion trades exchange one random tome for two tomes one tier lower. The Hero of the Village librarian gift is a separate T3 route.
- Identify Scroll is present as an item and guide topic but its intended loot injection and villager trade are commented out in this exact pin; no normal survival recipe or alternate source was found. Do not use it as a progression requirement.
- Inflame uses ordinary vanilla smelting recipes and, in the inspected path, consumes the first ingredient's declared count per strength iteration and emits one recipe result per iteration. It uses no fuel, FE, Terra, Botania mana or Source. The source helper does not establish throughput, remainder handling, multi-output parity or overflow behavior; measure these before treating it as an economy route.
- Generated spellbooks are world- and player-specific discoveries. A tome is consumed on use outside creative; once discovered, the player's remaining pool changes. No player-built renewable tome generator was identified; the repeatable T1 trade is the clearest replenishment route.

### Dependency graph and alternate routes

`Dictionary/Guide → obtain and use tome → compare generated spellbooks → record words (optional but recommended) → author or copy a book/scroll → cast → optionally automate with Lectern or trigger through Engraving`.

The Dictionary can be crafted before finding a tome, so reference access is not gated by spell discovery. A tome-derived book is immediately usable without knowing every word; vocabulary analysis matters when the player wants to author new phrases. Books are reusable and use cooldowns; scrolls are consumed when their use succeeds and have a shorter configured cooldown multiplier, not stronger spell effects by definition. Verbal casting avoids an item but has its own cooldown, hunger and overload-damage behavior. Tier advancement triggers track full per-player discovery of the corresponding shipped tier pool; they do not represent mere item ownership. No pack recipe, Scriptor mana addon, or shared mana bridge was found.

## Low-hanging setups useful in ordinary survival

### 1. Safe utility spellbook — early
- **Goal and payoff:** repeat a self/utility spell without dangerous verbal-cast costs.
- **Minimum entry and ingredients:** any authored tome spellbook; written book + Spellbook Binder if recopying.
- **Build/use steps:** reveal tome; identify or compare the spell; cast once in a safe area; use spellbook for repeat casts.
- **Sustainability and limits:** reusable; cooldown applies; the configured spellbook max-cost field is not enforced by the pinned helper; no fixed language recipe.
- **Evidence / pack test:** source/docs; in-pack cost and effect untested.

### 2. Inflame smelting lectern — early-to-mid, subject to test
- **Goal and payoff:** automate smelting of ordinary furnace recipes without fuel.
- **Minimum entry and ingredients:** book containing Inflame, Casting Lectern recipe, suitable target container and smeltable input.
- **Build/use steps:** place lectern facing the intended target; insert book; configure a target inventory using the helper-supported adjacent targeting; ensure redstone is off; test with one low-value item.
- **Sustainability and limits:** no fuel/mana cost per cast; Lectern cost gate and cooldown limit speed; chest transfer exactness and overflow are not yet verified in pack.
- **Evidence / pack test:** Inflame item conversion + lectern inventory target are source-backed; no in-world test.

### 3. Crystal-focused spell station — mid
- **Goal and payoff:** cast a spell repeatedly at a player, coordinate, nearby mob/animal or item focus.
- **Minimum entry and ingredients:** lectern, spellbook, Casting Crystal and relevant tag (player/coordinate/mob/animal).
- **Build/use steps:** craft crystal from amethyst/redstone base and desired tag; configure coordinate slots if relevant; put it in focus slot; remain within 16 blocks; stop with redstone.
- **Sustainability and limits:** rotating coordinate crystal stores four targets; same-dimension and range checks apply; exact cycle order and mob filters need client test.
- **Evidence / pack test:** source/doc; target selection not tested.
