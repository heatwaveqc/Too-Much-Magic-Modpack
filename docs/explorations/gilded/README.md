## Current owner-approved mechanics — separate Looting/Fortune, lava immunity (2026-11-16)

**Both virtual enchantments start at +3 at Gilded initiation and rise to +10 via different purchased Tyz skills.** \`tmm_gilded_fortune_mastery\` affects gold/Netherite mining tools; \`tmm_gilded_looting_mastery\` affects gold swords and axes for direct melee kills. Each skill has seven purchased Tyz ranks (one SP each), and increases only its own effect. Existing physical enchantments remain fully additive and uncapped by this design; Silk Touch wins against Fortune. The two Tyz skills use the existing one-wallet SP pool, not the native Gilded gold-mining XP progression.

**Molten Sovereignty is now implemented in source at native Gilded rank 18**, with \`tmm_progression:lava_immunity\`. It cancels direct lava damage and fire-tick damage while the Gilded is physically in lava; ignition is cleared while in lava. Ordinary fire away from lava still burns; this is **not** universal fire immunity. This choice is high-level and leaves rank 20 without a native capstone.

**Lava fishing** is only a researched proposal, **not** added to the mod manifest or an XP source yet. See [lava-fishing feasibility](LAVA-FISHING-FEASIBILITY.md). We have a NeoForge 1.21.1 candidate; class-exclusive catch access and XP credit need Aquaculture-specific validation.

## Owner-locked golden initiation and food extension (2026-11-16)

**Required initiation:** In `minecraft:the_nether`, wear **all four ordinary golden armor pieces** in their actual head/chest/legs/feet slots and *successfully finish eating* one of `minecraft:golden_apple`, `minecraft:enchanted_golden_apple`, or `minecraft:golden_carrot`. This triggers Gilded enrollment once, on the server. A golden carrot requires the usual hunger preconditions; enchanted golden apples can be eaten when full. No Piglin barter, extra altar, XP precondition or outside-Nether initiation. If a mod adds an enchanted golden carrot, confirm its actual registered ID and add it to the rite tag (none in vanilla).

**Diet = Nether food OR any legitimate gold food.** The Gilded restriction allows (a) explicit Nether-attainable food types from the existing tag, **or** (b) explicit golden food types, **or** (c) any food whose **crafting** recipe has a gold nugget/ingot/block ingredient, including gold-equivalent ingredient tags and nested golden food ingredients in a recipe chain. This gold exception applies even when other recipe ingredients originate outside the Nether. Do not derive membership merely from the word “gold” in an item name or mod namespace. Enchanted golden apples qualify explicitly even though uncraftable in modern vanilla.

The inactive prototype now includes:
- `gilded_initiation.json`: ritual conditions and a proposed successful-eating adapter contract.
- `data/tmm_progression/tags/item/gilded_rite_foods.json`: vanilla golden apple, enchanted golden apple and golden carrot; verified modded enchanted variants may be added here.
- `data/tmm_progression/tags/item/gilded_gold_foods.json`: explicit golden-food whitelist that is incorporated by the Nether-food tag.
- `gilded_food_policy.json`: proposed data-driven server recipe classifier for additional gold-derived crafted foods.

**Technical safety:** The existing `PathJson` only understands enrollment markers `admin`, `manual` and `sunset`. To prevent non-operator players bypassing this rite through `/tmmprogress enroll`, the prototype uses **`enrollment: "admin"` as a compatibility flag**. Actual players enroll by a **new trusted adapter calling `ProgressionApi.join(player,"tmm_progression:gilded")` only after the successful-use conditions**. Operator `admin join` remains for QA. The new ritual and food-policy JSON files are **data contracts only** and are NOT yet read by the engine. Enroll on successful **finish**, not click/use-start; verify item actually consumed and prevent repeated enrollment feedback or granting XP on the rite.

**Food enforcement:** Existing `ProgressionEvents.consumedFood` fires at `LivingEntityUseItemEvent.Finish`, too late to reliably cancel food effects. Implement a separate pre-consumption validation/cancellation hook for **already-Gilded** characters; ensure the rite remains accessible before enrollment. Validate both direct item tag entries and recipe-derived gold foods on the dedicated server, updating cache on recipe datapack reload. The discovered recipe ingredient tags must exist in the actual pack registry or be treated as empty, not cause parser failure. An empty or self-referential recipe tree must not grant a food category. Preserve unrelated potions, healing and non-food consumables.

**Live QA:** consume each of all three vanilla rite items in Nether with full gold armor => joins once; any missing piece, wrong armor material, Overworld/End, failed/canceled eating, creative/spectator, and attempts to run `/tmmprogress enroll` => fail. Verify armor swapping mid-eat, second-hand item, multiplayer, relog and named/gold-looking non-gold foods. Food checks: golden apples/carrots, gold-nugget recipe outputs, recipes using golden carrots, enchanted golden apple, all previously approved Nether foods, non-gold Overworld foods denied; **2× hunger and saturation points** for qualified gold foods without repeated potion effects, 1× for non-gold Nether foods, all subject to vanilla saturation/food caps. Modded enchanted carrot only if actually registered.

## Re-review: Ars enchantment hooks, native Piglin AI, and Gilded golden-food double nutrition (2026-11-16)

**Owner-locked addition:** While already enrolled in Gilded, **all qualified golden/gold-crafted foods** restore **2× normal hunger and 2× normal saturation gained** (ordinary vanilla food-bar/saturation caps still apply). Qualifiers are the explicit gold-food tag, including enchanted golden apples, or a valid gold-containing crafting recipe including nested golden-food ingredients; the *other* Nether foods remain at 1×. No second portion consumed and no duplication of special potion effects or achievements. The ritual item eaten *before* becoming Gilded receives ordinary values; later golden meals receive the perk.

**Food implementation is simpler than previously rated.** The pack already authors Dragon diets through `kubejs/data/dragonsurvival/data_maps/dragonsurvival/dragon_species/diet_entries.json` (native Dragon Survival species-specific override) and `kubejs/server_scripts/dragon_diets.js` (dynamic item tags). These are reusable patterns, **not a directly installable Gilded diet**, because the Gilded player path is not a Dragon Survival species. NeoForge `LivingEntityUseItemEvent.Start` and `Tick` are cancellable, letting a joined-player rule stop disallowed food early; inspect special instant food, bowls, serving blocks and client parity. At successful `Finish`, `ProgressionEvents.consumedFood` already receives the original stack. The proposed minimal player-only bonus is exactly one additional `player.getFoodData().eat(original FoodProperties)` for gold-qualified foods: its base nutrition and saturation are added a second time with normal caps, *without running potion effects a second time*. Alternatively KubeJS 1.21.1 offers `ItemEvents.foodEaten` and `player.addFood`, but the existing Java progression event provides a clearer owner-aware hook and single-award bookkeeping. **Do not change global food components through KubeJS `ItemEvents.modification`.** Critically, food saturation **points** double, but the saturation modifier must **NOT** double along with nutrition: that would quadruple effective saturation before capping.

**Exact pack-pinned Ars Nouveau source**: `baileyholl/Ars-Nouveau@4d323fba9200c3293a4f4848cf1e0fe70beb7098` is `ars_nouveau-1.21.1-5.13.5` per `docs/mod-comprehension/mods/ars-nouveau.md`. Its `LootingPerk.java` registers the Thread of the Drygmy as a carried attribute, and its `common/mixin/looting/EnchantedCountIncreaseFunctionMixin.java` and `LootItemRandomChanceWithEnchantedBonusConditionMixin.java` **wrap the native loot function's existing enchantment-level lookup** and add virtual Looting without adding another loot roll. The Drygmy familiar has a TODO for an older Looting event and supplies a potion effect; **it is not a demonstrated virtual Fortune example**. This is a strong, version-aligned reference pattern for modifying **Fortune** in `ApplyBonusCount.run`, based on actual block `LootContextParams.THIS_ENTITY` and `TOOL`. Use the context to require the actor is a joined Gilded and the actual active tool is gold/Netherite; add virtual +1–3 to actual Fortune exactly once, preserving Silk Touch. Do not inject loot duplication. NeoForge 1.21.1 also has `GetEnchantmentLevelEvent` and `IItemStackExtension#getEnchantmentLevel`; this may be a cleaner shared virtual-enchantment primitive **if** exact actor ownership can be passed or scoped. The raw event supplies **ItemStack, not player**, so a naïve unconditional listener would buff dropped, traded or machine-used gear. Check exact use sites before selecting it; simple item equality alone does not establish valid action context. For Unbreaking, also verify player/equipment context at actual durability wear, not merely the stack's enchant value.

**Piglin/Brute AI is also more tractable.** In Minecraft/NeoForge 1.21.1, the passive sensor checks whether a player lacks gold armor, guarded-block and container alarms invoke `PiglinAi.angerNearbyPiglins(Player,boolean)`, and the Brute logic has `PiglinBruteAi.findNearestValidAttackTarget`. For **alarm causes**, a narrow cancellable Mixin at `angerNearbyPiglins` when the triggering player is joined Gilded prevents anger **before it starts**, without wiping other Piglins' memories. For passive targeting by either ordinary Piglins or Brutes, existing `compat/progression/.../ClassAbilities.targetChange` already handles `faction_neutrality` with a 600-tick (30-second) direct-attack retaliation marker; reuse its `LivingChangeTargetEvent` pathway for Gilded entity selectors, with the same exception semantics. Avoid suppressing group retaliation on a Gilded-initiated attack; inspect broadcast anger pathways in the exact runtime. If target-change filtering is too late for a sensor-memory edge, add a targeted sensor eligibility filter, **not a periodic anger-memory cleaner**.

**Revised engineering risk:** virtual Fortune = **medium** (direct precedent; principal risk ownership context and modded loot interoperability); ordinary Piglin + Brute AI = **low/medium** (known cause and target seams; retaliation/regression tests); Gilded food restriction and golden-food bonus = **low/medium** (existing species-tag pattern + per-player event; hard cases are dynamic recipes/served food); use **medium** for Unbreaking until equipped-item/durability context is source-checked. None of these is implemented by adding this note.

## Source implementation handoff (2026-11-16)

- **Wired:** `runtime/GildedRules.java`, `ProgressionMod` registration, `ProgressionEvents` enrollment/XP hooks, `ClassAbilities` retaliation accessor, resource path/tags.
- **Gold weapon Looting:** golden swords and axes gain virtual +1/+2/+3 Looting at ranks 3/7/11, additive to stored enchantments and limited to direct melee mob kills. `GildedLootingMixin` and `GildedRareLootMixin` follow the pack-pinned Ars Nouveau Drygmy loot-function wrapping pattern; compatibility with simultaneous Drygmy perks must be tested live.
- **Fortune:** `GildedFortuneMixin` adjusts the existing loot formula's level argument; no duplicated rolls. Validate the exact actual method injection in a disposable client and overlevel enchantment stacking.
- **Unbreaking:** `GildedDurabilityMixin` prefilters damage for expected combined effective level; `GildedWearMath` has isolated probability tests. Verify both armor and actively held tool call paths at runtime; the compiler cannot establish that every wear event reaches this overload.
- **Gold equipment:** transient armor/toughness attribute differences, direct melee bonus and vanilla diamond-block harvest check. Gold's underlying item properties stay unchanged. Verify the effective pickaxe's speed/drops together, and confirm compatibility with any modded melee damage hooks.
- **Piglins:** narrow suppression at `PiglinAi.angerNearbyPiglins`, wearing-gold equivalent for Gilded, targeted Brute selection filter and existing retaliation event. Test not just first target selection but ongoing Brain memories and multi-player broadcast anger.
- **Food:** pre-use denial, successful-consumption ritual, golden food recipe recognition and second `FoodData.eat` call for 2× hunger/saturation gains only. Modded serving/feast pathways need in-world checks.
- **Repair:** the existing gear handler is now configured as separate `armor` and `mainhand` effects rather than `all`, excluding offhand-only gear. Gold and Netherite retain identical repair rates.
- **XP:** uses `BlockDropsEvent.getTool()` to verify Silk Touch from the real breaking tool, awarding one Gilded event per actual player gold-ore break rather than one per generated drop.
- **Not yet included:** My Nether's Delight has an identified NeoForge 1.21.1 release but is not hash-pinned in the mod manifest. Preserve the existing full-integration candidate unchanged until an explicit build request.

## Required Java implementation before activation

The JSON was originally drafted against the existing progression parser for `activities`, `milestones`, `skills`, `auto_ranks` and the real `gear_durability` handler. **Historical prerequisite (NOW ADDRESSED in source): five Gilded-specific effect names previously were not registered**: `tmm_progression:food_whitelist`, `tmm_progression:piglin_affinity`, `tmm_progression:gold_diamond_equivalence`, `tmm_progression:virtual_fortune`, `tmm_progression:virtual_unbreaking`. The current experimental runtime registers the handlers before path reload; the active development path was copied into `src/main/resources/data/`. The original statement that the parser would reject the path is obsolete. The handler parameters are *proposed contracts*, not a promise that Java already reads them. Do not stub the handler to no-op merely to pass the parser.

- `food_whitelist` must cancel/deny unauthorized **food use before the effects or hunger/saturation apply** (including instant food, long food-use interactions, modded bowls and placed feasts). Nonfood potions/medicines should be unaffected; must not disable all consumables. No automatic blacklist of another mod's valid Nether-only foods—explicitly add to tag after verifying ingredients.
- `piglin_affinity` must prevent unprovoked anger *initiation* from mining gold/opening/breaking protected storage, and neutralize ordinary targeting for the specific enrolled player by **Piglins and Brutes**. Do not wipe or reset existing anger/other-player targets; retaliation after player attack remains valid.
- `gold_diamond_equivalence` must reflect equivalent damage, harvest tier, armor points/toughness for **worn/wielded gold** without changing persistent item components or lowering gold's vanilla fast mining speed.
- `virtual_fortune` starts at **+3 additive virtual Fortune at initiation**, then seven purchased Tyz skill ranks increase it by one each to **+10**. Stored enchantments are still fully additive, Silk Touch wins, no second loot roll or item NBT mutation.
- `virtual_unbreaking` adds +1 effective level per unlocked rank (three ranks, **+3 at rank 12** as a provisional proposed final value) without changing stack enchantments; exact bonus magnitude remains open.
- `gear_durability` is the existing registered effect; both gold and Netherite share one tag and **the exact same** 1, 2 or 3 durability restored per five ticks at path levels 1, 10 and 18. These rates and unlock levels are prototype values, not owner-locked. Armor and active mainhand qualify; verify whether the existing `slot:all` behavior permits offhand parked equipment and narrow it if necessary.
- The `external` activity `tmm_progression:gilded_gold_ore_broken_without_silk` can earn XP **repeatedly** via `ProgressionApi.recordExternal`, but a new trusted block-break adapter must emit the signal **once per successful player-originated gold ore break when the actual tool does not have Silk Touch**. Use the exact verified ore tag. Do not award from raw drop count, pre-canceled breaks, recipes, ordinary gold blocks, general Nether activities or automation. No extra non-owner-approved restriction on placed/purchased ore is built into this prototype.
