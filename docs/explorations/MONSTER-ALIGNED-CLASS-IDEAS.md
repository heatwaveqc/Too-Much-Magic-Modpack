# Monster-aligned progression classes — filtered concepts (NOT approved)

**Updated:** 2026-11-15. **Scope:** exploratory design only, no runtime registration/skill JSON/mod installation.

Source: the project's 16 authoritative Hunter classification families in `research/hunter-creature-inventory-2026-11-15/docs/hunter/CREATURE-TAXONOMY.md`. Those families remain Hunter classifications whether or not they justify a player class. This file **replaces** the earlier one-to-one list of sixteen classes with a conservative ten-candidate shortlist.

## Mandatory admission tests

A player-aligned progression class is admitted only when **all three** conditions hold:

1. **Recognizable identity** in popular fiction, fantasy folklore, or established Minecraft lore; lore must drive mechanics, rather than provide a name for generic passive buffs.
2. **A whole playstyle**, with a repeatable source of class progression, at least three interlocking and substantially distinct abilities, and a limitation, resource, risk, or condition.
3. **Nonredundant** with active player species/forms (JAWR Vampire, Lich and Werewolf, existing Dragon forms), established magic schools, Monk/Hunter, and the other proposed classes. A taxonomy tag is NOT by itself a reason to add a class.

There is no target quota. The current 10 are *design candidates*, not owner-approved classes. Do not claim that creature membership automatically causes faction neutrality, boss affinity, or transformation. The Hunter taxonomy is independent of these proposed player identities.

## Retain for concept development (10/16)

### 1. Piglin — Gilded (LOCKED name, exploratory mechanics)

**Lore:** Piglin gold fixation, bartering and Bastions; native Piglin and Piglin Brute identity.  
**Loop:** Successful non-Silk-Touch gold ore breaks award class XP, regardless of Fortune yield; become a capable gold specialist, then use Netherite.  
**Mechanics:** Golden equipment functions at diamond-equivalent tool/armor/combat levels; +3 virtual Fortune at initiation, growing to +10 with seven Tyz purchases, on held gold/Netherite mining tools; gold and Netherite equipment gain effective Unbreaking and identical rapid equipped repair; ordinary Piglins and Brutes do not initiate hostility to unprovoking Gilded, even during gold/container alarm actions.  
**Distinctive constraint:** Eating is limited to Nether-attainable food types **or** golden/gold-crafted foods (including food with gold in its crafting recipe). Initiation is completed by successfully eating a golden apple (enchanted or ordinary) or golden carrot in the Nether while wearing four golden armor pieces. Requires pre-consumption food validation and recipe-aware eligibility, plus My Nether's Delight recipe auditing.  
**Differentiation:** **Equipment economy and faction customs**, not Nether fire magic, a generic mob disguise, or Werewolf-style body transformation. See [Gilded specification](PIGLIN-GOLD-PROGRESSION.md).

### 2. Illager — Raidmarshal (new candidate)

**Lore:** Pillager patrols, captains and banners, Evoker fangs/vexes, Ravagers and woodland mansions; *Minecraft Dungeons* Arch-Illager.  
**Loop:** Earn class reputation from legitimately defeating/organizing raid encounters, securing outposts and practicing crossbow/axe command techniques, with anti-farm and village-grief protections.  
**Mechanics:** (1) Banner-command stances that enhance a *limited* allied contingent; (2) crossbow volley/reload specializations; (3) limited Evoker-style fangs or vex conjurations, balanced with separate summon caps; (4) raid-alarm/intimidation, tactical control and a late Ravager-linked signature ability.  
**Cost:** Villager distrust/trade penalties, upkeep/limited command points, and danger from village guards; do not enable faction hostility toward unprovoking ordinary villagers globally.  
**Why not redundant:** **Raid command and social allegiance**, not Hunter's anti-Illager damage skill or a generic spellcaster.

### 3. Void — Enderbound (new candidate)

**Lore:** Endermen, ender pearls, chorus fruit, Endermites, carried blocks and End teleportation. Ender Dragon is also Dragon+Void but must retain boss rules.  
**Loop:** Successfully use/risk Ender pearls, discover End structures, and perform real chorus-fruit/teleport navigation feats (not spam-clicks); later earn safe anchor/return capabilities.  
**Mechanics:** (1) improved aimed pearl landing, safe landing/return anchors and recovery of failed throws; (2) short-range phase repositioning with pearl/energy costs; (3) Enderman-style block carrying or retrieving under explicit protection rules; (4) chorus-fruit displacement/risk control and Ender perception.  
**Cost:** Water sensitivity or resource-dependent teleports, cooldowns and unsafe-arrival constraints; no free generic cross-dimension teleport.  
**Why not redundant:** **Pearl/chorus logistics and spatial navigation as an economy**, not Monk's single Blink power, generic Psi spells, or a Dragon form.

### 4. Sculk — Deep Listener (new candidate)

**Lore:** Sculk sensors, shriekers, catalysts, Ancient Cities and the Warden's vibration-based tracking.  
**Loop:** Learn to identify, intercept and emit vibrations; gather safe sculk-charge milestones from real catalyst activity and Deep Dark exploration; never simply mass-trigger a Warden for XP.  
**Mechanics:** (1) vibration radar that locates motion through occlusion; (2) silence steps that reduce the player's own vibrations; (3) controlled redirection of vibrations to bait or protect areas; (4) late sonic pulse consuming sculk charge, with strict range and cooldown.  
**Cost:** Charge is tied to sculk-related events/environment, stealth competes with active attack vibration output, and noise may make Warden encounters more dangerous.  
**Why not redundant:** **Vibration/stealth information warfare** absent from existing Monk/Hunter/elemental magic. Avoid unconditional Warden pacification.

### 5. Aquatic — Conduit Keeper (new candidate)

**Lore:** Conduits, Ocean Monuments, Guardians/Elder Guardians, prismarine, tridents and Riptide.  
**Loop:** Explore submerged structures, gain conduit-frame/proximity milestones, collect prismarine, master water navigation and trident usage.  
**Mechanics:** (1) conduit aura-building and radius upgrades while immersed/in range; (2) currents/water movement and trident mastery; (3) guardian-style defensive beam/pulse on a charge; (4) underwater mining/rescue and control of Mining Fatigue at late tier.  
**Cost:** Most high-tier techniques require water or proximity to a built conduit structure; little benefit in dry land.  
**Why not redundant:** **Water-infrastructure specialization and conduit territory**, not Sea Dragon's form, breath or affiliation. Water Breathing alone is not a class.

### 6. Construct — Golembound (new candidate)

**Lore:** Minecraft iron/snow golems made from blocks and a carved pumpkin; golem repair using iron ingots; classic golem folklore and automata.  
**Loop:** Repair and protect real golems, harvest/craft verified golem components, and build limited guardian stations without infinite free spawn loops.  
**Mechanics:** (1) modular material sockets in a player construct chassis (iron/copper/stone roles, not all stacked); (2) deliberate iron-material self-repair and durability/tank stance; (3) command up to a capped small number of player-owned golems; (4) temporary siege/guard mode with immunity tradeoffs.  
**Cost:** Low mobility/maintenance resources, restricted module slots, costly companion upkeep.  
**Why not redundant:** **Material assembly, servitor control and protective stations**, not Gilded's gold gear restoration or generic technology machines.

### 7. Arthropod — Webspinner (new candidate)

**Lore:** Minecraft spider/cave spider walls and venom, cobwebs and abandoned mineshafts; popular spider-person and drider fiction.  
**Loop:** Produce/use web constructs and venom, traverse hazardous vertical routes, survive mineshaft challenges, train with spider-type materials.  
**Mechanics:** (1) climbing walls/ceilings; (2) finite web deployment, web swinging or anchors; (3) poison application and venom mitigation; (4) web sensing and immobilization.  
**Cost:** Web resource/charge and cooldowns; vulnerable to fire or slow in unsuitable terrain; must not allow permanent unbreakable web griefing.  
**Why not redundant:** **Vertical traversal and persistent web terrain**, not generic Beast speed, Monk leaping or Plant growth.

### 8. Plant — Verdant (new candidate, botanical Creeper interpretation is PACK lore)

**Lore:** Dryad/Ent folklore, living vines and rooted plants; **this pack explicitly classifies Creepers as Plant**, though that is not a canonical Minecraft biological fact.  
**Loop:** Earn biomass through genuine growth, sunlight, harvesting/planting distinct crops and surviving outdoors; prevent simple plant/break spam.  
**Mechanics:** (1) photosynthetic sustenance and biomass storage; (2) rooting for regeneration/knockback resistance; (3) vines and rapid foliage growth as movement/cover; (4) costly Creeper-like explosive seed/pollen bursts (without player death or free griefing).  
**Cost:** Sunlight/soil dependence and fire/drought weakness; plants that have no sunlight cannot freely gain biomass.  
**Why not redundant:** **Photosynthesis and terrain biology**, not Fae court bargains, generic Nature spellcasting, or Werewolf physiology.

### 9. Ooze — Gelborn (new candidate)

**Lore:** Minecraft slime/magma cube splitting, slime blocks and springy collisions; classic RPG slimes and slime-person fiction.  
**Loop:** Absorb/slime-process specific materials, grow a finite internal biomass pool, and master movement/impact absorption; no free item duplication from absorbing blocks.  
**Mechanics:** (1) elastic high-jump/fall absorption; (2) temporary compression/small-collision movement under strict suffocation checks; (3) split off a **non-player decoy** by paying biomass, never a second inventory-owning player; (4) choose protective gelatin or magma form with controlled heat traits.  
**Cost:** Consumes biomass for transformations and decoys, lower armor/weapon efficiency in compressed forms and vulnerability to drying/freezing as tuned.  
**Why not redundant:** **Variable-body physics and biomass**, not Beast/Arthropod mobility or Gilded durability.

### 10. Fae — Courtbound (new candidate)

**Lore:** Fey courts, favors, glamour and binding bargains from folklore, D&D/Feywild-inspired fiction, and the pack's Feywild content.  
**Loop:** Fulfill finite, validated bargains for court reputation, choose a court or covenant, and trade favors for glamour/navigation; never reward arbitrary repeatable player-defined promises.  
**Mechanics:** (1) glamours that alter mob perception or disguise without persistent item transformation; (2) favor-based enchantment or terrain tricks; (3) named bargains imposing enforceable buffs/debts; (4) court-specific travel/woodland sanctuary and temporary familiar summons.  
**Cost:** Binding obligations and favor debt, resource/lore-based exemptions (e.g. iron if approved) and court exclusivity.  
**Why not redundant:** **Social contracts and illusion**, not Plant photosynthesis, generic spellcasting or Forest Dragon physical transformation.

## Exclude from new class development (6/16)

| Hunter family | Exclusion reason; family stays in Hunter taxonomy |
| --- | --- |
| **Undead** | **Fail distinctness:** JAWR Vampire and Lich progression already occupy deathless physiology/identity. A third generic Gravebound would repeat hunger, sunlight, draining, immortality motifs. |
| **Dragon** | **Fail distinctness:** existing Forest/Cave/Sea Dragon forms already cover wings, scales, breath, hoards/affinity; a generic Wyrmblood duplicates them. |
| **Beast** | **Fail distinctness:** existing JAWR Werewolf/lycanthropy and related natural powers cover pack, claws, speed, predation and transformations; generic Wildheart duplicates them. |
| **Elemental** | **Fail distinctness:** extensively represented by installed magic schools and attunements. Fire/earth/air/water resistances plus bolts aren't a separate identity without a new distinctive economy. |
| **Demon** | **Fail distinctness:** infernal transformations, summons, pacts and souls overlap existing Occultism/Goety and Cave Dragon identity; a class called Infernal alone adds nothing sufficiently new. |
| **Aberration** | **Fail coherent identity and distinctness:** heterogeneous creatures don't define one playstyle, while generic psionics/mutation overlaps Psi and existing supernatural advancement. Could revisit only if a *specific* creature lineage yields an independent full loop. |

## Validation constraints and future decision

- A candidate's **fiction/lore reference is an inspiration**, not evidence the modpack already supports its abilities. All above effects are speculative custom implementations.
- The Hunter category map remains independently useful and does **not** mandate a playable class, passive mob neutrality or player transformation.
- Before code: for each selected class, prototype one legitimate class XP event, a *resource budget*, a signature ability absent from other paths, a disadvantage, and tests across death/multiplayer/claim boundaries.
- Consider **Deep Listener** first after Gilded to test event-based senses, then **Golembound** for equipment-mode/controlled NPC interactions. Prioritize diversity of engine requirements over filling a category checklist.
- No class except Gilded has owner-approved rules or JSON. This is an idea audit, not a build commitment.
