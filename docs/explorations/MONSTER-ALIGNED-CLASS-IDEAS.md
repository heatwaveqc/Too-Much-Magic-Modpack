# Monster-aligned player disciplines — concept map (NOT approved)

Grounded in `research/hunter-creature-inventory-2026-11-15:docs/hunter/CREATURE-TAXONOMY.md`. The 16 category names are an **authoritative Hunter taxonomy**, but these **player-aligned disciplines are proposals**. They should be distinct from the Hunter's damage-enchantment specialties, not imply all 16 form transformations, and should preserve boss/story/owner/retaliation exceptions. Categories can overlap on entities; players need not gain all category affiliations at once.

| Creature family | Player discipline (proposed) | Signature progression mechanics / cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undead | Gravebound | Deathless endurance; poison/hunger tolerance, nocturnal powers, specific holy/sunlight drawbacks; distinguish from JAWR Vampire and Lich |
| Illager | Hexbanner | Crossbow mastery, raid command, war banners, villagers distrust; no automatic vindicator/evoker loyalty without adapters |
| Dragon | Wyrmblood | Hoard-linked strength, scale armor, gradual breath/gliding; avoid replacing existing dragon forms |
| Void | Riftwalker | Blink, Ender movement, risky chorus-fruit feeding and spatial instability; no boss affinity |
| Piglin | **Gilded** (name locked) | Gold equipment at diamond strength, +3 Fortune, Netherite/gold Unbreaking+regen, Piglin/Brute peace, Nether-only foods |
| Sculk | Resonant | Detect vibration, avoid Warden summons, stealth vs sound, blindness/silence tradeoff; no blanket Warden peace without approval |
| Aquatic | Tideborn | Water breathing, trident current movement, underwater mining/combat; distinct from sea dragon form |
| Construct | Forged | Stabilized armor, knockback resistance, redstone-linked repair, restricted dietary/maintenance system |
| Beast | Wildheart | Sprint/jump, pack sensing and natural healing; distinguish from existing JAWR Werewolf physiology |
| Arthropod | Silkbound | Climb surfaces, resist webs, venom and silk utility; damage/benefits depend on particular arachnid/arthropod traits |
| Plant | Rootbound | Regenerate in light or on soil, root anchoring, photosynthetic mechanics; dry/fire vulnerabilities |
| Elemental | Primordial | Choose one elemental attunement (fire, frost, storm, earth); environmental replenishment and opposed-element weaknesses |
| Ooze | Gelborn | Slime-like shock absorption, compression and regeneration, reduced hard-armor efficiency |
| Demon | Infernal | Fire resistance, soul-fueled techniques, curse/debt mechanics; preserve boss and summoned-owner exceptions |
| Fae | Feybound | Glamour, charm, woodland movement and bargains; contractual costs and iron/silver vulnerability if thematic |
| Aberration | Unbound | Mind-sense, distortion and unusual resistances; instability or sensory overload as drawback |

**Suggested approach:** prototype Gilded's identity+diet+equipment+XP data model, then consider one very different class such as Resonant (perception/stealth) or Rootbound (terrain/environment regeneration) to verify that the progression chassis handles non-equipment classes. Do not auto-map neutralities from Hunter taxonomic membership; factions should have explicit, player-specific AI policies. No new mods or runtime class trees are approved by this concept map.
