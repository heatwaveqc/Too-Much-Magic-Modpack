# Hunter Mutagenic Brew — Permanent Mutation Design and QA

**2026-11-15. Draft source work only.** PR #148 is stacked above PR #143, #141, #136 and #127. Do not integrate the compiled JAR or modify quests until this has gameplay QA.

## Potion-to-mutation loop

1. Craft one Bio-Mutagen from **four Moss Blocks, four Rotten Flesh, and one Spider Eye** in a **shapeless 3×3 crafting recipe**.
2. Brew the Bio-Mutagen with a **brewing-stand-obtainable potion** in an ordinary Brewing Stand. The special conversion recipe only accepts registered potion types that the server confirms are outcomes of ordinary `PotionBrewing.addMix` recipes, plus exact normal-potion outputs verified from registered NeoForge `IBrewingRecipe` definitions. It copies the verified `PotionContents` into a Mutagenic Brew. An alternate-machine potion carrying syntactically valid potion data is **not automatically eligible**.
3. After Hunter initiation, drinking this special brew accumulates the source's **original positive duration** for **each** eligible effect, without granting the temporary potion effects. Ordinary potion drinking and Hunter potion XP are still separate mechanics. Mutagenic Brews grant mutation progress **instead** of ordinary potion XP.
4. Each effect and amplifier (zero-based) tracks its own duration. The target is **720,000 ticks = 36,000 seconds = 10 hours** per potency tier. There is **no minimum duration filter**: 1 tick contributes 1 tick. Effect duration of zero is not time; instantaneous effects such as Instant Health are not eligible for this passive mutation model.
5. A completed threshold unlocks a persistent effect. Each amplifier tier must independently accumulate its own ten hours. Lower-level potions **never upgrade** stronger mutation progress. Once unlocked, a higher amplifier (Speed II) **replaces**, rather than stacks with, the lower amplifier (Speed I), and the effect still counts as one mutation for metabolism. Unlimited distinct effects may be unlocked. The effect is periodically refreshed by the server and saved in a copy-on-death player attachment.
6. The ongoing cost is **0.0005 food exhaustion per tick per unlocked distinct mutation** by default. At 20 TPS, that is 0.01 exhaustion/s per mutation; 4 exhaustion drains a saturation/food point, approximately one point every 400 seconds or 1.5 points per 10 minutes **per mutation**, in addition to normal activity. No aggregate mutation count ceiling.

For scale: a three-minute potion requires **200** contributing brews to reach ten hours; an eight-minute potion requires **75**. Vanilla Regeneration II has an actual 22.5-second duration (450 ticks), so exactly **1,600 Mutagenic Regeneration II bottles** are needed; with brewing-stand batching, **534 rounds** of Nether Wart, Ghast Tear, Glowstone Dust and Bio-Mutagen each suffice for 1,600 bottles. Base Regeneration I progress cannot be substituted for II. This might be expensive but follows the requested endgame economy.

## Server-reloadable Hunter policy

The Hunter path JSON has a hunter_mutations object with target_duration_ticks (720000), exhaustion_per_mutation_tick (0.0005), maximum_amplifier (3; potency limit only, not mutation-count limit), and effect_blacklist (exact namespaced effect IDs). The initial blacklist includes Absorption, Saturation, instantaneous healing/harming, raid/omen reward effects, death-triggered spawn effects, Tombstone Ghostly Shape, and currently irreversible harmful vanilla effects (Poison, Wither, Levitation, Hunger, Blindness, Darkness, Weakness, Slowness, Nausea, Mining Fatigue, Unluck) pending an approved mutation purge. It can be edited by pack datapacks. Modded effects not blacklisted are eligible only when they are part of a **verified brewing-stand recipe output** with meaningful duration. Their presence in a custom potion component is not enough.

The brewing handler is registered with NeoForge's standard RegisterBrewingRecipesEvent / IBrewingRecipe / PotionBrewing.Builder.addRecipe hook (not a hardcoded datapack list or one-off KubeJS-only transfer). It accepts PotionItem subclasses with registered stand-brewed `POTION_CONTENTS`; modded items with entirely independent potion metadata require adapters. At server startup, `StandPotionEligibility` verifies all registered addMix output potion holders using `PotionBrewing.isBrewablePotion`, and samples `IBrewingRecipe` outputs using standard potion containers, registered base potions, and unmodified registered ingredient items. Stand recipes whose inputs depend on extra NBT, custom non-PotionItem containers, or unenumerable dynamic sources need explicit audited adapters. Custom effects must **exactly match** a verified stand-recipe output; arbitrary custom-effect lists are excluded. The eligibility check runs at both conversion and Hunter consumption, to prevent crafted or copied Mutagenic Brews from bypassing it. We preserve the approved base potion and any exactly verified custom effects rather than inventing guessed mod IDs. A brew with no eligible effects cannot be created. Custom multi-effect mixtures credit all eligible effects' original duration. Potency above maximum_amplifier is clamped to the configured cap.

**Important:** The restriction is about **which effect-bearing potion type the normal stand can produce**, not proof of where an individual bottle was originally manufactured. If an alternate machine produces an identical potion that is also obtainable through a normal brewing-stand recipe, it remains an eligible *potion type*.

A potion-like instant heal has no meaningful permanent timed status and is excluded for now. It can get a deliberately designed alternative passive ability later, rather than infinite bursts of healing every tick.

**Non-Hunter penalty:** The special Mutagenic Brew is consumed and attempts to apply **Wither II for 5 minutes (6,000 ticks, amplifier 1)** when the consumer has not joined Hunter. No normal source-potion effects are granted to them. Hunter brews that have no further mutation progress are not consumed. Eligibility and game-mode behavior still need in-world testing.\n\n**Bio-Mutagen texture:** unique 32×32 transparent green biomass pixel sprite at assets/tmm_progression/textures/item/bio_mutagen.png (not Oritech's copyrighted asset).\n\n## Everlasting Abilities and Pylons comparison

[Everlasting Abilities](https://modrinth.com/project/everlasting-abilities) has a NeoForge 1.21.1 release, requires Cyclops Core, and supplies persistent abilities via Ability Bottles / Totems, XP-based transfer and ranks. It has datapack controls, GUI and possible PvP ability loss. This is a useful reference for rank display and long-term persistence, but a poor direct replacement for an unlimited-effect ten-hour potion-absorption economy. We should **not automatically add it as a required mod**, and should check that standalone Everlasting players cannot stack duplicate power with Hunter mutations unchecked.

[Pylons](https://modrinth.com/mod/pylons) uses a Potion Filter to extract active effects, generally requiring 60 seconds of minimum active duration and an hour of total duration. This feature instead directly consumes the special potion's verified component data; the player never receives the source effect first.

**Hunger comparison:** Everlasting Abilities exposes general.general.exhaustionPerAbilityTick as food exhaustion per active ability per second; commonly configured as 0.01, matching this Hunter pilot's 0.0005 per game tick. Other modpacks have used 0.05. Existing installed late-game upkeep solutions include Draconic Evolution's Auto Feed Module (pulls and consumes inventory food automatically), ProjectE Body Stone / Life Stone on an activated Dark Matter Pedestal (restores nearby player food), and Mahou Tsukai's Drain Life Boundary (restores hunger per drained mob at full health with hunger restoration enabled). Rooted Boots and renewable food are other support paths. These mean hunger exhaustion is an early/midgame budget and cannot by itself gate extreme late-game potency.\n\n## Important unverified release gates

- Compile/JUnit and standalone dedicated-server boot, plus real client brewing and drinking.
- Verify original potion effects never apply, inventory/bottle transaction correctness, data component preservation and recipe availability.
- Save/reload/respawn/death, amplifier upgrades, one-tick increments, ten-hour unlock, hunger per mutation.
- Test Pylons/Everlasting interactions and modded potions with custom item classes; fail closed on unsuitable effects.
- Confirm no hunger bypass via permanent Saturation, infinite food from modded effects, other mods' hunger-elimination mechanisms, or unauthorized data.
- Add a purge/cure or voluntary permanent mutation removal procedure before shipping, since unfavorable modded effects could otherwise make a player character unplayable.
- Blacklisting an already unlocked mutation suppresses both its effect and metabolic upkeep while retaining saved historical progress. Decide whether future policy migration should delete old progress.
- Hunter still lacks a dedicated mutation GUI; /tmmprogress mutations currently gives a command summary.
