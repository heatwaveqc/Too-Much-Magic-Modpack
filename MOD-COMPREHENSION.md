# Mod comprehension project

**Entry point for autonomous Work sessions:** [docs/mod-comprehension/WORK-CHAT-INSTRUCTIONS.md](docs/mod-comprehension/WORK-CHAT-INSTRUCTIONS.md).

When asked to **"work on the mod comprehension project"**, start there, read the [research index](docs/mod-comprehension/INDEX.md), and continue the highest-value unfinished research. Do not require the user to name a mod or re-explain the procedure.

The project has two passes:

1. **Mod profiles:** mechanics and gameplay, exact inputs/outputs, progression, internal synergies, useful early setups, exposed hooks and version-scoped evidence.
2. **Cross-mod interactions:** already-supported connections and feasible compatibility opportunities, with costs, tests, quest ideas and balance constraints.

These documents are **research and design inputs**, not permission to implement recipes, quests, mod upgrades or balance changes. Follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the existing design and quest contracts.
